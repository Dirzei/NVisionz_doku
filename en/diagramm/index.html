envisionz-framework-diagramm

A chart surface of its own – JDK only, for screen, SVG, PNG, PDF and print

A building block for 2D charts without third-party libraries: Java2D for screen and PNG, own writers for SVG and PDF. A chart holds layers of data source and renderer, any number of axes, a colour field, markers and annotations; a Swing surface operates it with mouse and keys. The algorithms behind it – step sizes, bands, Delaunay, histogram, box plot, thinning, text width – have a page of their own.

Principles of the module

Depends on nothing. Only java.desktop: Java2D, javax.imageio for PNG, Swing for the view. The module writes SVG and PDF itself; its own PDF writer is smaller than a new dependency.
Runs without a display. SVG, PNG and PDF are produced without a window; the Swing view lives separately in the package swing.
Layers instead of fixed chart types. A layer joins a data source with a renderer and an x and y axis. New chart types are new renderers; the painter knows them only through the interface.
Live data without copying. Reihe is a thread-safe ring buffer of fixed size: the computing thread appends, the painter fetches a snapshot. Repainting happens only when the version has changed.
No locale, no machine fonts. Numbers in labels, SVG and PDF always use a decimal point; text widths in SVG and PDF come from the advance widths of Helvetica. The files are character-identical on every machine.
No pop-up windows. The surface has neither context menu nor dialog; the application chooses printer and page in its own sidebar.

Structure: chart, layers, renderers envisionz.diagramm · .daten · .darsteller

Diagramm, Schicht, Darsteller, Zeichner, Leinwand

A Diagramm holds title and subtitle, any number of x and y axes, the layers, optionally a contour field, markers and annotations. It reports changes to its structure as an Ereignis; the view polls data via version(). The Zeichner lays out margins from the labels, fits automatic axes to the visible layers and paints grid, field, layers, axes, colour bar and legend onto a Leinwand – the same order for screen, PNG, SVG and PDF. liegend(true) turns any chart sideways: the painter hands the renderers a canvas mirrored at the diagonal; texts stay upright.

Axes envisionz.diagramm.Achse

Achse – linear, logarithmic, time, categories, symbols

An axis has a fixed or automatic range, a position (first x at the bottom, further ones at the top, first y on the left, further ones on the right, or explicit) and ticks. Linear ticks sit at 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten, logarithmic ones at powers of ten with minor ticks, on the time axis at calendar boundaries of a time zone (1 ms to 1000 years). Category axes always run from −0.5 to n−0.5n - 0.5. Reversal, fixed step, custom format, rotated labels (−90° to 90°) and fonts of their own for title and labels. Step sizes and time axis →

  • format(v -> text), formatMuster, drehung(grad), titelschrift, beschriftungsschrift
  • striche(), zuPixel, ausPixel, ablesen(v), Achse.schritt(spanne, n)

Pie, polar and spider charts have their reference frame in the middle of the surface and belong in a chart with ohneAchsen(true): no frame, no grid, no axis bands. Several polar layers in one chart need the same radiusBis.

Colour field and contours envisionz.diagramm.Konturfeld · Farbskala

Konturfeld, Farbskala, darsteller.Isolinien

Polygons in data coordinates with one value per cell – the cells of an unstructured mesh or a cut directly, without resampling to a raster. Either cell colours, 2 to 64 bands of equal size, by ratios (logarithmic) or freely set, own band colours, isolines on the band boundaries with labels, mesh lines, a colour bar on the right or at the bottom. Band boundaries run smoothly through the cells because each cell is split into a fan around its centroid when drawn. Scattered points are triangulated by Delaunay. A colour raster computed elsewhere can be drawn directly. Bands, fans, Delaunay →

Output: SVG, PNG, PDF, print envisionz.diagramm.Ausgabe

Ausgabe, SvgLeinwand, Java2dLeinwand, PdfLeinwand, Stil

One painter, four targets. PNG via Java2D with anti-aliasing. SVG as text with locale-free numbers, clipping via clipPath, opacity as fill-opacity. PDF as one page of chart size with vectors, the standard font Helvetica in WinAnsi (bold and italic only when used), opacity via a graphics state, images compressed. Print without a print dialog: the application chooses printer and page, the chart is fitted, centred horizontally and aligned to the top. Stil brings HELL and DUNKEL with a palette of eight colours.

Used in

app-cfd: images of the residual history and the field

What for

Report images that look the same in every program, images from computations without a display, printouts.

The text width in SVG and PDF comes from the advance widths of Helvetica, not from a font on the machine (text width). In PNG, Java2D measures with the machine's font; image and SVG are therefore not pixel-identical in the position of texts.

Markers and annotations envisionz.diagramm.Marke · Anmerkung

Marke, Anmerkung

Markers are lines at a value or bands over a range on an x or y axis, above grid and field, below the layers. Annotations are text (with alignment and rotation), line, polygon (outline, fill, opacity), arrow to a point with text at the end of the shaft, and image in a rectangle of the data – each attached to an x and y axis and behind or in front of the layers.

Operation in Swing envisionz.diagramm.swing

DiagrammFlaeche, Klick

A JComponent without pop-up windows or context menu. Dragging with the left button selects a section (with Shift only in x), the wheel zooms around the pointer (Ctrl only x, Shift only y), right or middle button pan, a double click restores the initial view; arrow keys pan by a tenth, with Shift by half. Every change of the view goes into a history of at most 100 steps, Ctrl+Z undoes it (wheel and key steps within 0.6 s count as one). Crosshair with readout, optionally snapped to the nearest data point within 10 pixels; tooltips; clicks with pixel, values and hit; Ctrl+C copies the image. A 100 ms timer repaints only when the version has changed.

Saving the state envisionz.diagramm.Zustand

Zustand – presentation as XML

Writes and reads the state of a chart as XML (StAX, version 2; version 1 remains readable): title, subtitle, legend, equal scales, horizontal orientation, margins, axis offset, fonts, axes with kind, range, reversal, position, rotation, step and format pattern, layers with visibility and axes, series with their appearance, markers, contour field settings and all annotations. Optionally the points of the series and the field with geometry and values come along. Reading applies to an existing chart; whatever does not fit is returned as a note, nothing is applied before the whole file is read, and DTDs and external entities are refused.

Methods

  • Zustand.alsText(d[, mitDaten]), schreibe(d, writer[, mitDaten])
  • Zustand.ausText(d, text), lies(d, reader) – returns the list of notes

What for

Saving and restoring views together with the work.

Not part of the state: data sources other than series, custom renderers, a display format set as a function.

How the chart surface computes

Algorithms

Round step sizes (1, 2, 5 times a power of ten), logarithmic division, time axis in calendar units of a zone; bands linear, logarithmic and free, smoothing via node values, fan around the centroid, isolines on the band boundaries; Delaunay by Bowyer-Watson with a relative tolerance; classes by Sturges, half-open; type 7 quartiles and Tukey whiskers; thinning to four points per pixel column; text width from advance widths. With worked examples from the checks.

Limits

  • So far only the CFD user interface uses the module; the other charts of the workspace still run on JFreeChart.
  • The Delaunay triangulation is meant for a few thousand points (all triangles are tested for each point).
  • A milestone at the edge of the automatic range of a bar plan is cut in half; the range knows nothing about pixels.
  • The print path has not been tried on a real printer.
  • The state does not include data sources other than series or custom renderers.
