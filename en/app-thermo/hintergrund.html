Tables and combustion tools – background

What the app computes itself, although the thermodynamics live in the framework: how the property table interpolates between exactly computed nodes, how the equilibrium is built from the input and displayed, how the adiabatic flame temperature curve is sampled, when the reference band appears and what the reactor card shows.

What this is about

Partition functions, equilibrium and kinetics come from framework-thermo. The app calls them and makes its own decisions on the way: where it computes exactly and where it interpolates, which input goes to the framework in which unit, which rows it shows and when it overlays a literature mark. These decisions are described here.

The property table

An exact evaluation of the partition functions costs a few hundred milliseconds for polyatomic molecules at high temperature. The table runs from 200 K to 3000 K in steps of 25 K, that is 113 rows. Only the nodes 100 K apart are computed exactly, 29 of them. In between, cubic Hermite interpolation is used so that the three quantities fit together: at the nodes the enthalpy gets the heat capacity as its slope, the entropy cp/Tc_p/T. With u=(T−T0)/ΔTu = (T - T_0)/\Delta T and ΔT=100K\Delta T = 100\ \mathrm{K},

h(T)=h0H00(u)+ΔTcp,0H10(u)+h1H01(u)+ΔTcp,1H11(u),h(T) = h_0\,H_{00}(u) + \Delta T\,c_{p,0}\,H_{10}(u) + h_1\,H_{01}(u) + \Delta T\,c_{p,1}\,H_{11}(u),

with the Hermite basis functions H00=2u3−3u2+1H_{00} = 2u^3 - 3u^2 + 1, H10=u3−2u2+uH_{10} = u^3 - 2u^2 + u, H01=−2u3+3u2H_{01} = -2u^3 + 3u^2, H11=u3−u2H_{11} = u^3 - u^2. The heat capacity between the nodes is the derivative of this polynomial, cp=dh/dTc_p = \mathrm{d}h/\mathrm{d}T. At the nodes the table is exact; in between, enthalpy and entropy stay within 0.05 % of the exact value.

The table holds all values per mole. The mass-based display divides by the molar mass; it computes nothing anew.

The equilibrium

The card takes the pressure in bar and passes it on in pascal (p⋅105p \cdot 10^5). The equivalence ratio φ\varphi becomes the air ratio λ=1/φ\lambda = 1/\varphi. Methane and hydrogen go to the cached equilibrium calculation with their mass fractions of carbon and hydrogen, kerosenes with their own description. Of the 17 species the table shows only those with a mole fraction x≥10−12x \ge 10^{-12}, each as a mole fraction and in ppm:

xppm⁡=x⋅106.x_{\mathrm{ppm}} = x \cdot 10^6 .

“T adiabatic” first searches the adiabatic temperature and then computes the equilibrium at exactly that temperature. The “dissociation” box applies to both steps. Without dissociation a lean methane–air mixture burns completely according to CH4+2O2→CO2+2H2O\mathrm{CH_4} + 2\,\mathrm{O_2} \to \mathrm{CO_2} + 2\,\mathrm{H_2O}: the exhaust then holds water and carbon dioxide in the ratio 2 to 1 once the carbon dioxide of the air is subtracted. For the stoichiometric methane–air mixture with dissociation the adiabatic temperature lies between 2150 K and 2260 K.

The flame temperature curve

The card samples the adiabatic flame temperature at 45 points,

φi=0.3+0.05i,i=0,…,44,\varphi_i = 0.3 + 0.05\,i, \qquad i = 0, \dots, 44,

that is from 0.3 to 2.5. The result line names the largest value and its φ\varphi. For methane and air the maximum lies slightly rich, between φ=1.0\varphi = 1.0 and 1.11.1, because there dissociation and the heat capacity of the exhaust work together most favourably.

For kerosenes the card overlays a reference band from the literature, two horizontal lines at 2280 K and 2300 K. The band only holds at the reference point of the literature. The card therefore shows it only if

$$|p - 101\,325\ \mathrm{Pa}| < 2000\ \mathrm{Pa} \quad\text{and}\quad |T_0 - 298.15\ \mathrm{K}| < 5\ \mathrm{K}.$$

The reactor

The reactor card computes a reactor without spatial resolution at constant pressure up to the entered end time. It shows either the temperature over time or the mole fractions of selected species. The framework delivers mass fractions yiy_i; the card converts them row by row into mole fractions,

xi=yi/Mi∑jyj/Mj.x_i = \frac{y_i / M_i}{\sum_j y_j / M_j}.

The result line names the ignition delay if the mixture ignites within the time window, the final temperature and the number of steps. A hydrogen–air mixture at 1400 K ignites within 2 ms and ends more than 500 K above its initial temperature.

Limits

  • The interpolation is only as good as the nodes. Between two nodes it cannot show an effect that the exact calculation sees at neither of them.
  • The reference band is a reading aid at the reference point of the literature. Away from it, it is left out on purpose, even if the curve lies close.
  • Behind the flame the reactor temperatures lie above equilibrium. That is a property of the reduced mechanism, not an error of the card; the status line says so.
  • The first call builds the computation tables and takes some tens of seconds.
