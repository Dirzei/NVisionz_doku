envisionz-framework-vision

Video geometry

Recovering camera, motion and three-dimensional shape from images and videos: calibration, features, two views, bundle adjustment, scale, body models fitted to silhouettes, dense reconstruction and a closed surface. The subject is exclusively man-made objects. Every stage delivers its uncertainty and is first measured on rendered scenes with an exactly known truth.

Principles

  • Accuracy is measured, not claimed. Every stage is checked on synthetic scenes with an exactly known truth: the ray tracer generates every ray through the same back-projection the camera model uses; a known point in space therefore appears exactly where the projection puts it.
  • Always with uncertainty. Adjustments deliver the covariance; acceptance tests check that the error fits the promised uncertainty.
  • Portable. No window system, no image class: images are grey values with width and height. The source tree also compiles with Java 17. The numerics come from framework-math.
  • No third-party library. Calibration, the five-point method, P3P, bundle adjustment and the Poisson surface are implemented here following the literature.
  • Objects only. The module knows bodies, surfaces and edges. People appear at most as image regions about which nothing is stated.

How the module computes

The camera

Pinhole camera, radial and tangential distortion, back-projection with Newton and its invertible range, rotation vector, calibration after Zhang, vanishing points.

From two views to the surface

Epipolar geometry, five-point method, triangulation, bundle adjustment with gauge and covariance, scale, dense depth maps, Poisson surface and the test object with exact truth.

Who uses it

The geometry card of video forensics and the command line vision on the command line call the procedure. The authenticity check builds on this module; its trace "geometry and time" asks whether a rigid object can be explained between the images of a sequence by a single camera motion.

Limits

  • Real parts with measured values as a comparison are still to come; the file format for measured values is prepared.
  • A single-image measurement (dimensions from one image) is still missing.
  • Of compression, the disturbances of the test object only model the lossy part of JPEG.
