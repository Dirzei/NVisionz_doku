MFCC features and phoneme features – background

The computation behind Mfcc, MelFilterbank, Delta, Lautmerkmale and Kontextfenster: from 400 samples to 13 numbers, from there to 39 and to 273, with pseudocode following the source.

What this is about

At 16 kHz a sound signal is 16,000 numbers per second. Most of them are irrelevant for recognising a speech sound: whether an a can be told from an i is decided by the coarse shape of the spectrum – the resonances of the vocal tract – not by the pitch of the voice and not by the loudness. The MFCCs (mel-frequency cepstral coefficients) therefore condense a 25 ms piece of the signal into thirteen numbers every 10 ms, describing this coarse shape.

Two observations stand behind this. The ear resolves low frequencies finely and high frequencies coarsely, and it perceives loudness logarithmically. And a spoken sound is a convolution: the excitation by the vocal cords convolved with the transfer function of the vocal tract. The logarithm turns the product of the spectra into a sum, and the cosine transform separates the slowly varying envelope (vocal tract) from the rapidly fluctuating fine structure (pitch).

The chain at a glance

For each window the same sequence of steps runs:

x→pre-emphasis→Hamming window→|FFT⁡|2→mel filters→log⁡→DCT⁡→lifterx \;\to\; \text{pre-emphasis} \;\to\; \text{Hamming window} \;\to\; |\mathrm{FFT}|^2 \;\to\; \text{mel filters} \;\to\; \log \;\to\; \mathrm{DCT} \;\to\; \text{lifter}

Pre-emphasis

The voice falls off towards high frequencies by about 6 dB per octave. A first-order high-pass compensates for this so that the upper mel bands do not vanish in rounding noise:

yn=xn−αxn−1,α=0.97,y0=x0y_n = x_n - \alpha\, x_{n-1}, \qquad \alpha = 0.97, \qquad y_0 = x_0

Pre-emphasis runs over the whole signal, not per window, because it needs the predecessor of a value, and at the start of a window that lies in the previous window. A constant value is attenuated to 1−α=0.031-\alpha = 0.03.

Window and power spectrum

A window has ff values (25 ms, i.e. 400 at 16 kHz); the step to the next one is 10 ms (160 values). Before the FFT the window is weighted with a symmetric Hamming window so that the cut-off edges do not leak into the spectrum, and zero-padded to the FFT length NN (the next power of two, here 512):

wi=0.54−0.46cos⁡2πif−1,Pk=|Xk|2N,k=0,…,N2w_i = 0.54 - 0.46\cos\frac{2\pi i}{f-1}, \qquad P_k = \frac{|X_k|^2}{N}, \quad k = 0,\dots,\tfrac{N}{2}

The squared magnitude is divided by NN – the usual normalisation, which makes the values independent of the FFT length. The FFT itself comes from framework-math (RealFft.leistung).

Mel scale and filterbank

The mel scale is almost linear at the bottom and logarithmic at the top:

m=2595log10⁡(1+f700Hz⁡),f=700Hz⁡(10m/2595−1)m = 2595\,\log_{10}\!\Big(1+\frac{f}{700\,\mathrm{Hz}}\Big), \qquad f = 700\,\mathrm{Hz}\,\big(10^{m/2595}-1\big)

1000 Hz corresponds to 1000 mel, 8000 Hz to 2840 mel. For FF filters, F+2F+2 points are spread evenly on the mel scale between the lower and upper edge; each filter is a triangle from the centre of its predecessor through its own centre to the centre of its successor. The corner points are rounded down to FFT bins:

bi=⌊(N+1)fifs⌋,gj(k)={k−bjbj+1−bjbj≤k<bj+1bj+2−kbj+2−bj+1bj+1≤k<bj+2b_i = \Big\lfloor \frac{(N+1)\, f_i}{f_s} \Big\rfloor, \qquad g_j(k) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{k-b_j}{b_{j+1}-b_j} & b_j \le k < b_{j+1}\\[2mm] \dfrac{b_{j+2}-k}{b_{j+2}-b_{j+1}} & b_{j+1} \le k < b_{j+2} \end{cases}

The band energy is the weighted sum Ej=∑kgj(k)PkE_j = \sum_k g_j(k)\,P_k. Because the triangles add up to one in the interior, no energy is lost and none is counted twice.

Logarithm, cosine transform, lifter

The band energies are logarithmised (with the floor ε=ulp⁡(1)\varepsilon = \mathrm{ulp}(1), so that a zero does not become −∞-\infty) and transformed with an orthonormal DCT-II:

ck=f(k)∑j=0F−1ln⁡Ejcos⁡πk(2j+1)2F,f(0)=1F,f(k>0)=2Fc_k = f(k)\sum_{j=0}^{F-1} \ln E_j \,\cos\frac{\pi k (2j+1)}{2F}, \qquad f(0)=\sqrt{\tfrac1F},\; f(k>0)=\sqrt{\tfrac2F}

Of the F=26F = 26 coefficients the first 13 are kept. The lifter weights them with L=22L = 22 so that they have similar magnitudes:

c^k=ck(1+L2sin⁡πkL)\hat c_k = c_k\Big(1 + \frac{L}{2}\sin\frac{\pi k}{L}\Big)

The zeroth coefficient carries the total energy of the spectrum; in the default setting it is replaced by the logarithm of the frame energy ln⁡∑kPk\ln\sum_k P_k.

Change over time and neighbours

Thirteen numbers describe a moment, but a speech sound is a movement: a b differs from a p in how fast the spectrum builds up. Delta therefore fits a regression line through the neighbourhood of each coefficient and takes its slope – not the simple difference, which would amplify every bit of noise:

dt=∑n=1Wn(ct+n−ct−n)2∑n=1Wn2d_t = \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{W} n\,(c_{t+n} - c_{t-n})}{2\sum_{n=1}^{W} n^2}

With W=2W = 2 the denominator is 2(1+4)=102(1+4)=10. At the edge the outermost value is repeated, not padded with zeros: a zero would be a jump and create a derivative that does not exist. The second derivative is the derivative of the first; 13 values become 39 per window.

For phoneme recognition (Lautmerkmale) the time axis is kept. Instead of pooling it, each window gets its surroundings attached (Kontextfenster): 2K+12K+1 consecutive 39-vectors are concatenated, and only every ss-th window is kept. With K=3K = 3 and s=2s = 2 that is 39⋅7=27339\cdot 7 = 273 values per 20 ms. The context window replaces recurrence: every window remains an ordinary pattern for an ordinary network.

Pseudocode following the source

Mfcc.vonSignal and Mfcc.fenster

// Mfcc.vonSignal(roh)
y[0] ← roh[0]
for i ← 1 to n−1:  y[i] ← roh[i] − α · roh[i−1]          pre-emphasis over everything
T ← fensterZahl(n)        = 1 if n ≤ f, else 1 + ⌈(n − f) / step⌉
for t ← 0 to T−1:  fenster(y, t · step, result[t])

// Mfcc.fenster(signal, ab, ziel)
block ← 0                                                    length N, power of two
for i ← 0 to f−1:
    block[i] ← (0 if ab+i outside the signal, else signal[ab+i]) · hamming[i]
P ← RealFft.leistung(block)                                  N/2+1 squared magnitudes
for k: P[k] ← P[k] / N;   energy ← energy + P[k]
for j ← 0 to F−1:
    E[j] ← Σ_k g_j(k) · P[k]
    E[j] ← ln( max(E[j], ulp(1)) )
c ← Dct.vorwaerts(E)                                         orthonormal, type II
for k ← 0 to 12:  ziel[k] ← c[k] · (1 + L/2 · sin(π k / L))
if energieStattC0:  ziel[0] ← ln( max(energy, ulp(1)) )

Source: envisionz-framework-audio/src/envisionz/audio/mfcc/Mfcc.java, MelFilterbank.java, MfccEinstellung.java. The window curve and the lifter are computed once in the constructor; the buffers belong to the instance, so an Mfcc is not usable concurrently.

Delta.ableitung and Kontextfenster.baue

// Delta.ableitung(c, W)
denominator ← Σ_{n=1..W} 2·n²
for t, j:
    d[t][j] ← ( Σ_{n=1..W} n · ( c[edge(t+n)][j] − c[edge(t−n)][j] ) ) / denominator
    edge(i) = max(0, min(T−1, i))   – the outermost value is repeated

// Delta.mitAbleitungen(c, W)
d1 ← ableitung(c, W);  d2 ← ableitung(d1, W)
all[t] ← c[t] ++ d1[t] ++ d2[t]                              3·13 = 39 values

// Kontextfenster.baue(rahmen, K, s)
n ← ⌈T / s⌉
for i ← 0 to n−1:
    centre ← i · s
    for d ← −K to K:  append rahmen[edge(centre + d)]          (2K+1)·39 values

// Lautmerkmale.von(signal)
r ← Mfcc.vonSignal(signal)
if mittelwertAbzug:  subtract the mean of each coefficient over the whole utterance
if ableitungsWeite > 0:  r ← Delta.mitAbleitungen(r, ableitungsWeite)
return Kontextfenster.baue(r, kontextWeite, schritt)

Source: envisionz-framework-audio/src/envisionz/audio/mfcc/Delta.java, stt/Kontextfenster.java, stt/Lautmerkmale.java. The neighbours are taken before thinning out: resolution is lost, but no information about the context.

Decisions in the framework

Decision in the framework: Where there is no correct but only a customary answer, Mfcc consistently follows the common interpretation so that the features remain comparable with those of other tools: filter edges rounded down with (N+1) in the numerator, squared magnitude divided by NN, lifter 22, Hamming window in the symmetric version (f−1 in the denominator), zeroth coefficient as the logarithm of the frame energy. Anyone who rounds differently here gets coefficients that deviate by percents.
Decision in the framework: Default MfccEinstellung.fuer(rate): window 25 ms, step 10 ms, 13 coefficients from 26 filters, filterbank from 0 Hz to half the sampling rate, pre-emphasis 0.97, lifter 22. The setting is a data type of its own because features are comparable only if they were computed with the same values; it is stored alongside datasets and models. An upper filter edge above half the sampling rate is rejected with an exception, as are more coefficients than filters and an FFT length that is not a power of two.
Decision in the framework: The chain never jumps back and does not know the total length: each window is looked at once. For this reason MfccStrom over an AudioSource delivers the same as Mfcc.vonSignal over the whole signal. The mean subtraction of the phoneme features, on the other hand, needs the whole utterance and is therefore meant for offline use.

Worked example

At 16 kHz a window is 400 values long, the step 160; the FFT has 512 points, the power spectrum 257 bins of 16000/512=31.2516000/512 = 31.25 Hz each. The filter edges lie on bins 0, 2, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 20, … , 209, 231, 256; the first triangle is thus 4 bins wide (peak at bin 2, 62.5 Hz), the last one 47 bins (peak at bin 231, about 7.2 kHz). The centre of the first filter is at 68 Hz, that of the last at 7225 Hz.

One second of signal (16,000 values) gives 1+⌈(16000−400)/160⌉=991 + \lceil (16000-400)/160 \rceil = 99 windows; with s=2s = 2 that is 50 time steps of 273 values each. A word of half a second (8000 values) gives 49 windows.

How it is checked

MfccSelfTest (44 checks) and SttSelfTest check each stage against something that does not come from the stage itself:

  • Mel there and back hits the frequency to within 10−910^{-9} Hz (at 0, 100, 440, 1000, 4000, 8000 and 22050 Hz); 0 Hz is 0 mel; equal mel steps become wider towards the top.
  • For several sampling rates: every filter has peak 1, no negative weights, connected support; the triangles add up to between 0.999 and 1.001 in the interior.
  • Pure tones land in the expected band, and more than 99 % of the energy lies in three neighbouring bands.
  • A stream and the whole signal give the same number of windows with deviation below 10−910^{-9}; one second gives 99 windows, 25 ms at 16 kHz is 400 values, 10 ms is 160.
  • Constant features have derivative 0, a straight line has the same slope everywhere (deviation below 10−1210^{-12}), the second derivative of a straight line is 0; pre-emphasis attenuates a constant value to three hundredths.
  • Context window: the centre is the frame itself, at the edge there are copies of the outermost frame and no zeros, the neighbour is the true neighbour and not the next one kept; loudness changes the phoneme features only by 10−910^{-9}.

Limits

  • The features describe the coarse shape of the spectrum, not the pitch: they are not suited for melody or voice characteristics (see framework-voice).
  • Time resolution is limited by the 25 ms window; shorter events such as the release of a plosive are smeared.
  • The context window is as long as its width and not one step longer. Word context and stress lie beyond it and are the task of a language model.
  • Thinning out too much makes labels unreachable for a CTC loss: CTC needs at least as many time steps as the label has characters, plus one between each two equal ones. Lautmerkmale.zeitschritte predicts this.
  • There is no network for phoneme recognition itself yet; the feature chain is prepared, dictation depends on training data.
