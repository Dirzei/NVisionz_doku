envisionz-framework-audio

Reading, playing and recognising sound – without third-party libraries

Function overview for the presentation of the envisionz framework, as of 28 Sep 2026. One common source interface for PCM, own decoders for WAV, MP3 (Layer III) and MPEG-1 Layer II, playback and microphone via javax.sound, MFCC features, closed-vocabulary word recognition and the feature chain for later phone recognition. For each method: purpose, API, use in the framework, applications, examples. The examples are shortened. Many API names are German (e.g. Tonmischer = sound mixer, Horcher = listener, Wortgrenzen = word boundaries); they are shown unchanged.

Principles of the module

One interface for everything. WAV, MP3, Layer II, microphone and sine all deliver the same AudioSource; player, mixer and features do not know where the samples come from.
float, −1…+1, channel-separated. Every processing stage computes in floating point. Interleaving happens only immediately before the sound card.
Bit-exact round trip. The scaling factors are powers of two; 8, 16 and 24 bit come back unchanged. 32-bit integer PCM is rejected with a reason instead of being silently mangled.
No AudioSystem for reading. javax.sound.sampled only serves input and output at the sound card; volume is a factor computed in float, not MASTER_GAIN.
No third-party code. No JLayer, no Vosk, no JavaFX. FFT and DCT come from framework-math, the acoustic model from framework-neural.

Sources, playback and mixer envisionz.audio

AudioSource, SineSource, MicSource – where the samples come from

The one interface: sample rate, channel count, length (or LAENGE_UNBEKANNT), read into a buffer [channel][frame], optionally seekable. SineSource is both a source and a test instrument; the phase is recomputed from the frame counter, not accumulated. MicSource reads the microphone through a TargetDataLine and keeps a loss balance.

Methods

  • sampleRate(), channels(), frameLength(), position()
  • read(ziel, offset, frames) – −1 only at the end; seekable(), seek(frame)
  • SineSource(rate, kanaele, frequenz, amplitude, rahmen), SineSource.kammerton(rate)
  • MicSource(rate, kanaele), MicSource(mischer, rate, kanaele), eingaenge(rate, kanaele), starte(), beende()
  • verloreneRahmen(), zittern(), bilanz(), pegel()

Used in

app-forensik: ForensikPanel (sound chain)internal: all decoders, MfccStrom, Horcher

What for

Treat every sound source alike: play it, analyse it, turn it into features.

Player, Tonmischer, Versatzquelle, Pegel – making it audible

Player plays streaming through a SourceDataLine: stopping discards the device buffer (flush), the regular end plays it out (drain). Tonmischer sits in front: volume, linear balance (centre means a factor of one), channel layout and mute; the level meter measures before the volume control, i.e. the recording itself. Versatzquelle shifts a source by silence in front or by discarding the beginning – exactly in the data stream instead of in the sound card.

Methods

  • Player(quelle), play(), pause(), stop(), seek(sekunden), setGain(faktor), warteAufEnde(), zustand()
  • Tonmischer: lautstaerke, balance, stumm, kanalbild(Kanalbild.MONO|LINKS|RECHTS|TAUSCH|STEREO), ueber(quelle), pegel()
  • Versatzquelle(quelle, sekunden) – positive: silence in front, negative: beginning dropped
  • Pegel.von(puffer, rahmen), spitzeDb(), effektivDb(), uebersteuert(), balken(breite)

Used in

app-forensik: ForensikPanel (playback, picture-sound offset)app-forensik: Tondialog (mixer, level meter)

What for

Play the sound of a DVD backup in sync with the picture, listen to channels individually, judge the recording level.

Example – play a WAV as mono sum with lead-in

Tonmischer mischer = new Tonmischer();
mischer.kanalbild(Tonmischer.Kanalbild.MONO);
AudioSource kette = mischer.ueber(new Versatzquelle(new WavReader(new File("aufnahme.wav")), 0.35));
try (Player p = new Player(kette)) {
    p.play();
    p.warteAufEnde();
}

Reading and writing WAV envisionz.audio.wav

WavReader, WavWriter, WavFormat – RIFF directly

Read streaming and seekable; when writing, the sizes are filled in afterwards. The format's traps are handled in the code: 8 bit is unsigned (zero at 128), odd chunks carry a pad byte outside the chunk size, WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE holds the real code in the subformat GUID, an unusable data size (0 or 0xFFFFFFFF) means “rest of the file”. Overdrive is clipped, not wrapped around.

Methods

  • WavReader(file), format(), seek(frame), read(...)
  • WavWriter(ziel, format, rate, kanaele), write(quelle, offset, frames)
  • WavWriter.schreibe(quelle, ziel, format) – a whole source into one file
  • WavFormat.PCM_U8, PCM_S16, PCM_S24, IEEE_F32

Used in

app-forensik: ForensikPanel (save a sound excerpt)app-forensik: cmd.forensik.TonAuszuginternal: Datensatz (kws)

What for

Save sound excerpts from DVD material as WAV, read data sets for word recognition.

Example – concert pitch as 24-bit WAV

WavWriter.schreibe(new SineSource(48000, 2, 440.0, 0.5, 48000), new File("a440.wav"), WavFormat.PCM_S24);

MP3 decoder envisionz.audio.mp3

Mp3Source – MPEG-1/2/2.5 Layer III, sample-exact seeking

The whole chain in own classes: frame search (Mp3FrameReader, Mp3Header), side information (SideInfo, Granule), bit reservoir (ByteVorrat), scale factors, Huffman, requantisation, MS and intensity stereo, alias reduction and IMDCT (Hybridsynthese), synthesis filter bank. Seeking uses an own index of all frame starts instead of the hundred support points of the Xing header; after a seek three frames are discarded so that reservoir, overlap and filter memory are right again.   • Mp3Decoder.dekodiere(rahmen, ziel), vergissZustand()

Against ffmpeg the RMS deviation is 2.1·10−7 at a signal norm of 3.05·10−5, for all three MPEG versions (according to the module description).

MPEG-1 Layer II envisionz.audio.mp2

Mp2Decoder, Mp2Source, Mp2Tabellen – the sound of DVD and Video CD

DVD-Video mostly carries Layer II. Compared with Layer III there is no Huffman, no window switching and no alias reduction; the synthesis filter bank is the same. Because Layer II has no reservoir across frame boundaries, the decoder checks itself: after all fields the bit pointer must stand just before the frame end (restbits()). Mp2Source does not trust the first sync but demands three matching frames in a row and counts dropouts. Background, formulas and pseudocode →

Methods

  • Mp2Source(InputStream), bitrate(), rahmen(), aussetzer() – not seekable
  • Mp2Decoder(kanaele), dekodiere(d, versatz, rahmenlaenge, crc, ziel), restbits(), belegteTeilbaender()
  • Mp2Tabellen.stufen, zuteilungsbits, skalenfaktor, skalenfaktorPruefen(), faktorenPruefen()

Used in

}

MFCC features envisionz.audio.mfcc

Mfcc, MfccStrom, MelFilterbank, Delta

The classic feature chain: pre-emphasis, window, power spectrum (FFT from framework-math), mel filter bank, logarithm, DCT, lifter. Default setting per sample rate: 25 ms window, 10 ms step, 13 coefficients, 26 filters. Delta adds first and second derivatives, turning 13 values into 39; at the edges values are repeated instead of padded with zeros. Background, formulas and pseudocode →

Methods

  • MfccEinstellung.fuer(rate), fensterRahmen(), schrittRahmen()
  • new Mfcc(e).vonSignal(float[]) → double[time][coeff]; Mfcc.betoneVor(roh, beiwert)
  • MfccStrom(quelle, e), naechstes(ziel) – running over an AudioSource
  • MelFilterbank.zuMel/zuHertz; Delta.ableitung, Delta.mitAbleitungen(merkmale, weite)

Used in

Word recognition envisionz.audio.kws

Datensatz, KwsTraining, KwsModell, Horcher – closed vocabulary

A few dozen words, recorded by oneself, one network on top. Wortgrenzen finds the word in the recording, Wortmerkmal condenses it into a fixed number of segments with mean subtraction – which makes it insensitive to volume, time shift and, within limits, speaking rate. KwsModell stores network, normalisation, word list and the complete feature setting in one .ennet file, because model and features belong together. Horcher spots words in a running stream. Against coughing and slamming doors only the rejection threshold helps (default 0.6). Background, formulas and pseudocode →

Methods

Features for phone recognition envisionz.audio.stt

Lautmerkmale, Kontextfenster – one vector per time step

Unlike word recognition, the time axis is kept: MFCC every 10 ms, mean subtraction over the utterance, first and second derivatives, then the neighbouring windows added. The context window replaces recurrence: every time window stays an ordinary pattern for an ordinary network. Typical are three neighbours per side and every second window, i.e. 39 × 7 = 273 values per 20 ms. Background, formulas and pseudocode →

Methods
