MP3 and Layer II decoder – background

How an MPEG audio stream becomes PCM again: frames and bit reservoir, Huffman, requantisation, stereo methods, alias reduction, IMDCT with overlap, synthesis filterbank and the leaner path of Layer II (Mp3Decoder, Requantisierung, StereoVerarbeitung, Hybridsynthese, Synthesefilter, Mp2Decoder).

What this is about

MPEG audio is a perceptual codec: the signal is split into 32 subbands, each band gets only as many bits as its contribution to the auditory impression demands, and what is masked is dropped. The decoder reverses only the representation – where the encoder took its bit allocation from (the psychoacoustic model) is not in the stream and is not needed. In MPEG-1 a frame carries 1152 samples per channel; in Layer III these are two granules of 576 values each (32 subbands of 18 time values), in Layer II three time sections of 12 values per subband.

This decoder is written entirely within the framework, without a third-party library. The stages are named individually and can be tested individually, because an error in one of the later stages (such as swapped stereo sides) need not be audible at once.

Frames, reservoir and Huffman

A Layer III frame cannot be decoded by itself. The field main_data_begin of the side information points up to 511 bytes backwards: the main data of a frame may lie wholly or partly in its predecessors (bit reservoir). In this way the encoder lends a difficult passage bits from the easy ones before it. The frame layer therefore does not hand out individual frames but appends the main data of each frame to a byte store; the decoder reads from a place that may lie further back than the frame itself. The store is compacted rather than kept as a ring so that BitReader finds a contiguous region. If the store is not yet sufficient (at the start or after a jump), the decoder returns 0 values: the run-in.

For each granule and channel the scale factors follow, and then the frequency lines. The 576 lines are divided into three regions:

  • big_values: pairs (x,y)(x,y), with one of three tables depending on the region,
  • count1: quadruples of −1,0,+1-1, 0, +1,
  • the rest: zeros that cost not a single bit – that they are there is implied by the other two regions ending earlier.

This follows the spectrum: large values and many bits at the bottom, almost only zeros and ones at the top. The end is fuzzy because the encoder pads with fill bits; the bit pointer may therefore overshoot the limit part2_3_length by at most one code word and is then reset. More than that would mean: the tables are wrong.

Requantisation

What comes out of the Huffman stage are step numbers sis_i, not amplitudes. The characteristic is not linear but |s|4/3|s|^{4/3}: small values are resolved finely, large ones coarsely. On top come the gains, all as powers of two:

xi=sgn⁡(si)|si|4/3⋅214(G−210)⋅2−σ(sfb+p⋅pretabb⁡)[⋅2−2gwfor short blocks]x_i = \operatorname{sgn}(s_i)\,|s_i|^{4/3}\cdot 2^{\frac14(G-210)}\cdot 2^{-\sigma\,(sf_b + p\cdot \mathrm{pretab}_b)}\;\big[\cdot\,2^{-2\,g_w}\ \text{for short blocks}\big]

Here GG is the global_gain of the granule (zero point 210, in quarters), sfbsf_b the scale factor of band bb, σ=0.5\sigma = 0.5 or 1.01.0 depending on scalefac_scale, pp the bit preflag (instead of transmitting scale factors for the upper bands individually, it looks up a fixed boost pretab⁡\mathrm{pretab}) and gwg_w the subblock_gain of the window. The power |s|4/3|s|^{4/3} is tabulated once for all step numbers that occur (up to 15+819115+8191).

For short blocks the values lie band by band in the stream (first all three windows of band 0, then of band 1); the IMDCT needs them window by window. sortiereUm rearranges them – a pure reordering that must stand between requantisation and everything else.

Mid/side and intensity

MS stereo transmits mid and side instead of left and right; the side is usually much smaller and needs few bits. The back-conversion is the same formula once more:

M=L+R2,S=L−R2⇔L=M+S2,R=M−S2M = \frac{L+R}{\sqrt2},\quad S = \frac{L-R}{\sqrt2}\qquad\Leftrightarrow\qquad L = \frac{M+S}{\sqrt2},\quad R = \frac{M-S}{\sqrt2}

Intensity stereo transmits only one signal and an angle in the upper part of the spectrum, because there the ear localises only by the loudness distribution. The angle ι\iota (is_pos, 0 to 6) is held in the scale-factor field of the right channel, which is repurposed for it:

r=tan⁡ιπ12,Li=r1+rxi,Ri=11+rxir = \tan\frac{\iota\pi}{12},\qquad L_i = \frac{r}{1+r}\,x_i,\qquad R_i = \frac{1}{1+r}\,x_i

The value 6 is “fully left” (tan⁡\tan would be infinite; L=x,R=0L=x,\ R=0 holds), and the value 7 does not mean an angle but “this band is not intensity-coded”.

Alias reduction, IMDCT, overlap

The encoder’s filterbank splits into 32 subbands with overlapping edges; in doing so each band folds part of its neighbour into itself. On reassembly these parts cancel only if nothing happened in between – but quantisation did. The encoder therefore anticipated the folding, and the decoder undoes it: eight butterflies at each of the 31 band edges, as a rotation

x'18b−1−ix'18b+i=csi−caicaicsix18b−1−ix18b+i,i=0,…,7\begin{pmatrix} x'_{18b-1-i}\\ x'_{18b+i}\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} cs_i & -ca_i\\ ca_i & cs_i\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x_{18b-1-i}\\ x_{18b+i}\end{pmatrix},\qquad i = 0,\dots,7

where csi=1/1+ci2cs_i = 1/\sqrt{1+c_i^2} and cai=ci/1+ci2ca_i = c_i/\sqrt{1+c_i^2}, with the standard values ci=−0.6;−0.535;−0.33;−0.185;−0.095;−0.041;−0.0142;−0.0037c_i = -0.6;\,-0.535;\,-0.33;\,-0.185;\,-0.095;\,-0.041;\,-0.0142;\,-0.0037. Since csi2+cai2=1cs_i^2 + ca_i^2 = 1, this is a pure rotation and the energy is preserved. For short blocks the reduction is omitted (for mixed blocks it applies only at the edge between the two long subbands).

For each subband the IMDCT turns 18 frequency values XmX_m into 36 time values, weighted with a sine window:

yp=wp∑m=017Xmcos⁡(π72(2p+1+18)(2m+1)),wp=sin⁡(π36(p+12))y_p = w_p \sum_{m=0}^{17} X_m \cos\!\Big(\frac{\pi}{72}(2p+1+18)(2m+1)\Big),\qquad w_p = \sin\!\Big(\frac{\pi}{36}\big(p+\tfrac12\big)\Big)

Only the first half belongs to this granule; the second is kept in the overlap store and added to the first half of the next one. In this way successive blocks interlock, and no jump arises at the joins. There are four windows (long, start, short, stop); a short block consists of three IMDCTs of 6 → 12 values each, offset against each other by 6. Finally kehreFrequenzUm undoes the spectral mirroring of every second subband (sign change at every second value of the odd bands).

Consequence in practice: a single frame does not give a sound. After a jump the overlap store, the bit reservoir and the memory of the filterbank are missing.

Synthesis filterbank

Here a time signal arises for the first time. From 32 subband values per pass, 32 time values are produced; 18 passes give the 576 values of a granule. In each pass:

  1. The vector VV (memory over 1024 values) is shifted on by 64 places.
  2. The 32 band values SjS_j are multiplied by the cosine matrix, Vi=∑j=031Sjcos⁡((16+i)(2j+1)π64)V_i = \sum_{j=0}^{31} S_j \cos\big(\tfrac{(16+i)(2j+1)\pi}{64}\big), i=0,…,63i = 0,\dots,63.
  3. UU is assembled from VV in blocks of 32 with an offset (this is the polyphase structure).
  4. UU is weighted with the window DD (512 values of the standard) and summed in groups of 16: si=∑j=015D32j+iU32j+is_i = \sum_{j=0}^{15} D_{32j+i}\,U_{32j+i}.

Per pass the matrix takes 64⋅32=204864\cdot 32 = 2048 multiplications. There are faster ways via a DCT; the direct form was chosen because it shows the procedure and is fast enough.

Layer II

DVD-Video predominantly carries Layer II. Compared with Layer III, Huffman, window switching and alias reduction are missing; the synthesis filterbank is the same. A frame contains, in this order:

  1. Allocation: per subband and channel a few bits selecting the number of quantisation steps (0 means: not transmitted); there are subbands up to SBLIMIT4 = 27.
  2. Scale-factor selection: 2 bits per subband and channel saying whether the three time sections get their own scale factors or share some.
  3. Scale factors: 6 bits each, index ii into the table 2⋅2−i/32\cdot 2^{-i/3}.
  4. Samples: twelve blocks of three values per subband and channel, 36 values per subband; one of the three scale factors applies to each four blocks. A value is a fraction between −1-1 and +1+1, related to the number of steps by a(fraction+v)a\,(\text{fraction} + v); the top bit is meant inverted (one = positive), followed by two’s complement.

Layer II has no reservoir across frame boundaries. This gives a sharp self-check: after all fields the bit pointer must stand just before the end of the frame; restbits() reports the remainder, and a negative value means reading went past the end, i.e. a table is wrong.

Decisions in the framework

Decision in the framework: Seeking uses an index of its own over all frame starts instead of the seek points of the Xing header. After a jump the decoder starts ANLAUF_RAHMEN = 3 frames before the target, and overlap store, filter memory and reservoir are discarded; the first three frames are not output so that the state is correct again. Layer II is not seekable (Mp2Source reads an InputStream).
Decision in the framework: MS stereo is computed over all 576 lines, not only up to the last coded one. For short blocks the reordering spreads the values over the whole granule, and then non-zero lines lie beyond the line count – the error would occur only there and would be hard to find.
Decision in the framework: The decoder reads the MPEG-1, 2 and 2.5 versions of Layer III. Intensity stereo for MPEG-2 and 2.5 is not built because test material is missing: the decoder then throws an exception with that reason (MS stereo and mono are decoded). Layer I is not built.

Worked example

A step number s=8s = 8 in a long block, with G=214G = 214, sfb=2sf_b = 2, σ=0.5\sigma = 0.5 (scalefac_scale not set) and without preflag:

x=84/3⋅214(214−210)⋅2−0.5⋅2=16⋅2⋅12=16x = 8^{4/3}\cdot 2^{\frac14(214-210)}\cdot 2^{-0.5\cdot 2} = 16\cdot 2\cdot \tfrac12 = 16

Time scale: an MPEG-1 frame has 1152 values per channel, at 44.1 kHz thus about 26 ms (about 38 frames per second); a Layer II frame at 48 kHz exactly 24 ms. A Layer II scale factor with index 3 is 2⋅2−1=12\cdot 2^{-1} = 1.

How it is checked

  • Mp3SelfTest (291 checks) measures the decoder as a whole against ffmpeg: the RMS deviation is 2.1⋅10−72.1\cdot 10^{-7} at a signal norm of 3.05⋅10−53.05\cdot 10^{-5}, for all three MPEG versions (according to the module documentation).
  • Mp3stufenprobe (28 checks) tests the three later stages individually against something that does not come from the decoder, because a deviation in the whole does not say in which stage it lies: MS and intensity against the formulas of the standard on all 576 lines; alias reduction against coefficients the test computes itself from the standard values; the IMDCT against a MDCT of its own with the same sine window – added with overlap, the signal returns with the factor N/2=9N/2 = 9 (Princen–Bradley); the synthesis filter is linear, separates the channels, writes exactly 576 values from the write position, and a tone in subband kk lies at the band centre (2k+1)/128(2k+1)/128 with about 104 dB stopband attenuation in the middle of the neighbouring band.
  • 22 counter-experiments (one stage deliberately corrupted each) are all red. One stayed green at first: “without window DD”. The pitch is right even without the window; only the stopband attenuation tells them apart – with the window 2.8⋅10102.8\cdot 10^{10} as a power ratio, without it 2.3⋅1042.3\cdot 10^{4} and 195.
  • Layer II checks itself through restbits() and the table checks skalenfaktorPruefen() and faktorenPruefen() in Mp2Tabellen.

Limits

  • Only Layer II and III; Layer I is not built. Layer III only as a decoder, an encoder is not part of the module.
  • Intensity stereo for MPEG-2 and 2.5 is missing (see above).
  • After a jump the first frames are discarded; the target is thus reached only after three frames of run-in, not value-exact at the first value.
  • Playback through Player requires a sound card; the decoder itself does not.
  • The filterbank is the direct form with 2048 multiplications per pass, not a fast DCT version.
