envisionz-framework-echtheit

Authenticity check

Searches image sequences for traces of editing. Every trace computes on its own, states verdict, value and uncertainty, gives its reasoning and says where it cannot tell. There is deliberately no overall verdict.

Purpose

Indications are not proof. An unremarkable result only says that these methods found nothing; a suspicious one says that there is a place a person should look at. The module therefore delivers maps, time lines and reasons instead of one number for the whole.

The traces

Geometry of motionpoints that do not fit the common camera motion, and whether they follow a rigid motion of their owntoo few matches, uniform images

Spectrum and noise floorimprinted grids and tiles with too little noiseimages without measurable noise; images that are too small

How the traces compute

Geometry and time

Tracks from optical flow, fundamental matrix or homography, robust threshold, clustering per tile, rigidity; jitter, break and flicker against the normal level.

Spectrum, noise and camera pattern

Peaks in the averaged residual, noise floor per tile, the sensor pattern from many frames and the predicted correlation.

Traces of compression

Double rounding in the histogram, the last step size, the rhythm of an old group of pictures in the stream, vectors against the image motion.

Provenance record

Where it sits in the file, boxes and CBOR, COSE signature, chain, assertions, binding to the image data.

Light and shadow

Lines in homogeneous coordinates, majority over all intersections, common point, deviation as an angle.

Maps, time axis and report

The shared scale, from frame pair to frame, combining with weights and why there is no overall verdict.

Using it

In the interface through the card Authenticity, without it through Vision. Input is read through quelle.Videoauszug: a VIDEO_TS folder, program stream, MP4, elementary stream or a folder of still images.

Limits

  • Tested on generated material with a known intervention, not on a collection of real forgeries.
  • The thresholds are chosen so that unaltered test material stays unremarkable. On other material a trace may respond although nothing was edited, for example after unusual in-camera processing.
  • For provenance records in MP4 with the binding type for box structures the binding is reported as “not recomputed”.
