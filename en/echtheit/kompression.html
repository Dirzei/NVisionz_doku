Traces of compression – background

The computation behind Doppelquantisierung and Stromspur: how double rounding combs a histogram, how to measure it, why a former group of pictures remains as a rhythm in the data stream and how an overlay can be recognised from the motion vectors – with derivations, pseudocode following the source, a worked example, testing and limits.

What this is about

Whoever edits an encoded video and saves it again encodes it a second time. The first encoding is not gone then: it remains in the distribution of the coefficients and in the rhythm of the data stream. The traces measure whether it is there – and, if so, whether it is there everywhere.

Double quantisation

An encoder divides each DCT coefficient by the step size and rounds. If encoded with step q1q_1 and later with q2q_2, jq1j\,q_1 becomes round⁡(jq1/q2)\operatorname{round}(j\, q_1 / q_2). The number of multiples of q1q_1 falling on kk is

n(k)=#{j:round⁡(jq1/q2)=k}.n(k) = \#\big\{ j : \operatorname{round}(j\, q_1 / q_2) = k \big\}.

(derived; the pattern is known in the literature, Lukáš and Fridrich, Popescu and Farid.) Four cases show what can be measured:

q1→q2q_1 \to q_2n(k)n(k) for k=1,2,…k = 1, 2, \dotsIn the histogram
5 → 3 (coarse, then fine)0, 1, 1, 0, 1, 0, 1, 1, 0empty bins, distinct
3 → 5 (fine, then coarse)2, 2, 1, 2, 2, 1, 2, 2weak modulation
3 → 7 (fine, then coarse)2, 2, 3, 2, 2, 3, 2weak modulation
3 → 6 (integer multiple)2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2no trace

The degree of modulation follows from the ratio q1/q2q_1/q_2: if greater than 1 there are gaps; if smaller than 1, only ⌊q2/q1⌋\lfloor q_2/q_1 \rfloor and ⌈q2/q1⌉\lceil q_2/q_1 \rceil multiples alternate, that is, between neighbouring integers. With "fine, then coarse" the modulation is weak – not zero, but hard to separate from the smoothness of the distribution, and with an integer multiple it vanishes. So the trace gives no information in this case, without concluding "encoded once".

The last step from the histogram

The frames are split on the fixed 8×88 \times 8 grid, the orthonormal DCT-II of 255I−128255 I - 128 is formed, and for five low-order frequencies (0,1),(1,0),(1,1),(0,2),(2,0)(0,1), (1,0), (1,1), (0,2), (2,0) a histogram hh of round⁡|c|\operatorname{round}|c| is set up (up to 120). The image values are rounded to 8 bits, so the coefficients lie only near the multiples; a comb is required, not an exact hit. From p=40p = 40 downward:

ap=∑k≥1h(kp)∑v≥1h(v),ap−1/p1−1/p≥0.75.a_p = \frac{\sum_{k \ge 1} h(k p)}{\sum_{v \ge 1} h(v)}, \qquad \frac{a_p - 1/p}{1 - 1/p} \ge 0.75.

The largest pp meeting the condition is the step. The expression is corrected for chance: without a comb the share 1/p1/p falls on the multiples, giving 0; everything on multiples gives 1. At least 200 values must exist, otherwise the frequency counts as undetermined.

Unevenness of the multiples

The histogram of what lies on the multiples should be nearly smooth without double encoding. With yi=h(ip)y_i = h(i p) and the moving mean yi\bar y_i over i−2i - 2 to i+2i + 2:

u=1z∑imax⁡(0,|yi−yi|yi−1yi).u = \frac{1}{z} \sum_i \max\Big(0,\ \frac{|y_i - \bar y_i|}{\bar y_i} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar y_i}}\Big).

The subtracted 1/y1/\sqrt{\bar y} is the relative Poisson spread of a count, that is, the part of the unevenness that a singly encoded distribution shows too (derived). Bins with y<max⁡(6;0.02peak⁡)\bar y < \max(6;\ 0.02\, \mathrm{peak}) do not count, since only tails stand there; fewer than 4 bins give no value. The trace's measure is the second largest over the five frequencies – a single uneven coefficient is not enough.

Tiles

The step comes from the whole frame, the unevenness utu_t from 64×6464 \times 64 tiles with at least 160 values. The frame counts as doubly encoded if u≥0.3u \ge 0.3 or at least half of the tiles reach that. Then the map reverses – conspicuous is what does not carry the trace:

vt={min⁡(3,0.15/max⁡(ut,10−6))doubleut/0.3otherwise.v_t = \begin{cases} \min\big(3,\ 0.15 / \max(u_t, 10^{-6})\big) & \text{double} \\ u_t / 0.3 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}

In the "double" case a tile below half the threshold (ut<0.15u_t < 0.15) is an inserted piece encoded only once. The threshold 0.3 is a setting (caveat).

Pseudocode – Doppelquantisierung.pruefe(sequence, e)

for up to 64 evenly spaced frames:
    DCT per 8×8 block;  histograms per frequency, overall and per tile
for each frequency:  p ← step(h)                0 if no comb
    u[frequency] ← one(h, p)
if no step: cannot be evaluated ("uncompressed")
value ← second largest u
per tile:  ut ← measure with the frame's steps
double ← value ≥ 0.3 or half the tiles ≥ 0.3
map[t] ← double ? min(3; 0.15/max(ut, 1e-6)) : ut/0.3
conspicuous ← regions from 2 tiles                "double" alone is no verdict

Rhythm in the data stream

Within a group of pictures the frames are linked by the same reference chain, which breaks off at the boundary to the next. When an encoded sequence is re-encoded, this shows up in the prediction error: the coefficient demand of the P frames shows a rhythm with the length of the old group of pictures (from the literature, Wang and Farid; Stamm et al.). Without decoding, this can be measured on the data stream:

vj=coefficients≠0blocksper P frame,rj=vj−median|l−j|≤7⁡vl,σ^=1.4826⋅medianj⁡|rj|.v_j = \frac{\text{coefficients} \neq 0}{\text{blocks}} \quad \text{per P frame}, \qquad r_j = v_j - \operatorname{median}_{|l - j| \le 7} v_l, \qquad \hat\sigma = 1.4826 \cdot \operatorname{median}_j |r_j|.

The moving median removes cuts and scene changes. For each distance pp from 3 to min⁡(64,length/3)\min(64,\ \text{length}/3) and each phase the comb of positions is tested. The test statistic is the lower median of the cc residuals on the comb:

z=w((c−1)/2)c1.2533σ^,c≥3.z = \frac{w_{((c-1)/2)} \sqrt{c}}{1.2533\, \hat\sigma}, \qquad c \ge 3.

1.2533 is π/2\sqrt{\pi/2}: the median of cc normally distributed values has for large cc the standard error π/2σ/c\sqrt{\pi/2}\,\sigma/\sqrt{c} (from the literature). The lower median is deliberate: a single cut on the comb must not be able to raise the value, and in a series half of which are peaks, the value only counts once more than half carries it. So the hit is the smallest pp with z≥6z \ge 6 and z≥0.7zmax⁡z \ge 0.7 z_{\max}; the condition prevents a multiple of the true distance from winning. Distances related by divisibility to the stream's own group of pictures are not tested. The own group of pictures is the median of the distances between I frames.

Tiles. With a hit, the map shows where the rhythm is missing: vk=min⁡(3,0.3z˜/max⁡(zk,10−6))v_k = \min(3,\ 0.3\,\tilde z / \max(z_k, 10^{-6})), z˜\tilde z being the median over the tiles with at least 0.2 coefficients per block. Without a hit each tile searches for itself (one location may be re-encoded alone) with σ^≥0.5/blocks\hat\sigma \ge 0.5/\text{blocks}; the map is zk/6z_k/6.

Independently of this, a group of pictures with a broken link in the stream is reported (a cut without re-encoding).

Motion

From the forward vectors per macroblock (half pixels) the median g\mathbf{g} is determined per frame. In frames where the image moves (‖g‖≥2\|\mathbf{g}\| \ge 2), a block follows if ‖v−g‖≤2\|\mathbf{v} - \mathbf{g}\| \le 2. A tile counts as deviating in a frame if fewer than 30 % of its blocks follow. Over the at least 8 moving frames:

vk=frames with deviationcounted frames⋅10.6.v_k = \frac{\text{frames with deviation}}{\text{counted frames}} \cdot \frac{1}{0.6}.

A fixed overlay or a pasted region does not follow the image motion. Caveat: a station logo looks just the same; the trace shows the location, not the cause.

Pseudocode – Stromspur.bildgruppen(frames, groups, e)

rest ← entferneVerlauf(coefficients per block of the P frames, 7)
sigma ← 1.4826 · median|rest|;  own ← median of the I distances
for p from 3 to min(64, last/3), without divisors/multiples of own:
    for each phase: z ← wert(rest, p, phase, sigma)
hit ← smallest p with z ≥ 6 and z ≥ 0.7 zmax
if hit: map[k] ← min(3; 0.3 · median(z)/max(zk, 1e-6))    active tiles only
else:   map[k] ← z of its own search / 6
conspicuous ← hit or broken link or regions
course[j] ← rest[j] / (6 sigma), on the phase of the comb

Worked example

Unevenness. The smooth series 100, 90, 80, 72, 65, 58, 52, 47 gives u=0.00071u = 0.00071 (the deviations almost all lie below the Poisson spread); the alternating series 200, 60, 180, 55, 170, 50, 150, 40 gives 0.346.

Step. If all values lie on multiples of 5, the correction gives (1−0.2)/(1−0.2)=1(1 - 0.2)/(1 - 0.2) = 1. If at p=10p = 10 half lie on them: (0.5−0.1)/0.9=0.444(0.5 - 0.1)/0.9 = 0.444, so no comb.

Limit of the step search. With q1=12q_1 = 12, q2=6q_2 = 6, only even multiples of 6 occur: n(k)=0,1,0,1,…n(k) = 0, 1, 0, 1, \dots The search finds 12 as the step and a smooth histogram – the second encoding stays invisible; an integer ratio is fundamentally not detectable.

Map. In the double case a tile with ut=0.05u_t = 0.05 is capped at 3, with ut=0.10u_t = 0.10 it gives 1.5, at 0.15 exactly 1.

Rhythm. Ten residuals on the comb, sorted: 0.6; 0.65; 0.7; 0.7; 0.75; 0.8; 0.8; 0.85; 0.9; 0.95. At σ^=0.12\hat\sigma = 0.12, z=0.75⋅10/(1.2533⋅0.12)=15.77z = 0.75 \cdot \sqrt{10} / (1.2533 \cdot 0.12) = 15.77. A single cut of value 5 among otherwise zero residuals has the lower median 0. If only 10 of 20 positions carry a spike, the lower median is still 0, the upper 1: half is not enough. A distance pp is only tested once the sequence is at least 3p3p frames long; for p=7p = 7, 21.

Motion. If a tile deviates in 18 of 20 moving frames, vk=0.9/0.6=1.5v_k = 0.9 / 0.6 = 1.5. If only in 10 of 20: 0.83, no finding (EV-52). The numbers were recomputed with docs/theorie/rechnung/echtheit_kompression.java.

How it is tested

  • Double quantisation (Stufe2probe, test encoder on rendered material): encoded once unremarkable (EV-20), last step found (EV-21), "60 then 75", "50 then 85", "30 then 70" detected (EV-22 to EV-24), encoded once coarsely not (EV-25), "fine, then coarse" is not claimed (EV-26), two inserts found exactly, uncompressed material (EV-29) and too small images (EV-30) cannot be evaluated.
  • Stream (Stufe2probe, own MPEG-2 test encoder): encoded once unremarkable (EV-42), encoded twice with the right distance (EV-43), the course marks the frames (EV-44), single region re-encoded (EV-46), broken link (EV-47), empty and I frames only (EV-48, EV-49).
  • Motion: fixed location (EV-50), everything follows (EV-51), only in half the frames (EV-52), image stands still (EV-53).

Counter-tests turn the double decision, the comb condition, the period search and the broken link red.

Limits

  • If the second step equals the first or an integer divisor of it, there is no trace in the histogram; fine before coarse is not reliably detectable.
  • The rhythm is measured in coding order and samples only P frames, so only multiples of the P distance. If the old group of pictures is related by divisibility to the stream's own, it is not tested.
  • The test encoder is our own code; the trace is not measured on other material. Rate control can produce a periodicity of its own.
  • The rhythm trace reads MPEG-1/2 streams, other codings report as not evaluable. No rhythm does not mean "not re-encoded".
