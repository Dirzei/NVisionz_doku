Measuring and training – against a ground truth

The computation behind Tabellenmessung, Vergleich, Tafelernte and Tabellentraining: how generated tables with known content are made, how a read table of a different shape is aligned with its ground truth, which numbers count – above all “confidently wrong” – and how training characters are harvested from the same tables – with pseudocode after the source, a worked example, verification and limits.

What it is about

An improvement to the reading path is only one if it is measured. Measuring is only possible against a ground truth: a table whose content is known cell by cell. Real scans have none; typing them in would be expensive and error-prone itself. So framework-ocr generates tables: numbers in the manner of technical tables, set in free fonts, rasterised at a chosen resolution and aged like a scan. Each PDF comes with a ground-truth file. The measurement run reads the PDF on the same path as the read card and compares.

StepClasswhat

readTabellenlesenthe reading path of a scanned page in one call, as in the app
compareVergleichalign rows and cells, count

trainTafelernte, Tabellentraininglabelled characters from tables with the right shape, model

The report contains no cell text, so a measurement on someone else's data can be passed on without passing on the data.

Aligning: Needleman–Wunsch twice

One row too many, a cell broken in two, two columns in one – after such a fault every comparison at fixed positions is off, and a single shape error would count as hundreds of wrong cells. So the rows of the ground truth are first aligned with those of the reading, then the cells within each row pair, both with the recursion of Needleman and Wunsch:

Di,j=min⁡(Di−1,j−1+k(ai,bj),Di−1,j+λ,Di,j−1+λ),Di,0=iλ,D0,j=jλ.D_{i,j} = \min\big(D_{i-1,j-1} + k(a_i, b_j),\ D_{i-1,j} + \lambda,\ D_{i,j-1} + \lambda\big), \qquad D_{i,0} = i\lambda,\ D_{0,j} = j\lambda.

The gap cost is λ=0.55\lambda = 0.55 (0 for empty cells). The cost of matching two cells is the edit distance relative to the longer one,

k(x,y)=lev⁡(x,y)max⁡(|x|,|y|)∈[0,1],k(x, y) = \frac{\operatorname{lev}(x, y)}{\max(|x|, |y|)} \in [0, 1],

that of two rows the result of the cell alignment divided by the larger number of non-empty cells. Because any match costs at most 1 and two gaps 1.1, a wrong match is preferred to a double omission: a misread cell stays one wrong cell and does not break into a missing and an extra one.

The traceback compares floating-point sums for equality. So the border values are accumulated (Di,0=Di−1,0+λD_{i,0} = D_{i-1,0} + \lambda) rather than computed as iλi\lambda – in floating point that is not always the same, and the traceback then found no step.

What is counted

NumberMeaning
shape rightrow and column counts match
numbers exactshare of the ground-truth numbers read correctly character by character
confidently wrongread, wrong, not marked – goes into the data unseen
uncertain wrongwrong and marked – a hit of the checks
false alarmright and marked anyway – costs review time
missing, extracells without a partner
CERedit distance of all cells over the characters of the ground truth; missing and extra cells in full
confusionsper character pair truth→read, only in cells differing by at most half their length

The most important number is confidently wrong. An empty or marked cell is seen by the user; a confidently wrong one is not. Cells are compared after trimming spaces at the edges; one inside remains an error (31 .5 against 31.5).

A merged cell carries its value in the top row of the ground truth; in print it stands centred. If the cell is as tall as the others, the reader cannot see the merge and delivers the value in a middle row. With equal row counts it is moved up and counted separately – after all it was read correctly.

Harvesting training characters

A model reads best the typeface it was trained on; a network trained on system fonts reached 100 % on its training data and was no better than template matching on a scan. The generated tables supply characters that have passed exactly through the reading path – skew, specks, grid, crop, the model's splitter – together with their labels from the ground truth. Harvesting is careful:

  • only from pages whose shape matches the ground truth – otherwise the cells belong to wrong values;
  • only cells whose number of split characters equals the character count of the ground truth without spaces – otherwise the character-to-character assignment is unknown, and the cell is discarded;
  • no merged cells.

For each character a sample of at most -hoechstens: patterns is kept (default 3000), evenly over all pages: the nn-th pattern of a character replaces a random one of the HH kept with probability H/nH/n (reservoir sampling, fixed seed). Rare characters are repeated at most eight times; more and the network memorised instead of generalising. Single characters drawn from the free training fonts and stroke digits are added.

Pseudocode – measurement run

for each page with ground truth:
    image ← page image;  if none (typeset page):  report, continue
    table, types ← read(image, model, settings as on the read card)
    m ← compare(ground truth, table.text, table.uncertain)
    one line per page: shape, numbers exact, confidently wrong, missing, CER
totals, confusions; report without cell text

Verification

The comparison is tested on small tables with known results: equal, misread, marked, one row too many, one row and one column too few, edges, merged cells, nothing recognised, 40 rows against one. The measurement run reads three generated pages at 100 %, a wrong value in the ground truth arrives as exactly one more confidently wrong cell, and the report contains no cell text. Harvesting takes exactly the characters of the ground truth, nothing when the shape is wrong, and discards a cell with the wrong character count.

Limits

  • Generated tables are as close to real scans as the ageing goes; they do not reproduce a foreign typeface. A model that does well on the tables is therefore not automatically good on a scan in another face.
  • The alignment minimises cost; with two equally good alignments, the order of the traceback decides.
  • Confusions in heavily changed cells are not counted – there it is a shape error.
