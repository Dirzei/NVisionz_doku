JPEG – background

From bit stream to coefficient: category and extra bits, sequential and progressive, hierarchical doubling and the integer CMYK computation.

What this is about: coefficients, not points

A JPEG image is split into blocks of 8 × 8 samples; stored are their quantized cosine coefficients. The inverse transform and the colour conversion are on the page Compression. This page covers how the coefficients come out of the stream, in which order they may stand there and how the special processes build on that.

Category and extra bits

A value c≠0c \ne 0 is written as category n=⌊log2⁡|c|⌋+1n = \lfloor \log_2 |c| \rfloor + 1 (Huffman-coded) and nn extra bits vv. Positive values stand directly, negative ones as c+2n−1c + 2^n - 1, so that the top extra digit carries the sign. Back (T.81, F.12, EXTEND):

c={v−2n+1,v<2n−1v,otherwisec = \begin{cases} v - 2^n + 1, & v \lt 2^{n-1} \\ v, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}(1)

Category 1 thus gives ±1\pm 1, category 2 the values −3,−2,2,3-3, -2, 2, 3. The DC component is written as the difference to the DC of the previous block of the same component; at every restart marker this chain begins at zero.

Apart from the inverse transform, decoding is therefore integer and unique. The inverse transform itself is not bit-exactly fixed in T.81; two correct decoders may differ by one step.

Progressive: bands and bit planes

A progressive image sends the same quantized coefficients in a different order: each scan carries a range [Ss,Se][S_s, S_e] of the zigzag sequence (spectral selection) and only the upper bits from position AlA_l (successive approximation). A first pass delivers c≫Alc \gg A_l; each refinement one bit lower. In the refinement every already non-zero coefficient gets a correction bit that increases its magnitude by 2Al2^{A_l} – away from the sign:

c←c+sgn⁡(c)2Alif correction bit=1 and (|c|&2Al)=0c \leftarrow c + \operatorname{sgn}(c)\,2^{A_l} \quad\text{if correction bit} = 1 \text{ and } (|c| \,\&\, 2^{A_l}) = 0(2)

This yields the check without expected numbers: the same image written sequentially and progressively must be read identically point by point, since the coefficients are the same.

Hierarchical: doubling

A hierarchical image begins with a reduced base image; each further frame is a difference on the previously doubled reference. Doubling (T.81, annex J) takes the rounded-down mean of the neighbours, at the edge the last value is repeated, first horizontally, then vertically:

b2x=ax,b2x+1=⌊ax+ax+12⌋,aw:=aw−1b_{2x} = a_x, \quad b_{2x+1} = \left\lfloor \frac{a_x + a_{x+1}}{2} \right\rfloor, \quad a_{w} := a_{w-1}(3)

From 10, 21 in a row comes 10, 15, 21, 21.

CMYK in integers

With four components red is computed as (255−K)(1−C/255)(255 - K)(1 - C/255). In integers this needs round⁡(ab/255)\mathrm{round}(a\,b/255); for that stands the trick

t=ab+128,round⁡(ab255)=⌊⌊t/256⌋+t256⌋t = a\,b + 128, \qquad \mathrm{round}\!\left(\frac{a\,b}{255}\right) = \left\lfloor \frac{\lfloor t/256 \rfloor + t}{256} \right\rfloor(4)

It is exact for all a,b∈[0,255]a, b \in [0, 255]: t/256⋅(1+1/256)=t/255⋅(1−1/65536)t/256 \cdot (1 + 1/256) = t/255 \cdot (1 - 1/65536), and the error stays below half a step.

What follows

  • Sequential and progressive differ only in order; a progressive reader must collect all scans before it transforms back.
  • Differences between decoders lie in the inverse transform, not in the stream; a check against a foreign decoder therefore needs a tolerance of one, a check progressive against sequential none.
  • The hierarchical process computes in integers with rounded-down means; an encoder that doubles more precisely produces references a standard decoder does not hit bit-exactly.

How it is checked

jpeg.TheorieFCprobe checks EXTEND over all categories, reads images the JDK's JPEG encoder writes sequentially and progressively (against its reader at most one step, among each other bit-identical), recomputes doubling on a small field and the CMYK trick over all 65,536 pairs. The foreign anchors (Pillow, the JPEG reference software) stand in Jpegprobe, Progressivprobe and Hierarchischprobe.

Limits

  • Arithmetic lossless (SOF11, SOF15) is named and not read.
  • The inverse transform is designed for verifiability, not speed.
  • Table segments (DQT, DHT) must be filled exactly by their tables; a remainder or a table running past the segment is rejected. Until 2026-10-25 the segment end was computed two bytes too early, and skipped segments ended two bytes before their end.
  • A frame component that occurs in no scan is rejected; until 2026-10-25 it came out silently as zeros.
