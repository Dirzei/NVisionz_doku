MDCT and filter bank – windows and block switching

The computation behind Fensterform, Fensterart and Fensterbank: sine and KBD windows and why both satisfy the Princen-Bradley condition, long and short frames with transition windows, planning valid sequences, the grid with lead-in, and what “bit-exact” means for a floating-point computation. With pseudocode following the source, measurements from the probe and limits.

What it is about

The MDCT cancels its aliasing only if the windows of two neighbouring blocks fit each other. This page covers the windows themselves and a second question: how long a block should be. Long blocks resolve frequencies finely and are right for steady sounds. At a transient – a strike, a click – a later quantiser spreads its error over the whole block, though. At M=1024M = 1024 and 48 kHz a block covers 2M=20482M = 2048 samples, i.e. 42.7 ms; the error would then be audible even before the strike, a pre-echo. A frame can therefore switch to eight short blocks of 2m=2M/8=2562m = 2M/8 = 256 samples or 5.3 ms each.

Switching must not disturb the aliasing cancellation. At every frame boundary the same slope must fall and rise on both sides: long on long, short on short. Two transition windows mediate in between.

Window shapes

A slope is the rising half of a window, mm values w0,…,wm−1w_0, \dots, w_{m-1}; the falling slope is the same one read backwards. Both shapes satisfy the Princen-Bradley condition wn2+wm−1−n2=1w_n^2 + w_{m-1-n}^2 = 1, the prerequisite for cancellation (derivation).

Sine window

wn=sin⁡(π(n+12)2m)w_n = \sin\!\left(\frac{\pi\,(n + \frac12)}{2m}\right)

Because π(m−1−n+12)2m=π2−π(n+12)2m\frac{\pi(m-1-n+\frac12)}{2m} = \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi(n+\frac12)}{2m}, wm−1−nw_{m-1-n} is the cosine of the same angle, and sin2⁡+cos2⁡=1\sin^2 + \cos^2 = 1. The sine window has a narrow main lobe, so it separates closely spaced frequencies well.

Kaiser-Bessel-derived window (KBD)

The slope is formed by accumulating a Kaiser kernel of length m+1m+1:

Kj=I0(πα1−(2jm−1)2),j=0,…,m,wn=∑j=0nKj∑j=0mKjK_j = I_0\!\left(\pi\alpha\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{2j}{m} - 1\right)^2}\right), \quad j = 0,\dots,m, \qquad w_n = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{j=0}^{n} K_j}{\sum_{j=0}^{m} K_j}}

I0I_0 is the modified Bessel function of the first kind of order zero, computed as the series I0(x)=∑k≥0(x2/4)k(k!)2I_0(x) = \sum_{k \ge 0} \frac{(x^2/4)^k}{(k!)^2} until a term drops below 10−1710^{-17} of the sum. Because the kernel is symmetric, Kj=Km−jK_j = K_{m-j}, we have ∑j=0m−1−nKj=∑j=n+1mKj\sum_{j=0}^{m-1-n} K_j = \sum_{j=n+1}^{m} K_j, and the squares of wnw_n and wm−1−nw_{m-1-n} add up to the whole sum: Princen-Bradley holds exactly. KBD rises more gently than the sine at the start and attenuates distant frequencies more strongly.

Convention in the framework: KBD with α=4\alpha = 4 for the long and α=6\alpha = 6 for the short slope, as in AAC. The slopes are computed once per length and kind and kept. The shape of a frame belongs to its right side; its left slope has the shape of the previous frame, also for the first short block of a short frame. Only then is the shared slope the same on both sides when the shape changes.

Window types

A frame covers 2M2M samples and advances by MM. Its type says whether the left and the right slope are long or short:

Typeleftrightshape of the window over 2M2M samples
LANGlonglonglong slope rising, long slope falling
STARTlongshortlong slope, flat 1, short slope falling, zero
KURZshortshorteight blocks of 2m2m, offset by mm
STOPPshortlongzero, short slope rising, flat 1, long slope

The short blocks are placed as in AAC: block jj starts M/2−m/2+jmM/2 - m/2 + j\,m samples after the frame start; together the eight blocks cover 9m9m samples in the middle of the frame. The short slope of START falls exactly where the first short block of the following frame rises. A sequence is valid if at every boundary the right slope of one frame is as long as the left slope of the next, and if the first frame is long on the left.

Pseudocode – Fensterbank.planen(shortWanted)

leftShort ← false
for i ← 0 to n−1:
    rightShort ← shortWanted[i] or (i+1 < n and shortWanted[i+1])
    sequence[i] ← type with (leftShort, rightShort)      LANG, START, KURZ or STOPP
    leftShort ← rightShort
return sequence

A frame wanted short thus makes its predecessor short on the right (START) and its successor short on the left (STOPP). If only one long frame lies between two short sections, it becomes KURZ itself; if two lie in between, they become STOPP and START. If frame 0 is wanted short, it becomes START, because the beginning has a long slope.

Pseudocode – Fensterbank.pruefeFolge(sequence)

if sequence[0] short on the left:  exception “the beginning has a long slope”
for i ← 1 to n−1:
    if rightShort(sequence[i−1]) ≠ leftShort(sequence[i]):
        exception with position i and both slope lengths

The grid and the lead-in

Frame tt lies on positions [tM,tM+2M)[tM, tM + 2M) of a signal that is extended at the front by MM zeros. Without this lead-in the first frame would have no predecessor to cancel its aliasing with. At the back the signal is padded with zeros up to the end of the last frame. A value at position ss lies at s+Ms + M in the extended signal and is covered by frames ⌊(s+M)/M⌋−1\lfloor (s+M)/M \rfloor - 1 and ⌊(s+M)/M⌋\lfloor (s+M)/M \rfloor. For LL values one therefore needs

T(L)=⌊L−1+MM⌋+1(L>0),T(0)=0T(L) = \left\lfloor \frac{L - 1 + M}{M} \right\rfloor + 1 \quad (L > 0), \qquad T(0) = 0

That is ⌈L/M⌉+1\lceil L/M \rceil + 1 frames. The probe checks T(1)=2T(1) = 2, T(M)=2T(M) = 2, T(M+1)=3T(M+1) = 3.

Pseudocode – zerlege and setzeZusammenRoh

function zerlege(pcm, sequence, shapes):
    T ← rahmenZahl(L);  check lengths and sequence
    x ← zeros of length (T+1)·M;  x[M … M+L−1] ← pcm
    previous ← shapes[0]
    for t ← 0 to T−1:
        coeff[t] ← analyse(x, t·M, sequence[t], shapes[t], previous)   window, MDCT
        previous ← shapes[t]

function setzeZusammenRoh(coeff, sequence, shapes, L):
    y ← zeros of length (T+1)·M;  previous ← shapes[0]
    for t ← 0 to T−1:
        synthese(coeff[t], …, y, t·M)        inverse MDCT, 2 · window, add up
        previous ← shapes[t]
    return y[M … M+L−1]

function setzeZusammen(…):  round(setzeZusammenRoh(…)) per value

For a short frame, analyse and synthese compute eight small MDCTs of length mm; their coefficients lie one after another in the same MM slots as those of a long frame.

What “bit-exact” means here

Window, MDCT and inverse compute in double. The unrounded inverse therefore does not match the signal exactly but up to rounding errors. The probe's criterion: rounded to integers, every value comes back unchanged, and the unrounded error lies far below half a step – otherwise the rounding would be luck and not proof. How it is tested

The Mdctprobe (90 checks); for the checks of the transform itself see the MDCT page.

  • Princen-Bradley for sine and KBD, long and short, m=128m = 128 and 1024, each to 10−1310^{-13} (measured at most 1.1⋅10−151.1 \cdot 10^{-15}). In addition w0=sin⁡(π/4096)w_0 = \sin(\pi/4096) for the long sine window, KBD rises more gently than the sine at the start and is monotonic, short KBD (α=6\alpha = 6) is not merely the compressed long one.
  • Sequences: the wish 0 0 1 1 0 0 0 1 0 1 0 0 becomes LANG START KURZ KURZ STOPP LANG START KURZ KURZ KURZ STOPP LANG; every frame wanted short becomes KURZ, exactly one additionally had to become short. LANG→KURZ, KURZ→LANG, START→LANG and a short beginning are rejected, START→STOPP is valid. Wrong sequence lengths are rejected; frame counts for 0, 1, MM, M+1M+1 values.
  • Round trip, each with both checks “0 wrong values” and “unrounded below 10−610^{-6}”: only long with sine and with KBD; all short; tight transitions with a shape change at every frame; START/KURZ/STOPP with a shape change at every position; six random sequences; lengths 0, 1, M−1M-1, MM, M+1M+1, 2M2M; full scale +8,388,607+8{,}388{,}607 and −8,388,608-8{,}388{,}608; single impulse; chirp; M=128M = 128 with a random sequence.
  • Without transition: LANG directly before KURZ does not reconstruct (error above 100). This is checked as a fact, to make clear that START and STOPP are necessary.

Limits

  • MM must be a power of two from 32 upwards (default 1024), so that m=M/8m = M/8 is a usable power of two as well.
  • There is no transient detection yet: the caller specifies which frames become short; planen only turns that into a valid sequence.
  • No quantiser yet and no stream format for coefficients. From the quantiser on, the way back is deliberately inexact, and the criterion changes from “bit-exact” to measures from framework-audiometrik.
  • Not thread-safe; the work arrays are reused.
  • The whole signal is held in memory, as are the coefficients of all frames.
