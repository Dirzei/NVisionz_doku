MDCT and filter bank – quantiser and lossy method

The calculation behind Quantisierer and method 4 of the stream: bands and step sizes, how much noise the masking threshold and the threshold in quiet allow, the detection of attacks, mid and side, the frame format and what a missing frame costs. With measured values from the probe and limits.

What this is about

Up to the filter bank every sample comes back unchanged. The quantiser is the step where that ends: the coefficients of the MDCT are transmitted rounded, as coarsely as hearing permits. The method is identifier 4 in the EVAS stream, next to the three lossless ones. From here on success no longer means “bit exact” but is measured: signal-to-noise ratio and noise-to-mask ratio from framework-audiometrik.

Bands and step size

The nn coefficients of a block (n=1024n = 1024 in a long, n=128n = 128 in a short one) are divided into bands. They follow the critical bands of the Bark scale; bands that would hold fewer than four coefficients are merged with the next one. At 48 kHz this gives 25 bands in a long and 12 in a short block.

Each band jj carries an integer step sjs_j from 0 to 255 and with it the step size

Δj=2sj/4,qk=round⁡(ckΔj),ck'=qkΔj.\Delta_j = 2^{\,s_j/4}, \qquad q_k = \mathrm{round}\!\left(\frac{c_k}{\Delta_j}\right), \qquad c_k' = q_k\,\Delta_j .

One step more enlarges the step size by the factor 21/42^{1/4}, that is 1.5 dB. The error of a coefficient is at most Δj/2\Delta_j/2. Encoder and decoder compute the step size of a step in the same method, and without a power function: the four values 20,21/4,21/2,23/42^{0}, 2^{1/4}, 2^{1/2}, 2^{3/4} are written out as numbers, the rest is a power of two. The decoder therefore produces the same values on every machine.

How much noise is allowed

A uniform quantiser of step size Δ\Delta produces, per coefficient, noise of power

σ2=Δ212.\sigma^2 = \frac{\Delta^2}{12} .

Per coefficient as much is allowed as the masking threshold of its critical band permits. It is built from the band energies of the block: every band masks its neighbours, upwards more strongly than downwards, and the sum is lowered by an offset that depends on the quality level QQ (0 to 9, default 5):

A(Q)=1+3QdB,Δj=12σzul,j2,sj=⌊4log2⁡Δj⌋.A(Q) = 1 + 3\,Q \;\text{dB}, \qquad \Delta_j = \sqrt{12\,\sigma^2_{\text{zul},j}}, \qquad s_j = \left\lfloor 4\log_2 \Delta_j \right\rfloor .

The masking calculation is the same one the measuring chain uses to judge the result (there with an offset of 10 dB, which corresponds to level 3). Level 5 therefore places the noise 6 dB below the threshold of the measurement.

Threshold in quiet

Masking falls without limit with the distance from the masker. Far from a tone it would demand an accuracy nobody hears. In every critical band at least the noise corresponding to a tone at the threshold in quiet is therefore allowed:

Lq(f)=3.64(fkHz)−0.8−6.5exp⁡(−0.6(fkHz−3.3)2)+10−3(fkHz)4dB.L_q(f) = 3{.}64\left(\frac{f}{\text{kHz}}\right)^{-0{.}8} - 6{.}5\,\exp\!\left(-0{.}6\left(\frac{f}{\text{kHz}} - 3{.}3\right)^{2}\right) + 10^{-3}\left(\frac{f}{\text{kHz}}\right)^{4} \;\text{dB} .

The level refers to the assumption that a full-scale sine is reproduced at 96 dB; LqL_q is capped at these 96 dB. Within a band the smallest value of the threshold counts, not the one at its centre: the top band reaches from 15.5 kHz to half the sampling rate, and at its centre the threshold would already lie above full scale.

The smallest step

The source is rounded to integers. Its rounding noise has the standard deviation n/24\sqrt{n/24} in the coefficient domain. The smallest step size is eight times that,

Δmin⁡=8n3,\Delta_{\min} = \sqrt{\frac{8\,n}{3}} ,

that is step 23 for 1024 and step 17 for 128 coefficients. The rounding noise of the source then falls to zero instead of leaving isolated ones in every band.

Attacks and short frames

Without a countermeasure the quantisation noise of an attack spreads over the whole long window and appears before it as a pre-echo. An attack is detected from the energy of the first difference of the signal, measured in sections of M/8=128M/8 = 128 samples: if a section lies more than ten times above the mean of the eight preceding ones and above −60 dB of full scale, it counts as an attack. The frame in whose middle half it lies becomes short, because that is where the eight short blocks are:

t=⌊p+M/2M⌋.t = \left\lfloor \frac{p + M/2}{M} \right\rfloor .

Fensterbank.planen turns the wishes into a valid sequence with transition windows. All channels share one sequence.

Mid and side

With two channels every frame is quantised twice: as left and right, and as

m=12(l+r),s=12(l−r),l=m+s,r=m−s.m = \tfrac12 (l + r), \qquad s = \tfrac12 (l - r), \qquad l = m + s, \quad r = m - s .

The version with fewer bits is taken – by actual cost, as in the lossless methods. With two identical channels the side is zero and costs one byte per frame.

The frame in the stream

The raster is M=1024M = 1024. A signal of NN samples gives ⌈N/M⌉+1\lceil N/M\rceil + 1 frames, one more than the lossless methods, because the windows overlap. The envelope (sync word, body length, CRC-16) is the same as there. The body:

Frame number32determines the position in the signal

per channel and per block (one in a long, eight in a short frame):
Band count8bands up to the last occupied one; 0 means the block is empty

Steps8, then Ricethe first step, the following ones as differences; occupied bands only
ValuesRicethe quantised values of the occupied bands in sequence

The bit per band is not a refinement: Rice needs at least one bit per value, and a pure tone occupies few of two dozen bands. Silence costs one byte per frame and channel.

The decoder limits the result to the value range of the word width, because the noise can lift a full-scale source above it.

What follows

  • The overall checksum in the header refers to the source. The decoder cannot recompute it and does not compare it for this method.
  • A missing frame affects two rasters. Each frame can still be read on its own (no bit reservoir, no backward pointer). Because of the overlap, however, half an edge is missing in both neighbours as well: in lenient mode 2M2M samples are affected, everything before and after comes back unchanged.
  • The encoder may use floating point with logarithms, the decoder may not. Choosing the steps is the encoder’s business alone; two machines may in rare borderline cases write different streams, but read every stream identically.

  • Building blocks: step sizes exact (step 4 is 2, step 40 is 1024), offset per level, smallest step, threshold in quiet at 1 kHz (3.37 dB) and 3.3 kHz (−4.98 dB), bands without gaps and never narrower than four.
  • Choice of steps: sixteen times the amplitude raises every step by 16; quality level 9 against 1 gives steps finer by 15 or 16; the error per coefficient is at most half a step size.
  • Round trip: silence returns as silence (126 bytes for 5000 samples in two channels); the lengths 0, 1, M−1M-1, MM, M+1M+1, 2M2M and 3000 are kept; encoding twice gives the same stream; a full-scale square wave stays within the value range.
  • Quality: for noise, stream and signal-to-noise ratio grow with every level, three levels are 9 dB; KBD shape, 24 bit and 8 bit.
  • Two channels: identical channels cost the mono stream plus one byte per frame; channels in opposite phase come back mirrored.
  • Attack: an onset at sample 24,100 makes exactly frame 24 short; the error in the 450 samples before it is about 24 times smaller with short frames than without.
  • Damaged streams: strict mode aborts; in lenient mode exactly the range of the frame is missing. 300 frames with a valid CRC but corrupted content never end in anything but a rejection or a signal.

Limits

  • The hearing model is coarse. No tonality estimate, no masking over time; the assumption “full scale equals 96 dB” is a convention.
  • The noise-to-mask ratio of the measuring chain knows no threshold in quiet. For pure tones it therefore reports noise above the threshold that stems from the omitted tails of the window. The table above gives it for noise only.
  • There is no listening test. What is tested is that the calculation does what it should, not how the result sounds.
  • No fixed bit rate. The quality level fixes the noise, the size follows from the signal.
  • The values are Rice coded: in an occupied band every value costs at least one bit.
  • With mid/side each version computes its threshold on its own; that masking between the channels acts differently is not taken into account.
  • At most two channels; the whole signal is kept in memory. The batch run and the FLAC comparison stay with the lossless methods.
