Hertzian contact and rolling bearings – background

The computation behind Hertz and Waelzlager: elliptical and line contact after Hertz with the complete elliptic integrals, stresses below the contact centre, basic rating life after ISO 281, load spectrum, reliability and Stribeck load distribution – with pseudocode, test cases and limits.

What it is about

Two curved elastic bodies touch under load in a small area with very high pressure. Hertz (1882) gave the solution for smooth frictionless bodies whose gap near the contact can be approximated as quadratic. In a rolling bearing this contact sits between rolling element and raceway; its pressure governs the life, and the distribution of the bearing load over the rolling elements governs which contact is loaded most.

Rating life after ISO 281

After Lundberg and Palmgren the basic rating life reached by 90 % of identical bearings is

L10=(CP)p[106revolutions],p=3 (ball),103 (roller),L10h=106L1060n.L_{10} = \Big(\frac{C}{P}\Big)^p \ [10^6\ \text{revolutions}], \qquad p = 3 \text{ (ball)}, \ \tfrac{10}{3} \text{ (roller)}, \qquad L_{10h} = \frac{10^6 L_{10}}{60\,n}.

CC is the dynamic load rating, P=XFr+YFaP = X F_r + Y F_a the equivalent load. For a load spectrum with revolution fractions qiq_i, P=(∑qiPip/∑qi)1/pP = (\sum q_i P_i^p/\sum q_i)^{1/p} – exactly the load whose life makes the linear damage sum ∑qi/Li\sum q_i/L_i equal to one. For a higher reliability SS, Lnm=a1L10L_{nm} = a_1 L_{10} with

a1=0.95(ln⁡(1/S)ln⁡(1/0.9))2/3+0.05,a_1 = 0.95\Big(\frac{\ln(1/S)}{\ln(1/0.9)}\Big)^{2/3} + 0.05,

a Weibull distribution with shape 1.5 and a minimum life of 0.05 L10L_{10}; the ISO 281 table (0.64 at 95 %, 0.25 at 99 %) is rounded from it.

How it is checked

  • Ball on plane in closed form, crossed equal cylinders as a circle, swapped axes equal, force 23πabp0=P\tfrac23\pi a b p_0 = P.
  • Elliptical contact against the Boussinesq integration at seven points.
  • Line contact in closed form, shear maxima against Johnson, surface σr=−(1+2ν)p0/2\sigma_r = -(1 + 2\nu)p_0/2.
  • L10L_{10}, hours, load spectrum against the damage sum, a1a_1 against the ISO table, JrJ_r against the gamma function.

Limits

Hertz: smooth, frictionless, linear elastic, contact small against the radii of curvature; no edge loading of short rollers. Life without the factor aISOa_{ISO} (lubrication, contamination, fatigue limit). Stribeck without clearance, ring deformation or centrifugal force of the rolling elements.
