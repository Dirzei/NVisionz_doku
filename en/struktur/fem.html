Plane frames of beam elements – background

The computation behind Rahmen: degrees of freedom, element stiffness after Euler-Bernoulli and Timoshenko, consistent mass, rotation into the global system, supports and the generalised eigenproblem – with pseudocode, checks and limits.

What it is about

A plane frame consists of straight members rigidly connected at nodes. Each node has three degrees of freedom: the displacements uu, vv and the rotation θ\theta. From the element matrices follow the static system Kx=fK\,x = f and the eigenproblem Kx=ω2MxK\,x = \omega^2 M\,x of free vibration.

Element stiffness

In the local system the axial part carries EA/LEA/L, the bending part the cubic Hermite shape function. With Przemieniecki's shear parameter

Φ=12EIκGAL2,c=EIL3(1+Φ)\Phi = \frac{12\,EI}{\kappa\,G A\,L^2}, \qquad c = \frac{EI}{L^3 (1 + \Phi)}

the bending block for (v1,θ1,v2,θ2)(v_1, \theta_1, v_2, \theta_2) is

Kb=c(126L−126L6L(4+Φ)L2−6L(2−Φ)L2−12−6L12−6L6L(2−Φ)L2−6L(4+Φ)L2).K_b = c \begin{pmatrix} 12 & 6L & -12 & 6L \\ 6L & (4+\Phi)L^2 & -6L & (2-\Phi)L^2 \\ -12 & -6L & 12 & -6L \\ 6L & (2-\Phi)L^2 & -6L & (4+\Phi)L^2 \end{pmatrix}.

Φ=0\Phi = 0 is Euler-Bernoulli; with Φ>0\Phi > 0 the beam becomes softer by the shear part (Timoshenko). The mass is consistent (ρAL/420 times the well-known Hermite mass matrix, plus ρAL/6 · [[2, 1], [1, 2]] axially). For Timoshenko the same mass without rotary inertia is used; for slender beams that suffices.

Derivation

For bending the transverse displacement in the element is cubic, v(x)=∑kNk(x)qkv(x) = \sum_k N_k(x)\,q_k with the Hermite functions (ξ=x/L\xi = x/L)

N1=1−3ξ2+2ξ3,N2=L(ξ−2ξ2+ξ3),N3=3ξ2−2ξ3,N4=L(ξ3−ξ2),N_1 = 1 - 3\xi^2 + 2\xi^3, \quad N_2 = L(\xi - 2\xi^2 + \xi^3), \quad N_3 = 3\xi^2 - 2\xi^3, \quad N_4 = L(\xi^3 - \xi^2),

which set displacement and slope at the ends individually to 1. The strain energy 12∫EIv''2dx\tfrac12\int EI\,v''^2\,dx gives Kjk=∫0LEINj''Nk''dxK_{jk} = \int_0^L EI\,N_j''N_k''\,dx – exactly the block above with Φ=0\Phi = 0; the kinetic energy 12∫ρAv˙2dx\tfrac12\int \rho A\,\dot v^2\,dx gives Mjk=∫0LρANjNkdxM_{jk} = \int_0^L \rho A\,N_j N_k\,dx, i.e. ρAL/420\rho A L/420 times (156,22L,54,−13L;4L2,13L,−3L2;…)(156, 22L, 54, -13L; 4L^2, 13L, -3L^2; \dots). Axially the shape is linear, giving EA/LEA/L and ρAL/6(2,1;1,2)\rho A L/6\,(2, 1; 1, 2). Both integrals are recomputed with sympy.

Why the statics are exact. Without distributed load in the element EIv''''=0EI\,v'''' = 0 holds: the true deflection is cubic and lies in the trial space. With nodal loads the solution therefore hits the nodal values exactly, whatever the number of elements – the probe demands 1e-12. Distributed loads enter as equivalent nodal loads; the nodal values are exact then as well (Tong), only the shape between nodes is not.

Timoshenko from the literature. With shear deformation an element has two deformation parts; Przemieniecki combines them in the factor 1/(1+Φ)1/(1 + \Phi) and the modified diagonal terms (4+Φ)(4 + \Phi), (2−Φ)(2 - \Phi). Check: a single element as cantilever gives v=PL3/(3EI)+PL/(κGA)v = PL^3/(3EI) + PL/(\kappa G A), the sum of bending and shear part – recomputed.

What follows. The natural frequencies are Rayleigh-Ritz approximations in the trial subspace and therefore lie above the true ones with the consistent mass; the error of a natural frequency falls like h4h^4 (twice the order of the trial functions). A lumped mass matrix breaks this bound.

With c=cos⁡αc = \cos\alpha, s=sin⁡αs = \sin\alpha of the element angle each element matrix is rotated by TT (K=TTKlocalTK = T^T K_{local} T) and added into the global matrix.

Supports and solution

Locked degrees of freedom are removed. Statically LinAlg.solve solves the reduced system; without supports KK is singular, and that is reported. The natural frequencies come from Verallgemeinert.dicht(K, M) in framework-math (generalised symmetric eigenproblem).

for each element: length L and angle α from the nodes
    K_e ← axial and bending block (Φ = 0 or 12 EI/(κ G A L²));  M_e ← consistent mass
    K ← K + Tᵀ K_e T;  M ← M + Tᵀ M_e T
remove locked degrees of freedom
static:  x ← solve(K, f)                            singular → message “no supports”
dynamic: ω² ← eigenvalues of (K, M), ascending;  ω ← √ω²

Source: envisionz-framework-struktur/src/envisionz/struktur/fem/Rahmen.java.

How it is checked

  • Cantilever with end load, 4 elements: v=PL3/(3EI)v = PL^3/(3EI), θ=PL2/(2EI)\theta = PL^2/(2EI), u=NL/(EA)u = NL/(EA) to 1e-12; for Timoshenko PL/(κGA)PL/(\kappa G A) is added.
  • Simply supported beam with distributed load (equivalent nodal loads): midspan 5qL4/(384EI)5qL^4/(384EI), support rotation qL3/(24EI)qL^3/(24EI) to 1e-12.
  • Cantilever, 40 elements: three lowest natural frequencies against (βL)2EI/(ρAL4)(\beta L)^2\sqrt{EI/(\rho A L^4)} to 2e-6 (error like (βh)4(\beta h)^4), consistent mass from above; first axial mode π2LE/ρ\frac{\pi}{2L}\sqrt{E/\rho} from above to 1e-4.
  • Cantilever rotated by 0.7 rad: the same natural frequencies to 1e-10.
  • Error cases: same node, negative Young's modulus, invalid degree of freedom, wrong load length, missing supports.

Limits

Plane only, linear only (no buckling, no large deformations), dense matrices. Rotary inertia is missing in the mass.
