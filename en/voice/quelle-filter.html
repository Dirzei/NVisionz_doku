Source-filter synthesis – background

How a pulse and a few resonators become a vowel: glottal pulse, noise, formant filters, parallel summation, gliding between sounds and the master control “humanity” (GlottalSource, NoiseSource, Formant, Synthesizer, VoiceProfile).

What this is about

A human voice arises in two separate stages. The vocal folds open and close periodically and produce a pulse train with many overtones – the source. The vocal tract (throat, oral cavity, lips) acts on it as a resonator that reinforces some frequency ranges: the formants. Which vowel is heard is decided by the position of the first two formants, not by the pitch. This source-filter model (Fant 1960) can be rebuilt directly: a pulse generator followed by a few second-order band-pass filters.

This implies a constraint that is tested in the framework: a filter can only amplify what is already there. A pure sine source would give the same tone for any filter setting; the source must carry overtones.

The excitation

Glottal pulse after Rosenberg

Within a period with phase t∈[0,1)t \in [0,1) and open quotient qq (default 0.6) the pulse consists of a rising cosine hump, a faster fall and a closed phase with value zero. With tp=q/2t_p = q/2 and tn=qt_n = q:

g(t)={12(1−cos⁡(πt/tp))t<tpcos⁡(π2t−tptn−tp)tp≤t<tn0tn≤t<1g(t) = \begin{cases} \tfrac12\big(1-\cos(\pi\,t/t_p)\big) & t < t_p\\[1mm] \cos\!\Big(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\,\dfrac{t-t_p}{t_n-t_p}\Big) & t_p \le t < t_n\\[1mm] 0 & t_n \le t < 1 \end{cases}

This gives the typical source spectrum, falling at about 12 dB per octave. Because the pulse falls so steeply at the top, a high formant such as the second one of /i/ (about 2100 Hz) would receive almost nothing; aspiration (below) remedies that.

Jitter and shimmer

Two micro-fluctuations make the difference between robot and human. At every period change, fundamental frequency and amplitude are redrawn:

F0'=F0(1+uJ),A'=1+uS,u∈[−1,1]uniformly distributedF_0' = F_0\,(1+u\,J),\qquad A' = 1+u\,S,\qquad u \in [-1,1]\ \text{uniformly distributed}

Here JJ is the jitter (relative spread of F0F_0) and SS the shimmer (relative spread of the amplitude). The random sequence depends on a seed: same seed, same signal.

Noise and aspiration

The noise source is white noise wnw_n with a one-pole low-pass for colouring:

rn=crn−1+(1−c)wn,c∈[0;0.99]r_n = c\,r_{n-1} + (1-c)\,w_n,\qquad c \in [0;\,0.99]

c=0c = 0 is bright (like /s/), larger cc darker (like /sh/). As aspiration, a small share of noise is mixed into the voice, but only while the glottis is open (openGate: 1 in the open phase, 0.2 otherwise). In humans breath escapes only when the vocal folds are open; constant noise sounds like a band, modulated noise like breath.

The formant filter

A formant is a second-order band-pass with centre frequency ff and bandwidth BB, in the version of the Audio EQ Cookbook (RBJ). Mathematically it is a pole pair in the zz-plane that is excited by the source signal. With ω0=2πf/fs\omega_0 = 2\pi f/f_s, Q=f/BQ = f/B and α=sin⁡ω0/(2Q)\alpha = \sin\omega_0/(2Q):

b0=α1+α,b1=0,b2=−α1+α,a1=−2cos⁡ω01+α,a2=1−α1+αb_0 = \frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha},\quad b_1 = 0,\quad b_2 = -\frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha},\quad a_1 = \frac{-2\cos\omega_0}{1+\alpha},\quad a_2 = \frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha}

yn=b0xn+b2xn−2−a1yn−1−a2yn−2y_n = b_0 x_n + b_2 x_{n-2} - a_1 y_{n-1} - a_2 y_{n-2}

Narrow (small BB) means more ringing and more resonant, wide means more damped. The coefficients may be set at run time while the filter state is kept – so moving formants do not produce clicks.

The synthesizer

Lip radiation. At the mouth the tract acts like a differentiator with about +6 dB per octave. This is a pre-emphasis of the excitation gng_n:

sn=gn−0.96gn−1s_n = g_n - 0.96\,g_{n-1}

Parallel formant sum after Klatt: every formant filters the same signal sns_n, and the outputs are added with fixed weights:

yn=1.0F1+0.95F2+0.7F3+0.22F4+0.10F5y_n = 1.0\,F_1 + 0.95\,F_2 + 0.7\,F_3 + 0.22\,F_4 + 0.10\,F_5

F1 to F3 come from the vowel or sound table; F4 and F5 are fixed at 3300 Hz (bandwidth 280 Hz) and 4500 Hz (350 Hz) and give the voice “head” and presence; without them a three-formant voice sounds hollow. All formant frequencies are multiplied by the tract scale of the profile (short tract: higher formants, brighter, “smaller” voice).

Gliding (coarticulation). The synthesizer does not jump from target to target. Per sample each parameter approaches its target by the fraction gg:

xn=xn−1+g(xtarget−xn−1),τ=1gfsx_n = x_{n-1} + g\,\big(x_{\text{target}} - x_{n-1}\big),\qquad \tau = \frac{1}{g\,f_s}

This applies to F1 to F3, their bandwidths and to F0F_0. Gliding F0F_0 matters: pitch staircases are the classic robot marker. The speed of the F0F_0 glide depends on the melody depth of the profile, gF0=0.02−0.0184dg_{F_0} = 0.02 - 0.0184\,d with d=(depth−0.2)/0.8d = (\text{depth}-0.2)/0.8: the machine jumps in steps, the human glides.

The voice profile and the master control

VoiceProfile bundles fundamental frequency, melody range, tract scale, breathiness and the humanity h∈[0,1]h \in [0,1]. From hh the liveliness parameters are derived linearly:

J=0.004+0.021h,S=0.010+0.070h,dmelody=0.2+0.8h,b=b0(0.6+0.9h)J = 0.004 + 0.021\,h,\qquad S = 0.010 + 0.070\,h,\qquad d_{\text{melody}} = 0.2 + 0.8\,h,\qquad b = b_0\,(0.6 + 0.9\,h)

At h=0h = 0 the voice speaks almost monotonically and rigidly, at h=1h = 1 micro-variable, breathing and with full sentence melody. The reference voice VoiceProfile.neutral() has F0=130F_0 = 130 Hz, melody range 0.5, tract scale 1.0, h=0.6h = 0.6 and b0=0.15b_0 = 0.15; another preset profile exists.

Decisions in the framework

Decision in the framework: The synthesis sampling rate is fixed at 22,050 Hz. The highest representable frequency is thus 11,025 Hz; the formant frequency is limited to 49 % of the sampling rate. The module has no dependency, not even on framework-api; the access interface VoiceProfileAccess keeps core from depending on the facade.
Decision in the framework: The humanity hh is the single “truth” of liveliness: jitter, shimmer, melody depth and breathiness are derived from it and not set individually. Even at h=0h = 0 jitter 0.004 and shimmer 0.010 remain – the lower bound is not zero.

Worked example

One formant. F1 of /a/ lies at 700 Hz with 80 Hz bandwidth; at 22,050 Hz, ω0=0.1995\omega_0 = 0.1995, Q=8.75Q = 8.75, α=0.01132\alpha = 0.01132. This gives b0=0.01120b_0 = 0.01120, b2=−0.01120b_2 = -0.01120, a1=−1.9384a_1 = -1.9384 and a2=0.9776a_2 = 0.9776. The pole lies at radius a2≈0.9887\sqrt{a_2} \approx 0.9887 – close to the unit circle, hence narrow and ringing.

One pulse. At F0=130F_0 = 130 Hz (reference voice) a period lasts 22050/130≈169.622050/130 \approx 169.6 samples; the glottal pulse is “open” for 0.6⋅169.6≈1020.6\cdot 169.6 \approx 102 of them and zero afterwards.

Glide times. With τ=1/(gfs)\tau = 1/(g f_s): vowel glide g=0.018g = 0.018 gives 2.5 ms, aspiration phase g=0.006g = 0.006 gives 7.6 ms, burst g=0.05g = 0.05 gives 0.9 ms. The F0F_0 glide ranges from 2.3 ms (d=0d=0, machine) to 28 ms (d=1d=1, “lively”).

Humanity. h=0h = 0: J=0.004J = 0.004, S=0.010S = 0.010, melody depth 0.2. h=1h = 1: J=0.025J = 0.025, S=0.080S = 0.080, melody depth 1.0. The reference voice with h=0.6h = 0.6 has J=0.0166J = 0.0166, S=0.052S = 0.052, melody depth 0.68 and breathiness 0.15⋅1.14=0.1710.15\cdot 1.14 = 0.171.

How it is checked

What can be tested follows from acoustics, not from a recording; whether the voice is intelligible is decided by the ear.

  • VoiceTest (116 checks): F1 is larger for the open /a/ than for /i/ and /u/, F2 of /i/ lies far above that of /u/, F1 < F2 < F3 holds everywhere; a formant filter passes more at its centre frequency than far beside it, and a narrow bandwidth damps more strongly beside it than a wide one.
  • The source carries overtones (measured at the fundamental and the first two harmonics); without jitter the waveform is periodic to within 0.05 per sample; darkly coloured noise changes more slowly between neighbouring samples than bright noise (the difference between /s/ and /sh/).
  • Stimmprobe (46 checks): the fundamental frequency is recomputed from the generated signal with an autocorrelation. If 100, 150 or 220 Hz is requested, the measurement must lie within 5 %; it reads no number from the module. An absurd negative pitch yields only a finite output (lower clamp 50 Hz).
  • Formant positions are measured on the isolated resonator with noise excitation (according to the module documentation F1 and F2 of the vowels a, e, i, o, u deviate by less than 6 %), because in a voiced vowel the strongest peak is often a harmonic of F0F_0 and not the formant.
  • Eleven counter-experiments (one component deliberately corrupted each) are all red; two stayed green at first and were sharpened.

Limits

  • Formant synthesis is small and fully traceable but audibly synthetic. That is the choice here, not a defect of the method that more parameters could cure.
  • The formant table is an approximation from the phonetics literature for a neutral reference; fine tuning is done by ear.
  • There are only three variable formants; F4 and F5 are fixed. For very high or very low voices the simple scaling is only approximately right.
  • The sampling rate of 22,050 Hz is not adjustable; more bandwidth for fricatives above 11 kHz is not provided.
