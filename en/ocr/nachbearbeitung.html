Post-processing and calibrated confidence

What comes after the network: the most probable readings of a line over all paths, the choice among them with a character language model and a list of technical words – as a weight, never as a replacement –, and a confidence that keeps what it reports.

Readings over all paths

For each window the network gives a distribution over the classes; class 0 is the blank class. A path picks one class per window; the corresponding reading results from merging repetitions and dropping the blank class. The greedy reading takes the single path of the strongest classes. The probability of a reading, however, is the sum over all paths that collapse to it – and the most probable reading need not be that of the strongest path. Two windows with (blank 0.4; a 0.35; b 0.25): the strongest path is blank twice, i.e. the empty reading with 0.16; the reading “a” collects three paths (blank-a, a-blank, a-a) and reaches 0.4025.

The prefix beam search keeps two sums per reading ℓ\ell: pb(ℓ)p_b(\ell) over the paths ending in the blank class and pn(ℓ)p_n(\ell) over those ending in the last character. A window with distribution yy continues them as follows (cc a character, ee the last character of ℓ\ell):

pb'(ℓ)←pb'(ℓ)+(pb(ℓ)+pn(ℓ))y0,pn'(ℓ)←pn'(ℓ)+pn(ℓ)ye,p_b'(\ell) \leftarrow p_b'(\ell) + \bigl(p_b(\ell) + p_n(\ell)\bigr)\, y_0, \qquad p_n'(\ell) \leftarrow p_n'(\ell) + p_n(\ell)\, y_e, pn'(ℓc)←pn'(ℓc)+{pb(ℓ)ycc=e(pb(ℓ)+pn(ℓ))ycc≠ep_n'(\ell c) \leftarrow p_n'(\ell c) + \begin{cases} p_b(\ell)\, y_c & c = e \\ \bigl(p_b(\ell) + p_n(\ell)\bigr)\, y_c & c \ne e \end{cases}

The case distinction is the core: a repeated character extends the reading only if the blank class stood in between – otherwise it merges with the previous one (“a, blank, a” is “aa”, “a, a, a” is “a”). After each window the most probable readings remain (default 12); per window only classes above 0.001 are followed. All arithmetic is done in logarithms.

Character language model and word list

A word is a sequence of letters between two boundaries. The model gives the probability of the next character after up to three preceding ones (four-grams); the boundary counts as a character, so the model also knows how words begin and end. Smoothing follows Witten and Bell: with the count n(h,c)n(h, c) of character cc after context hh, the total N(h)N(h), the number T(h)T(h) of distinct continuations and the context h'h' shortened by one character

P(c∣h)=n(h,c)+T(h)P(c∣h')N(h)+T(h)P(c \mid h) = \frac{n(h, c) + T(h)\, P(c \mid h')}{N(h) + T(h)}

Below the empty context stands the uniform distribution over the VV characters of the model; a context never seen falls back entirely to the shorter one. Every character thus keeps a probability above zero: a word the list does not know is less probable but never impossible. For the model built from the single word AB, V=3V = 3 and P(A∣word start)=11/12P(\mathrm{A} \mid \text{word start}) = 11/12, for B and the end 1/241/24 each.

The model is learned from word lists in order of frequency (5000 German and 5000 English words from the project's own documentation pages). A list carries no counts; the word at rank rr counts with weight 1/(r+10)1/(r + 10). A lower-case word also counts capitalised with one fifth (sentence start). The score of a line is the sum of the gains of its words over the uniform distribution,

G(w)=∑iln⁡(P(wi∣wi−3..i−1)V)+ln⁡(P(end∣w)V),G(w) = \sum_{i} \ln\bigl(P(w_i \mid w_{i-3..i-1})\, V\bigr) + \ln\bigl(P(\text{end} \mid w)\, V\bigr),

plus a fixed bonus for a word of the list with at least three letters (also capitalised at the start of a sentence or all in capitals). Digits, punctuation and letters outside the model (Greek) do not count; a letter sequence touching a digit – identifiers, units attached to numbers – remains unscored.

The choice: a weight, never a replacement

Only readings the recogniser offers are candidates: the greedy reading with the word rules and the best of the beam search. For each the optical probability over all paths is computed. The largest sum

ln⁡Poptical(ℓ)+λB(ℓ)\ln P_{\text{optical}}(\ell) + \lambda\, B(\ell)

wins, with the score BB and the weight λ=0.5\lambda = 0.5 (word bonus 4). Three bounds keep the language model in its role:

  • Margin. A reading is eligible only if its optical probability is at most a factor of 20 below the best. If “Drnck” is read with 0.97 against 0.03, it stays, even if the list only knows “Druck”; at 0.94 against 0.06 the language model may choose.
  • Digits stay. The digits of a reading must be the same as in the greedy reading. The language model never decides on a number.
  • Lines without letters stay as read; no readings are searched at all.

If the greedy reading stays, its confidence stays. If another one wins, the confidence is at most its share of the optical probability of all readings considered: what only the language model brought forward is never more certain than the optics allow.

Calibrated confidence

The raw confidence of a reading is the smallest probability of a character read, but at most the complement of the most probable omitted one. It orders correctly – uncertain readings are wrong more often – but its value is not a hit rate. The calibration maps it to the probability that the reading is entirely correct: a reported 0.95 should be right in 95 % of the cases.

The method is isotonic regression: from pairs (raw confidence, right or wrong) of a test set, the monotonically increasing step function with the least squared error. The pairs are sorted; neighbouring blocks whose hit rates fall instead of rise are merged into one block with the common rate until the sequence rises. It needs no assumption about the shape of the curve. Afterwards the blocks are merged into steps of at least m=nm = \sqrt{n} pairs – otherwise the chance of single pairs would determine the curve. A step of nsn_s pairs with ksk_s hits reports (ks+12)/(ns+1)(k_s + \tfrac12)/(n_s + 1): twenty correct readings do not make a certainty. Between the steps the map is interpolated linearly.

The quality is measured by the expected calibration error: the readings sorted by reported probability into ten bands of equal width, per band the distance between mean report and hit rate, weighted by the share of readings in the band. For a raw confidence ss that hits with s2s^2 it is ∫01(s−s2)ds=1/6\int_0^1 (s - s^2)\,ds = 1/6; after calibration on 4000 such pairs it is 0.012 on fresh pairs.

Measured

The measurement uses painted lines (words, numbers, units) in six typefaces that were not part of the training, each in five kinds of ageing; the same line is read greedily and with the language model. The character error is counted without blanks.

For the second row the language is learned from one half of the word lists and the lines are made from the other: even words the list does not know are read better on average, because their letter sequences fit the language. A weight of 1 instead of 0.5 reverses this (9 lines better, 9 worse) – hence the cautious default. The gain is real but limited: most of the remaining errors are not confusions among which the readings would offer a choice.

Calibration: learned on 1200 lines and measured on 1200 others, the calibration error falls from 0.100 to 0.018.

Files next to the model

The language lies as sprache.txt in the model folder (word list with weights; the model is built on loading), the calibration as NAME.kal next to NAME.gew (steps as text). The text reader takes both if they are there; if one is missing it reads without. They are produced with cmd.ocr.Ocr -op:sprachmodell and -op:nachlese -kalibrierung: (see command line).

Limits

  • The model knows words, not sentences: it says nothing about the sequence of words.
  • The calibration holds for the kind of material it was learned on – here painted, aged lines. For real scans it has to be learned again on one's own test set (the same call).
  • Calibrated is the confidence of the whole line, not that of a single character.
  • Number columns and the format pass of the table recognition do not use the post-processing.
  • Greek letters and words attached to digits are not scored by the model; there the optics alone still decide.
