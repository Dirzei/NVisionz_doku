The live image – background

How a camera picture reaches the screen: which thread opens the camera, which receives the pictures, which computes and where drawing happens.

What this is about

The magnifier should enlarge a live image without stalling, even when the computation takes longer than the gap between two camera pictures. It therefore separates four things: opening the camera runs in the background, the pictures arrive on the camera's thread, computation runs on a thread of its own, and drawing happens only on the event thread.

The path of a camera picture

Start calls liveStarten on the event thread. It hands the opening to BusyRunner.run; in the background the camera is opened with the chosen format and started. When the callback comes back, the card first asks whether the panel has been closed in the meantime – then it releases the camera at once instead of attaching it.

Every picture arrives on the camera's thread. neuesKamerabild copies it, measures the brightness on the way for the hint about a black picture, and requests a computation. Several requests merge into one: as long as a job is still waiting, no second one is queued; the waiting job takes the newest picture when it starts. The computing thread enlarges through the model into a back buffer, swaps it with the front buffer and asks for a repaint. If the camera reports an error, it goes through invokeLater to liveStoppen, which releases the camera and shows the message.

Limits

  • At most one picture is computed at a time. If pictures arrive faster than they are computed, intermediate pictures are dropped; the display always shows the newest.
  • A camera error stops the live image. The app does not make a second attempt by itself.
