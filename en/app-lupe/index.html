envisionz-app-lupe

Magnifying stamps and small print

Overview of the app, as of 6 October 2026. A magnifier for people with severely reduced sight: magnify images or the live image of a camera up to 16 times, with enhanced contrast or in false colours, and announce every change in large type. The desktop app is the first version; an Android version using the same image core is planned.

What the app is for

Someone with poor sight often cannot read a stamp, a label or a package leaflet with the naked eye, not even with a hand lens. The app turns it into a large, calm picture on the screen: strongly magnified, sharpened, optionally with enhanced contrast, in greyscale, inverted or with emphasised edges. Every change appears as a large announcement in the image, so nobody has to rely on small labels.

Image core and operating model live in separate, platform-neutral modules that deliberately do without Swing and AWT and are compiled at language level Java 17. The planned Android version can therefore use the same core; the desktop app is just the user interface around it.

How to open images, start the camera, choose magnification and display mode, and what happens when the camera is switched off is shown on the How to use page.

What it can do

Image source: camera or images

The live image of a built-in or plugged-in camera, under Windows through Media Foundation, without third-party libraries and without C code. The camera is switched on only on request, the microphone never. Alternatively opened images (PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF, PPM, PGM) act as the camera: the magnifier works with them exactly as with the live image.

Magnification and view

Nine zoom steps from 1 to 16 times, by mouse wheel, keys, side bar or slider. The view is moved with the arrow keys or by dragging. Freezing holds the picture, for example to read a spot at leisure.

Display modes

Natural colours, contrast enhanced, fine detail enhanced, greyscale, yellow on black, white on blue, negative and edges emphasised. Plus adjustable sharpening and the choice of smoothing when magnifying.

Announcements

Every change – zoom step, display mode, view, freezing – appears in large type at the bottom of the image, in German or English. On the phone it is to be spoken; on the desktop speech is in progress.

Respecting the camera block

If the camera is switched off with a key on the device or in the privacy settings, the app reports it and does not try to bypass the block. If the driver then delivers a black or an unchanging replacement picture, the app says "black" or "still image" instead of "Live".

Data and formats

  • Read: PNG, JPEG, BMP, GIF, PPM and PGM; images over 4096 pixels per edge are reduced by averaging on loading.
  • Camera: the formats the camera offers (NV12, YUY2, MJPEG and others); the largest with at least 15 frames per second is suggested.
  • Write: the displayed image as PNG.
  • Workspace: settings, list of opened images and the selected image. The camera does not start by itself on restore.

What it builds on

  • framework-imaging: the platform-neutral image core – cropping, scaling, contrast, palettes, sharpening.
  • framework-lupe: the operating model – zoom steps, view, display modes, freezing, announcements and settings.
  • framework-kamera: the camera interface with a camera made of image files; framework-kamera-desktop the Windows camera through the Foreign Function & Memory API.
  • swing: side bar with cards, icons, background runner.
  • api: app interface and workspace state.

The same computation without a user interface is offered by the command-line tool cmd-lupe.

Status and limits

  • Images from files, the live camera under Windows and the native program are finished and tested on the device, as are the messages for a switched-off camera.
  • Cameras exist only under Windows so far; on other systems the magnifier works with images.
  • Spoken announcements on the desktop and the Android version are still to come.
