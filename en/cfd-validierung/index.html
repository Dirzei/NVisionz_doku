Validation of the example cases

One page for every example case of the CFD collection with a stored result: case summary and result view and, where a reference is stored, theory of the reference, comparison and key figures.

What these pages are for

Where a reference exists, the example cases of framework-cfd carry the data for a comparison: a profile from the literature, an analytical solution, the shock relations or a conserved quantity. The pages show what the solver gives on the supplied mesh and how far it is from the reference. The result is the stored solution of the case; the comparison is the same as in the Case tab of the CFD app, and the numbers come from it, not from hand entry. In the plots the reference is shown as dots and the computation as a line (at all positions of the wall or the cross-section). Where the reference is a computation by other authors, the page says so.
