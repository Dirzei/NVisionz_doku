envisionz-framework-cfd · Validation of the example cases

NLR QE 0.11-0.75-1.375, design point

Symmetric quasi-elliptical NLR aerofoil after AGARD AR-138, appendix A2, inviscid at the design point without incidence. The design solution is shock-free: the supersonic region closes without a shock.

What the case shows

Purpose

The case checks the inviscid transonic flow around an aerofoil at Mach 0.7861 without incidence against a known solution: the aerofoil is designed so that the supersonic region above its thickest part closes without a shock.

Theory of the reference

The reference is the design solution of the aerofoil from the data collection AGARD AR-138 (R91): an exact solution of the potential equation, obtained with the hodograph method, in which the flow is computed in the plane of the velocity and the aerofoil is only determined from it. It is not a measurement. Compared is the pressure coefficient at the aerofoil wall,

cp=p−p∞12ρ∞U∞2,c_p = \frac{p - p_\infty}{\tfrac{1}{2}\,\rho_\infty U_\infty^2} ,

at 47 points of the design solution.

What the picture should show

The result view shows the Mach number: above the thickest part of the aerofoil lies a supersonic region. In the diagram (cpc_p decreasing upwards) line (computation) and points (design solution) should lie close together. The RMS deviation is 0.0701, the largest deviation 0.207.

Limits of the comparison

  • The design solution holds for the potential equation, the computation solves the Euler equations. As long as no shock occurs both flows are the same; a shock in the computation is a deviation from the design.
  • Shock-free solutions are sensitive: even small changes of Mach number, contour or mesh produce a weak shock at the end of the supersonic region. A deviation there is usual for an Euler method.
  • The contour of the computation is built from the tables of the design solution and is thus itself an approximation of the designed aerofoil.
  • The tolerance (RMS 0.0500) is a guard against regressions, not a measure of accuracy.

Result view

Validation

The reference is the pressure distribution of the design solution (R91, appendix A2, table 2.1): an exact solution of the potential equation from the hodograph method, not a measurement. The diagram shows the computation as a line and the design solution as points.
