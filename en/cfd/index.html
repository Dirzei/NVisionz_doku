envisionz-framework-cfd

Flow computation (CFD core)

Computational core without a user interface for compressible flows on unstructured grids: face-based finite-volume grid with checks and own generators, Riemann solvers and a central flux, second-order reconstruction with limiters, explicit and implicit time integration, characteristic boundaries up to the mixing plane, Navier-Stokes with turbulence and transition models, LES, mixtures, real gases and chemistry.

4dependencies: fileformats, compress, math, thermo
4cell types: tetrahedron, pyramid, prism, hexahedron
5flux methods: roe, lmroe, hllc, ausm, zentral
47probes, 376 counter-probes

Grid and geometry envisionz.cfd.netz, geometrie, erzeugen, ein, aus

The grid lives in flat arrays: nodes, cells and faces as CSR lists, owner and neighbour per face, interior faces first, boundary faces per boundary behind them. Face vectors and volumes are computed so that the sums over closed hulls vanish. The grid check reports errors and key figures before computing. Background, formulas and examples →

Netz, Netzbauer, Zellart, Rand

Netzbauer: knoten(x, y, z), zelle(art, nodes…), randflaeche(name, ring…), randart, baue() → Netz, with face matching via sorted nodes and orientation away from the owner. Netz: arrays eigner, nachbar, flaechenVektor, flaechenMitte, volumen, zellMitte; rand(name), gesamtvolumen(), bandbreite(), umnummeriert(perm), mitKnoten(x).

Geometrie, Wandabstand

Face vector 12∑xi×xi+1\tfrac12\sum\mathbf x_i\times\mathbf x_{i+1}, face centroid, volume and cell centre from pyramids around the node mean. Exact wall distance per cell via a search tree of the wall triangles (for the turbulence models).

Netzpruefung, Umnummerierung

Netzpruefung.pruefe(grid) → errors (volume ≤ 0, open cell, open boundary, orientation, boundary integral), warnings (non-orthogonality above 70°, unnamed boundaries), key figures (volume, skewness, bandwidth); text(). Umnummerierung.rcm(grid): reverse Cuthill-McKee.

Netzgenerator, Dreiecksnetz, Netzverformung

Boxes in four cell types (optionally distorted), channel, wedge, plate, step, sector, plate cascade, cylinder, C-grid around airfoils (Profilkontur), Ringleb; Schaufelpassage (H-grid of a blade row), Rotationskeil. Dreiecksnetz.vernetze: Delaunay with boundary recovery and Ruppert refinement, boundary layer from layers, as a layer grid of prisms and hexahedra. Grid deformation with radial basis functions.

Verfeinerung, Verfeinerungsstufen, Uebertragung

Conforming adaptive refinement of layer grids by jump or error estimator, coarsening over several levels, conservative transfer of fields; cell values onto another grid by local interpolation.

Reading and writing: Netzleser, VtkSchreiber, Bildschreiber, Schnitt

Gmsh (2.2, 4.1), PLOT3D, CGNS and multi-block grids, format recognised by content; VTK for ParaView (text or binary), colour images as PNG without a user interface, cutting planes through 3D grids.

Example – build and check a grid

Netz n = Netzgenerator.quader(8, 8, 8, 1, 1, 1, Zellart.TETRAEDER, 0.2, 7L);
Netzpruefung p = Netzpruefung.pruefe(n);
System.out.print(p.text());          // key figures, warnings, errors, "Netz in Ordnung"
int[] perm = Umnummerierung.rcm(n);  // reduce the bandwidth
Netz m = n.umnummeriert(perm);

Spatial discretisation envisionz.cfd.fluss, rekonstruktion

Per face a numerical flux between the reconstructed states of both sides. Five methods behind one interface; second order by linear reconstruction of the primitive quantities with gradient and limiter. Numerical fluxes → Gradients and limiters →

Flussverfahren: Roe, Hllc, AusmPlusUp, Zentral

fluss(gas, wl, ol, wr, or, nx, ny, nz, f), name(), kopie(); Flussverfahren.nach("roe" | "lmroe" | "hllc" | "ausm" | "zentral[:EPS]"). Roe with Harten's entropy fix (fraction 0.1) and an optional low-Mach correction; HLLC with Einfeldt's wave speeds; AUSM+-up with a reference Mach number; central after Kennedy and Gruber with a pressure sensor for LES.

Gradienten, Limiter

new Gradienten(grid, Art.KLEINSTE_QUADRATE | GREEN_GAUSS), berechne(w, rand, k, grad), randOrte for periodic boundaries. new Limiter(grid, Art.KEINER | BARTH | VENKATAKRISHNAN, K), bezug(scale), vektor(true), berechne(w, rand, k, grad, phi).

Example – one flux

Gas g = Gas.LUFT;
double[] wl = {1.2, 100, 0, 0, 1e5}, wr = {1.1, 90, 5, 0, 0.95e5}, f = new double[5];
Flussverfahren.nach("hllc").fluss(g, wl, 0, wr, 0, 1, 0, 0, f);   // mass, momentum, energy per face

Time integration and solvers envisionz.cfd.loeser

Explicit with SSP-RK3 (time-accurate) or Jameson's four-stage scheme with local time steps (steady); implicit with LU-SGS or Newton-Krylov; dual time stepping for time-accurate implicit computations. Background and formulas →

EulerLoeser

Residual over all faces in parallel, time step from the sum of the spectral radii (|vn|+c)|S|(|v_n| + c)|S| per cell, CFL number, order 1 or 2, wall forces, boundary fluxes per boundary. If a reconstructed density or pressure drops to zero, the face uses the cell values.

Implizit

Backward Euler with local time step, (V/Δt+∂R/∂U)ΔU=−R(V/\Delta t + \partial R/\partial U)\,\Delta U = -R; the CFL number grows with the drop of the residual. LU-SGS (optionally with the boundary derivative) or Newton-Krylov with GMRES and block ILU(0) or algebraic multigrid from framework-math.

Residuumkern, Schrittkern, Sicherung, Schub

Residual or the whole explicit step on the graphics processor (via the ServiceLoader, checked bit-identical to the CPU); exact restart with the whole state of the solver; integral quantities on outlet boundaries from the boundary fluxes.

Boundary conditions envisionz.cfd.rand

Every boundary forms a ghost state from the interior state and the outward normal; the boundary flux is then the ordinary numerical flux. Subsonic boundaries prescribe as many quantities as characteristics enter. Background and formulas →

Randbedingung: Fernfeld, Einlass, Zustrom, Auslass, NichtreflektierenderAuslass

Far field via the Riemann invariants normal to the boundary; inlet with total pressure, total temperature and direction; inflow with velocity or mass flow; outlet with static pressure; partially non-reflecting outlet after Rudy and Strikwerda.

HaftendeWand, Periodisch, Mischungsebene, RadialesGleichgewicht

No-slip wall adiabatic, with fixed temperature or heat flux; inviscid wall and symmetry as a reflection; periodic boundaries translated or rotated; mixing plane with area-weighted means (also in radial bands); outlet with radial equilibrium behind a blade row. Catalogue of boundary types with inputs and defaults: fall.Randarten.

Physical models envisionz.cfd.physik, loeser

Ideal gas as the base case; mixtures, real gases and properties from framework-thermo; laminar viscosity and heat conduction; turbulence models loosely or fully coupled; large-eddy simulation; species transport with reaction. Viscous fluxes → Turbulence → Species and mixtures →

Gas, Gemisch, Realgas, Zaehigkeit, GemischTransport

Primitive and conserved quantities, physical flux, speed of sound, total enthalpy. Thermally ideal mixture with cp(T)c_p(T) from partition functions; real gases via an equation of state (Helmholtz, GERG-2008, cubic, AGA8); viscosity constant or after Sutherland, from properties after Chapman-Enskog and Wilke.

Turbulenz, TurbulenzSst, TurbulenzKe, Transition, Grobstruktur

Spalart-Allmaras (also SA-neg), k-ω SST, k-ω after Wilcox, low-Reynolds k-ε after Chien, wall functions; transition γ-Reθ after Langtry and Menter; LES with Smagorinsky and WALE.

Spezies, Mechanismus

Mass fractions with convection, diffusion (Fick, Lewis number or per species) and formation rates; combustion mechanisms from framework-thermo with stiff chemistry per cell and heat release.

How it is checked

The computation is checked against solutions known independently and against a second computation by hand. The reference solutions live in the module (envisionz.cfd.loesung): exact Riemann problem after Toro, manufactured solutions for the order, oblique shock from the θ-β-M relation, Ringleb flow, Blasius boundary layer.
