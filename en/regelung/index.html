envisionz-framework-regelung

Control engineering and signal processing

Compute cores without a user interface for linear time-invariant systems: transfer functions with frequency response, margins and the Nyquist criterion, root locus, state space with discretisation and pole placement, PID with anti-windup, optimal control (LQR, Kalman), system identification, filter design and spectral analysis up to order analysis during run-up.

Principles of the module

Compute, not draw. The module delivers numbers – frequency responses, poles, controller matrices, spectra. Plotting is up to the chart modules.
Numerics from framework-math. Roots via the companion matrix and EigenQr, discretisation via the matrix exponential, fitting with Levenberg-Marquardt, spectra with our own FFT.
Continuous phase. The phase is built from the phases of the linear factors and does not jump at ±180°; margins and Nyquist counting rely on it.
No silent failure. Improper transfer functions, poles on the imaginary axis for the Nyquist criterion, singular weighting matrices or segment lengths that are not powers of two are reported.

Limits

  • Transfer functions and root locus for single-input single-output systems; multivariable systems via state space and the Riccati solvers.
  • No dead time in transfer functions (only in the identification of the first-order lag).
  • Filters: Butterworth, Chebyshev I and FIR; no elliptic filters, no band-pass.
