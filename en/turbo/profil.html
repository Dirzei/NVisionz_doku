Airfoil shapes and deformation – background

The computation behind Naca, Cst, Parsec, Doppelkreisbogen and Verformung: the NACA four- and five-digit series with thin-airfoil theory, Kulfan's class and shape functions, Sobieczky's eleven parameters, the double-circular-arc compressor profile and shape deformation after Hicks and Henne and as free-form deformation.

What it is about

Designing or optimising an airfoil needs it as a formula with few parameters: a table of coordinates can neither be changed smoothly nor searched sensibly. Each class provides one description and from it a closed contour in the node order of the panel method (trailing edge, lower side, leading edge, upper side, chord 1). The profiles are described, not computed; the flow around them is computed by Panel in framework-thermo.

NACA series

Thickness. Both series share the thickness distribution

yt=5t(0.2969x−0.1260x−0.3516x2+0.2843x3−0.1015x4),y_t = 5t\,\big(0.2969\sqrt{x} - 0.1260\,x - 0.3516\,x^2 + 0.2843\,x^3 - 0.1015\,x^4\big),

with maximum thickness tt near x=0.3x = 0.3 and leading-edge radius r=(5t⋅0.2969)2/2=1.1019t2r = (5t\cdot 0.2969)^2/2 = 1.1019\,t^2. The trailing edge is open (half thickness 0.0105t0.0105\,t); with the coefficient 0.2969−0.1260−0.3516+0.2843=0.10360.2969 - 0.1260 - 0.3516 + 0.2843 = 0.1036 instead of 0.1015 it is closed exactly. The thickness is applied normal to the camber line.

Four digits (e.g. 2412: camber 2 %, at 40 %, thickness 12 %): two parabolas meeting at pp with horizontal tangent, yc=m(2px−x2)/p2y_c = m(2px - x^2)/p^2 ahead of and yc=m(1−2p+2px−x2)/(1−p)2y_c = m(1 - 2p + 2px - x^2)/(1-p)^2 behind pp.

Thin-airfoil theory. With x=(1−cos⁡θ)/2x = (1 - \cos\theta)/2 the characteristics of the camber line are integrals of its slope,

αL0=−1π∫0πdycdx(cos⁡θ−1)dθ,A1=2π∫0πdycdxcos⁡θdθ,cl,i=πA1,\alpha_{L0} = -\frac1\pi\int_0^\pi \frac{dy_c}{dx}(\cos\theta - 1)\,d\theta, \qquad A_1 = \frac2\pi\int_0^\pi \frac{dy_c}{dx}\cos\theta\,d\theta, \qquad c_{l,i} = \pi A_1,

the last being the lift at shock-free entry (A0=0A_0 = 0). For the NACA 2412 this gives αL0=−2.077°\alpha_{L0} = -2.077°. The integration uses Gauss-Kronrod separately ahead of and behind the kink of the camber line.

Five digits (e.g. 23012: design lift 0.15⋅20.15\cdot 2, camber maximum at 0.05⋅30.05\cdot 3, thickness 12 %): cubic up to mm, then straight,

yc=k16(x3−3mx2+m2(3−m)x)(x<m),yc=k1m36(1−x)(x≥m).y_c = \frac{k_1}{6}\big(x^3 - 3mx^2 + m^2(3 - m)x\big) \;(x < m), \qquad y_c = \frac{k_1 m^3}{6}(1 - x) \;(x \ge m).

The series table gives mm and k1k_1; here both are computed from their definition. The camber maximum lies at dyc/dx=0dy_c/dx = 0, i.e. p=m(1−m/3)p = m(1 - \sqrt{m/3}) – solved for mm with Brent. k1k_1 follows from the required design lift, since A1A_1 is linear in k1k_1.

Against the table: mm agrees to the printed three to four digits (at most 3.4⋅10−43.4\cdot 10^{-4}), k1k_1 with the table's mm for 230, 240, 250 to 3⋅10−43\cdot 10^{-4}. For 210 and 220 the computation gives 351.56 and 51.318 instead of the printed 361.4 and 51.64; the deviation lies in the table, not in the definition (with the table value the 210 would have a design lift of 0.308 instead of 0.3).

Class and shape functions (CST)

Following Kulfan each side is

y(ψ)=ψ0.5(1−ψ)⏟C(ψ)∑i=0nAi(ni)ψi(1−ψ)n−i⏟S(ψ)+ψΔyTE.y(\psi) = \underbrace{\psi^{0.5}(1-\psi)}_{C(\psi)}\;\underbrace{\sum_{i=0}^{n} A_i\binom{n}{i}\psi^i(1-\psi)^{n-i}}_{S(\psi)} + \psi\,\Delta y_{TE} .

The class function CC supplies the round nose (ψ\sqrt\psi) and the sharp trailing edge; the shape function SS is a Bernstein polynomial and therefore smooth and well behaved. It follows that the leading-edge radius is A02/2A_0^2/2 and the slope at the trailing edge ΔyTE−An\Delta y_{TE} - A_n. Because yy is linear in the AiA_i, fitting to given points is a linear least-squares problem (Householder QR from framework-math).

On the thickness of the NACA 0012 the error falls with every degree (degree 1: 3.6⋅10−43.6\cdot 10^{-4}, degree 9: 1.8⋅10−51.8\cdot 10^{-5} of the chord, root mean square); the leading-edge radius approaches that of the NACA formula from below, but slowly, because the fit does not weight the nose specially.

PARSEC after Sobieczky

Per side z=∑k=16akxk−1/2z = \sum_{k=1}^{6} a_k x^{k-1/2}; the six coefficients follow from six linear conditions on parameters with an intuitive meaning:

a1=±2rLE,z(1)=zTE±ΔzTE2,z'(1)=−tan⁡(α±β2),z(X)=Z,z'(X)=0,z''(X)=Zxx.a_1 = \pm\sqrt{2r_{LE}}, \quad z(1) = z_{TE} \pm \tfrac{\Delta z_{TE}}{2}, \quad z'(1) = -\tan\big(\alpha \pm \tfrac{\beta}{2}\big), \quad z(X) = Z, \quad z'(X) = 0, \quad z''(X) = Z_{xx}.

The plus sign holds on the upper side, the minus sign on the lower; α\alpha is the direction of the trailing edge, β\beta its wedge angle, X,Z,ZxxX, Z, Z_{xx} position, height and curvature of the thickness crest. Eleven parameters in all. Conversely kennwerte recovers them from the coefficients (crest as zero of z'z').

Double circular arc

In the transonic compressor both sides are circular arcs through leading and trailing edge. The camber line for the camber angle θ\theta has the sagitta f=tan⁡(θ/4)/2f = \tan(\theta/4)/2 (a circular arc meets its chord at half the opening angle), the sides have f±t/2f \pm t/2. The area is the difference of two circular segments,

A=Ru2(φu−sin⁡φucos⁡φu)−Rl2(φl−sin⁡φlcos⁡φl),R=1/4+f22|f|,sin⁡φ=12R,A = R_u^2(\varphi_u - \sin\varphi_u\cos\varphi_u) - R_l^2(\varphi_l - \sin\varphi_l\cos\varphi_l), \qquad R = \frac{1/4 + f^2}{2|f|}, \quad \sin\varphi = \frac{1}{2R},

the wedge angle at the edges 2arctan⁡2fu−2arctan⁡2fl2\arctan 2f_u - 2\arctan 2f_l (biconvex 4arctan⁡t4\arctan t).

Deformation

Hicks and Henne. Bumps are added to a given side,

b(x)=sinw⁡(πxln⁡0.5/ln⁡h),b(x) = \sin^w\!\big(\pi x^{\ln 0.5/\ln h}\big),

with crest b(h)=1b(h) = 1 (because hln⁡0.5/ln⁡h=1/2h^{\ln 0.5/\ln h} = 1/2) and b(0)=b(1)=0b(0) = b(1) = 0; ww sets the width. The amplitudes are the design variables.

Free-form deformation after Sederberg and Parry: a rectangle with (l+1)(m+1)(l+1)(m+1) control points QijQ_{ij} encloses the airfoil; each point with local coordinates (u,v)(u, v) is mapped to

P(u,v)=∑i=0l∑j=0mBil(u)Bjm(v)Qij.P(u, v) = \sum_{i=0}^{l}\sum_{j=0}^{m} B_i^l(u)\,B_j^m(v)\,Q_{ij} .

The Bernstein polynomials form a partition of unity and reproduce linear functions exactly; the undisplaced uniform lattice therefore maps identically, and an affine map of all control points (a rotation, say) maps the airfoil exactly the same way. A single displaced point moves every airfoil point by its weight Bil(u)Bjm(v)B_i^l(u)B_j^m(v).

How it is checked

  • NACA thickness in closed form: yt(0.3)=0.50014ty_t(0.3) = 0.50014\,t, leading-edge radius 1.1019t21.1019\,t^2, crest at 0.30, trailing edge open and closed.
  • NACA 2412: αL0=−2.077°\alpha_{L0} = -2.077° (thin-airfoil theory) to 5⋅10−45\cdot 10^{-4} degrees, upper and lower side symmetric about the camber line.
  • Five-digit series against the table (see above); 23012 with the table values: πA1=0.30\pi A_1 = 0.30, crest at 0.15.
  • CST: error falls with the degree; a CST airfoil is recovered exactly with the same degree (1e-12).
  • PARSEC: all eleven parameters recovered (1e-10); symmetric parameters give mirrored sides.
  • Double circular arc: area against the polygon of the contour (order 2), biconvex against 23t\tfrac23 t.
  • Hicks-Henne: crest exactly at hh; free-form deformation: identity, rotation and single weight exact.

Limits

  • Five-digit series only without reflex (third digit 0); no six-series and no modified thickness distributions.
  • CST with the airfoil exponents (0.5 and 1); the fit weights all points equally.
  • Double circular arc with sharp edges (no edge radii).
  • Deformation two-dimensional; lattice with uniform initial spacing.
