From design to flow calculation – background

How a designed blade row enters the flow solver as a body force, and how the computed field turns back into the figures of the design: metal angles and zone from the double circular arc, deviation after Carter, the loss coefficient as a friction coefficient, evaluation at two planes and the profile as a mesh contour – with derivation, measured values from the probe and limits.

What it is about

The design (plane blade cascades, profile shapes) describes a blade row by a few numbers: metal angles, chord, pitch, camber. The flow solver can compute a row as a body force after Hall without meshing the blades – it needs metal angles, zone, pitch and a friction coefficient. The bridge translates in both directions: design → source (Gitterreihe), result → design figures (Gitterauswertung), and the profile as a contour for the mesh generator (Profilbruecke).

The bridge is a module of its own that depends on both sides. Neither the design nor the solver gains a dependency it does not need; nothing depends on the bridge.

The row as a body force

Angles in radians against the axis, positive in the pitch direction – as in the cascade and in the body force. The camber line of the double circular arc is a circular arc; with stagger angle λ\lambda and camber angle θ\theta the metal angles are

κ1=λ+θ2,κ2=λ−θ2,σ=cs,b=ccos⁡λ.\kappa_1 = \lambda + \frac{\theta}{2}, \qquad \kappa_2 = \lambda - \frac{\theta}{2}, \qquad \sigma = \frac{c}{s}, \qquad b = c\cos\lambda .

The zone of the body force extends along the axis from the leading edge x0x_0 to x0+bx_0 + b – over the axial chord, not the chord. In the body force the metal angle runs linearly from κ1\kappa_1 to κ2\kappa_2; the normal force turns the flow onto the camber surface, with K=2πK = 2\pi as default.

Deviation after Carter. The body force turns the flow to the metal angle it is given. A real cascade falls short by the deviation δ=mθ/σ\delta = m\,\theta/\sqrt{\sigma} (Carter, for the circular arc m=0.23+κ2/500m = 0.23 + \kappa_2/500, κ2\kappa_2 in degrees). On request the row therefore uses the outlet angle of the correlation instead of the metal angle at the trailing edge:

κ2C=κ2+sgn⁡(θ)δ.\kappa_2^{C} = \kappa_2 + \operatorname{sgn}(\theta)\,\delta .

The sign follows the camber: the outlet angle moves back towards the inlet, the turning becomes smaller.

Sections of a meshed blade. If the blade is given as a MERIDL file, the evaluation of the flow solver supplies metal angles, chord, σ\sigma and position of maximum camber per section. The bridge turns them into a row with pitch s=c/σs = c/\sigma and gives span and δ\delta after Carter in degrees per section – for comparison with the deviation back-calculated from computation and measurement.

From loss coefficient to friction coefficient

The design knows the loss as a coefficient ζ=Δp0/(12ρW12)\zeta = \Delta p_0 / (\tfrac12 \rho W_1^2), the body force as a friction force per mass fp=−cf|W|2/(s|cos⁡κ|)e^W\mathbf{f}_p = -c_f |\mathbf{W}|^2/(s\,|\cos\kappa|)\,\hat{\mathbf{e}}_W. Along a stream tube with path dl=dx/cos⁡κdl = dx/\cos\kappa the incompressible flow loses stagnation pressure

dp0dl=−ρfp=−ρcfW2scos⁡κ⟹dp0dx=−ρcfW2scos2⁡κ.\frac{dp_0}{dl} = -\rho f_p = -\frac{\rho\,c_f\,W^2}{s\cos\kappa} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dp_0}{dx} = -\frac{\rho\,c_f\,W^2}{s\cos^2\kappa}.

With WW fixed and κ\kappa linear over the zone the integral is closed, ∫0bdx/cos2⁡κ=b(tan⁡κ2−tan⁡κ1)/(κ2−κ1)\int_0^b dx/\cos^2\kappa = b\,(\tan\kappa_2 - \tan\kappa_1)/(\kappa_2 - \kappa_1), and

ζ=2cfs∫0bdxcos2⁡κ⟹cf=ζs(κ2−κ1)2b(tan⁡κ2−tan⁡κ1),without turning cf=ζscos2⁡κ2b.\zeta = \frac{2\,c_f}{s}\int_0^b \frac{dx}{\cos^2\kappa} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad c_f = \frac{\zeta\,s\,(\kappa_2 - \kappa_1)}{2\,b\,(\tan\kappa_2 - \tan\kappa_1)}, \qquad \text{without turning } c_f = \frac{\zeta\,s\cos^2\kappa}{2\,b}.

The factor 2 comes from the reference 12ρW2\tfrac12\rho W^2. With Carter's deviation the shifted angle κ2C\kappa_2^C is inserted, the one actually used. If no coefficient is given, the row takes the profile loss after Soderberg from the turning of the metal angles.

The same row in the case file. schalter returns the lines -koerperkraft:FROM,TO,K1,K2,K,CF (angles in degrees) and -schaufelteilung:S, locale-independent and with seventeen digits – from them the solver builds the same source that koerperkraft returns directly.

Figures from the calculation

Two planes across the axis, one ahead of and one behind the row. For each plane the cell row whose centres lie closest to the plane counts (all cells at the smallest distance, equal to 10−910^{-9} of the mesh length). Averaging is weighted with the mass flow ρWaV\rho W_a V:

Wa=∑ρWaVWa∑ρWaV,Wt=∑ρWaVWt∑ρWaV,p0=∑ρWaVp0∑ρWaV,p=∑pV∑V,\bar W_a = \frac{\sum \rho W_a V\, W_a}{\sum \rho W_a V}, \quad \bar W_t = \frac{\sum \rho W_a V\, W_t}{\sum \rho W_a V}, \quad \bar p_0 = \frac{\sum \rho W_a V\, p_0}{\sum \rho W_a V}, \quad \bar p = \frac{\sum p V}{\sum V},

with the stagnation pressure of the cell's ideal gas, p0=p(1+γ−12M2)γ/(γ−1)p_0 = p\,(1 + \tfrac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2)^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)}. From this β=arctan⁡(Wt/Wa)\beta = \arctan(\bar W_t/\bar W_a) and W=Wa2+Wt2W = \sqrt{\bar W_a^2 + \bar W_t^2}.

The design figures: turning β1−β2\beta_1 - \beta_2, loss ζ=(p01−p02)/(p01−p1)\zeta = (p_{01} - p_{02})/(p_{01} - p_1), Lieblein diffusion factor and de Haller number from the computed angles with the ratio Wa2/Wa1W_{a2}/W_{a1}, and the deviation sgn⁡(θ)(β2−κ2)\operatorname{sgn}(\theta)(\beta_2 - \kappa_2) against the metal angle – comparable with Carter's value.

The profile as a mesh contour

A design contour runs from the trailing edge over the lower side to the nose and back over the upper side. The mesh generator expects both sides separately, each from the nose to the trailing edge. Profilbruecke splits at the node with the smallest xx and reverses the lower side; the contour then goes into the unstructured profile mesh or the C mesh of the profile.

Without turning the mapping ζ→cf\zeta \to c_f is exact; the 1.9 % in [TC-03] are the compressible share and the dissipation of the scheme. With turning WW falls along the row while the formula keeps it fixed – [TC-04] therefore only asks for the order of magnitude (0.0394 against 0.0454). Nine counter-probes spoil sign, integral, factor, angle of the case file, zone, stagger, the averaging of the stagnation pressure, the choice of cell row and the lower side of the profile; each one turns the probe red.

Limits

  • The plane cascade is complete. In an annulus the row with a ring zone gives the same angles and the friction coefficient of the design pitch – there the pitch grows with the radius, the friction coefficient does not.
  • The mapping ζ→cf\zeta \to c_f keeps WW fixed along the row: exact without turning and incompressible, with turning only the order of magnitude.
  • No program coupling of the meridional through-flow to the solver and no command line of its own; the lines from schalter go into any case file.
