Surge and rotating stall – background

The equations behind Greitzer and MooreGreitzer: the compression system of duct, plenum and throttle as an oscillator, the stability limit in the parameter BB, the surge limit cycle and the three-state model with the amplitude of rotating stall.

What it is about

At the left edge of the map the compressor becomes unstable. Two phenomena must be told apart: surge, an axial oscillation of the whole system in which the flow periodically collapses or reverses, and rotating stall, a region of separated flow that travels round at a fraction of the rotor speed and lowers the mean flow. Which one occurs depends not only on the compressor but on the system behind it: a large plenum stores enough mass to reverse the duct flow. Greitzer (1976) captured this in one parameter.

The two-state model

With the Helmholtz frequency and the Greitzer parameter

ωH=aAcVpLc,B=U2ωHLc\omega_H = a\sqrt{\frac{A_c}{V_p L_c}}, \qquad B = \frac{U}{2\,\omega_H L_c}

and time τ=ωHt\tau = \omega_H t, the momentum balance in the duct and the mass balance in the plenum read

dΦdτ=B(Ψc(Φ)−Ψ),dΨdτ=1B(Φ−ΦT(Ψ)),\frac{d\Phi}{d\tau} = B\,\big(\Psi_c(\Phi) - \Psi\big), \qquad \frac{d\Psi}{d\tau} = \frac{1}{B}\,\big(\Phi - \Phi_T(\Psi)\big),

with flow coefficient Φ\Phi, pressure coefficient Ψ\Psi and throttle ΦT=γsign⁡(Ψ)|Ψ|\Phi_T = \gamma\,\mathrm{sign}(\Psi)\sqrt{|\Psi|}. Example from the probe: a=340a = 340 m/s, Ac=0.01A_c = 0.01 m², Vp=0.5V_p = 0.5 m³, Lc=1L_c = 1 m give ωH=3400.02=48.083\omega_H = 340\sqrt{0.02} = 48.083 1/s.

The characteristic is the cubic one of Moore and Greitzer,

Ψc(Φ)=ψ0+H(1+32x−12x3),x=ΦW−1,\Psi_c(\Phi) = \psi_0 + H\Big(1 + \tfrac32 x - \tfrac12 x^3\Big), \qquad x = \frac{\Phi}{W} - 1,

with its peak at Φ=2W\Phi = 2W; to the left of it, it rises.

The stability limit

At equilibrium (Ψc(Φ)=Ψ\Psi_c(\Phi) = \Psi, Φ=ΦT(Ψ)\Phi = \Phi_T(\Psi)) the Jacobian with slopes Ψc'\Psi_c' and ΦT'\Phi_T' is

A=(BΨc'−B1/B−ΦT'/B),det⁡A=1−Ψc'ΦT',tr⁡A=BΨc'−ΦT'/B.A = \begin{pmatrix} B\,\Psi_c' & -B \\ 1/B & -\Phi_T'/B \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \det A = 1 - \Psi_c'\Phi_T', \qquad \mathrm{tr}\,A = B\,\Psi_c' - \Phi_T'/B .

The point is statically stable for det⁡A>0\det A > 0, i.e. Ψc'ΦT'<1\Psi_c'\Phi_T' < 1 (the throttle cuts the characteristic more steeply). It is dynamically stable for tr⁡A<0\mathrm{tr}\,A < 0. On the rising branch this means

B<Bcrit=ΦT'Ψc'.B < B_\text{crit} = \sqrt{\frac{\Phi_T'}{\Psi_c'}} .

There a complex pair crosses the imaginary axis (Hopf bifurcation) with λ=±idet⁡A\lambda = \pm i\sqrt{\det A}: the frequency of the emerging oscillation is ω/ωH=1−Ψc'ΦT'\omega/\omega_H = \sqrt{1 - \Psi_c'\Phi_T'}. Small BB (small plenum, slow rotor) damps; large BB lets even a weakly rising characteristic surge. In the probe the equilibrium lies at Φ=0.42\Phi = 0.42, Bcrit=0.75453B_\text{crit} = 0.75453; the real part of the eigenvalue changes sign between 0.99Bcrit0.99\,B_\text{crit} and 1.01Bcrit1.01\,B_\text{crit}.

Limit cycle. Just above the limit the disturbance grows until the curvature of the characteristic bounds it. The class integrates with Dormand-Prince, lets the motion settle and measures the frequency from upward crossings of Φ\Phi through a mark (events from framework-math). At 1.01Bcrit1.01\,B_\text{crit} the probe measures 0.895ωH0.895\,\omega_H against the Hopf value 0.8990.899 – the difference is that of the finite amplitude.

Moore-Greitzer: surge and rotating stall

Moore and Greitzer (1986) add the amplitude of the first circumferential harmonic of the disturbance; JJ is its square. In the form of Krstić and others, with ξ\xi as time in units of R/UR/U:

dΦdξ=1lc(−Ψ+ψ0+H(1+32x(1−J2)−12x3)),\frac{d\Phi}{d\xi} = \frac{1}{l_c}\Big(-\Psi + \psi_0 + H\big(1 + \tfrac32 x\,(1 - \tfrac J2) - \tfrac12 x^3\big)\Big),

dΨdξ=Φ−γΨ4B2lc,dJdξ=J(1−x2−J4)3aH(1+ma)W.\frac{d\Psi}{d\xi} = \frac{\Phi - \gamma\sqrt{\Psi}}{4B^2 l_c}, \qquad \frac{dJ}{d\xi} = J\Big(1 - x^2 - \frac J4\Big)\frac{3aH}{(1 + ma)W}.

For J=0J = 0 the two-state model remains, with BG=2BB_G = 2B and τ=ξ/(2Blc)\tau = \xi/(2Bl_c) – the probe compares both histories to 3e-12. JJ grows where 1−x2>01 - x^2 > 0, i.e. on the part of the characteristic between Φ=0\Phi = 0 and the peak. The equilibrium with stall has

J=4(1−x2),Ψ=ψ0+H(1−32x+52x3),J = 4(1 - x^2), \qquad \Psi = \psi_0 + H\Big(1 - \tfrac32 x + \tfrac52 x^3\Big),

the characteristic with fully developed stall; its intersection with the throttle is found with Brent.

Events. rechne reports two events: stall when JJ crosses a threshold upwards, and reverse flow when Φ\Phi falls through zero – the mark of deep surge. At B=2B = 2 reverse flow occurs in the probe at ξ=19.34\xi = 19.34.

Pseudocode – stability of an operating point

(Φ*, Ψ*) ← intersection of characteristic and throttle     Brent
s ← Ψ_c'(Φ*);  d ← Φ_T'(Ψ*)
if s · d ≥ 1:  statically unstable
if s ≤ 0:  B_crit ← ∞                    falling characteristic
else B_crit ← √(d / s);  ω/ω_H ← √(1 − s d)
if B > B_crit:  integrate the limit cycle, frequency from crossings

Source: envisionz-framework-turbo/src/envisionz/turbo/stabilitaet/Greitzer.java.

How it is checked

Stabilitaetsprobe (7 checks): equilibrium, BcritB_\text{crit} against the closed formula and sign change of the real part around the limit, Jacobian against differences; limit cycle just above the limit against the Hopf frequency; Helmholtz frequency and BB by hand; Moore-Greitzer with J=0J = 0 equal to Greitzer with BG=2BB_G = 2B; equilibrium with stall (residual zero, derivatives 1e-17) and convergence from small JJ; deep surge with reverse flow; error cases. Counter-checks (1/B1/B replaced by BB in the plenum equation, reciprocal under the root of the Helmholtz frequency, J/4J/4 instead of J/2J/2 in the characteristic, J/2J/2 instead of J/4J/4 in the amplitude equation) are detected.

Limits

  • Incompressible in the duct, one lumped plenum; higher-order duct acoustics are missing.
  • Only the first circumferential harmonic of the stall; no time lag of the characteristic.
  • Cubic characteristic; measured characteristics in the unstable branch are rarely available and are not read in.
