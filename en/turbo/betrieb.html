Gas turbine in operation – background

The equations behind Teillast, Instationaer and Gaspfad: operating points away from the design point with a compressor map and the turbine swallowing law, accelerating and settling with shaft, volume and heat storage, and back-calculating component damage from measurements.

What it is about

The design (cycle) fixes one point: pressure ratio, turbine inlet temperature, mass flow. In operation the load, the ambient conditions and the state of the components change; the machine finds a new point where compressor and turbine pass the same mass flow and the shaft is in balance. Three questions follow: which point is reached (part load), how the machine gets there (transient), and what the measurements tell about the state of the components (gas path analysis). All three compute on the same component equations.

Part load

Corrected quantities. So that the map holds independently of inlet temperature and pressure, it works in corrected speed and corrected flow

n=N/NAT1/T1A,μ=m˙T1/T1A(p1/p1A)m˙A.n = \frac{N/N_A}{\sqrt{T_1/T_{1A}}}, \qquad \mu = \frac{\dot m\,\sqrt{T_1/T_{1A}}}{(p_1/p_{1A})\,\dot m_A}.

On a hot day the corrected speed therefore drops at the same mechanical speed: at 35 °C instead of 15 °C, n=288.15/308.15=0.96700n = \sqrt{288.15/308.15} = 0.96700 – the probe meets this to ten digits.

Map. The class uses an analytic map that gives exactly πA\pi_A and ηA\eta_A at the design point (n,μ)=(1,1)(n, \mu) = (1, 1):

π=1+(πA−1)n2(1+a(1−(μ/n)2)),η=ηA(1−b(μ/n−1)2−c(n−1)2).\pi = 1 + (\pi_A - 1)\,n^2\Big(1 + a\big(1 - (\mu/n)^2\big)\Big), \qquad \eta = \eta_A\Big(1 - b\,(\mu/n - 1)^2 - c\,(n - 1)^2\Big).

The factor n2n^2 is the similarity of the velocity triangles (pressure rise like U2U^2), μ/n\mu/n the incidence. With a>0a > 0 the characteristic falls and the inverse μ(n,π)\mu(n, \pi) is closed-form; above the characteristic it returns NaN.

Turbine. The efficiency stays fixed, the flow follows Stodola's ellipse law:

m˙4T4p4=ΦA1−(p5/p4)21−(p5A/p4A)2,\frac{\dot m_4\sqrt{T_4}}{p_4} = \Phi_A\,\sqrt{\frac{1 - (p_5/p_4)^2}{1 - (p_{5A}/p_{4A})^2}},

with back pressure p5=p1p5A/p1Ap_5 = p_1\,p_{5A}/p_{1A}. The law describes a multistage turbine whose stages are not choked; for large pressure ratios it tends to the constant swallowing capacity.

Equations and unknowns. The unknowns are μ\mu and T4T_4. The first equation is flow compatibility: what leaves the combustor (m˙+m˙B\dot m + \dot m_B) must pass the swallowing law. The second depends on the operating mode:

  • beiLeistung: PT−PV=PuseP_T - P_V = P_\text{use} (shaft balance at given speed, generator on the grid);
  • beiBrennstoff: m˙B\dot m_B given (combustor balance);
  • beiTemperatur: T4T_4 given.

Newton on two unknowns solves it (Jacobian from central differences), starting at the design point, the step limited to 20 % in μ\mu and 15 % in T4T_4 and halved on a step out of the map. At the design point all three modes give exactly the point of Kreisprozess.einwellig.

Component state. Ageing and damage enter as four factors (1 = new): compressor efficiency, compressor flow (the characteristic is stretched in μ\mu), turbine efficiency and swallowing capacity. At the same power a compressor 2 % worse costs 16 K more T4T_4, a turbine 2 % worse 33 K – the turbine counts twice because it also drives the compressor.

Transient operation

Between two operating points storage terms govern the path. The class carries three, written per component (volume method after Fawke and Saravanamuttoo):

JωA2NdNdt=PT−PV−PL(N),dp4dt=RT4V(m˙+m˙B−m˙T),McdTWdt=(hA)(T4−TW).J\omega_A^2\,N\,\frac{dN}{dt} = P_T - P_V - P_L(N), \qquad \frac{dp_4}{dt} = \frac{R\,T_4}{V}\,\big(\dot m + \dot m_B - \dot m_T\big), \qquad M c\,\frac{dT_W}{dt} = (hA)\,(T_4 - T_W).

The shaft stores rotational energy, the volume between compressor and turbine stores mass (isothermal at the gas temperature), the hot parts store heat. The compressor works against p3=p4/(1−Δp/p)p_3 = p_4/(1 - \Delta p/p) and delivers the flow from the inverse characteristic; if p3p_3 lies above the characteristic, the computation reports the surge line. The combustor is adiabatic except for the heat given to the wall:

T4,ad=m˙cpT3+m˙BHuηB(m˙+m˙B)cp,T4=m˙4cpT4,ad+(hA)TWm˙4cp+hA.T_{4,ad} = \frac{\dot m c_p T_3 + \dot m_B H_u \eta_B}{(\dot m + \dot m_B)\,c_p}, \qquad T_4 = \frac{\dot m_4 c_p T_{4,ad} + (hA)\,T_W}{\dot m_4 c_p + hA}.

The state y=(N,p4/p4A,TW/T4A)y = (N,\ p_4/p_{4A},\ T_W/T_{4A}) is integrated with RODAS (framework-math); the system is stiff because the volume is orders of magnitude faster than shaft and wall (eigenvalues in the probe −107 1/s against −0.5 1/s). At the steady end state all derivatives vanish, and the point is the same as Teillast.beiBrennstoff with PL(N)P_L(N) as useful power.

Shaft time constant. Holding volume and wall in equilibrium leaves one first-order equation for NN. Linearised:

τ=JωA2N−ddN(Puse(N)−PL(N)),\tau = \frac{J\omega_A^2 N}{-\dfrac{d}{dN}\big(P_\text{use}(N) - P_L(N)\big)},

and −1/τ-1/\tau is the slow eigenvalue of the full system as long as the fast storages really are fast (probe: −0.5042 against −0.5073 1/s). A negative τ\tau means the shaft runs away.

Gas path analysis

A few quantities zz are measured (compressor delivery pressure and temperature, turbine exit temperature, fuel flow, air mass flow); sought are the deviations δ\delta of the four component factors from the new state (Urban 1972). At the same operating point (speed and useful power fixed) the model of the new engine is computed, the deviation taken relative and linearised:

Δzi=zizi0−1≈∑jHijδj,Hij=∂(zi/zi0)∂δj.\Delta z_i = \frac{z_i}{z_{i0}} - 1 \approx \sum_j H_{ij}\,\delta_j, \qquad H_{ij} = \frac{\partial (z_i/z_{i0})}{\partial \delta_j}.

The influence matrix comes from central differences through Teillast.beiLeistung. With measurement uncertainties σi\sigma_i and W=diag⁡(1/σi2)W = \mathrm{diag}(1/\sigma_i^2) the estimate is weighted least squares

δ^=(HTWH)−1HTWΔz,Cov⁡(δ^)=(HTWH)−1,χ2=(Δz−Hδ^)TW(Δz−Hδ^),\hat\delta = (H^T W H)^{-1} H^T W\,\Delta z, \qquad \mathrm{Cov}(\hat\delta) = (H^T W H)^{-1}, \qquad \chi^2 = (\Delta z - H\hat\delta)^T W (\Delta z - H\hat\delta),

with expectation m−pm - p for the residual sum of squares. Nonlinearly Gauss-Newton improves it, forming HH anew at each δ\delta; this hits imposed damage of a few percent to rounding accuracy, the linear estimate to about 4e-4.

Observability. With fewer than four independent measurements there are combinations of damage that no measurement sees. The class decomposes W1/2HW^{1/2}H into singular values; values below 10−6smax10^{-6} s_\text{max} count as zero, and the corresponding right singular vectors are the invisible combinations. The minimum-norm solution is then reported and the covariance via the pseudoinverse – with the note that it only says something in the observable subspace.

Relation to the filters. Gas path analysis evaluates one instant. Whoever has a sequence of measurements and wants to track slow deterioration can carry δ\delta as the state of a state-space model (EKF, UKF, particle filter); the influence matrix is then the Jacobian of the observation.

Pseudocode – part-load point

n ← corrected speed;  u ← (n · μ_V, T₁/T₁A · T₄A)        start: μ, T₄
repeat until |r| < 1e-13
    state(u): π, η from map; T₃; combustor to T₄; turbine to p₅
    r ← (ṁ₄ / swallowing law − 1,  condition of the mode)
    J ← central differences;  d ← −J⁻¹ r
    λ ← min(1, 0.2 μ/|d_μ|, 0.15 T₄/|d_T|);  halve λ until |r(u + λd)| < |r|
    u ← u + λ d

How it is checked

  • Teillastprobe (7 checks): design point in all three modes equal to Kreisprozess.einwellig; mass, power and combustor balance at four part-load points to 7e-15; a second route (Brent on μ\mu with the components written out) hits the same point; the three modes give the same point; hot day; characteristic and inverse; effect of the four state factors; error cases.
  • Instationaerprobe (6): at the part-load point the derivatives vanish (6e-14); starting from the design state the computation runs into this point (1e-8); shaft time constant against the eigenvalue; a small disturbance decays with the eigenvalue; heat storage against the mixing formula; error cases and surge line.
  • Gaspfadprobe (5): imposed damage recovered; linearisation error of second order (measured order 1.999); 4000 noisy measurements against (HTWH)−1(H^TWH)^{-1} and χ2\chi^2 against m−pm - p; observability with two, three and five measurements; error cases.

Counter-checks (Stodola without the squares, characteristic with nn instead of n2n^2, efficiency without the speed term, shaft balance without the factor NN, volume without fuel flow, wall without hAhA in the denominator, weight 1/σ1/\sigma instead of 1/σ21/\sigma^2, too small a rank threshold, residual sum without weight) are detected.

Limits

  • Single-shaft machine with an analytic map; measured maps (tables with beta lines) and multi-shaft machines are missing.
  • Fixed properties per component as in the design, no cooling air, no power turbine with its own speed.
  • Transient: isothermal volume, one heat storage, no control; surge itself is not computed through (see stability).
  • Gas path: linear calibration at the new state, measurement errors normal and independent, no sensor faults.
