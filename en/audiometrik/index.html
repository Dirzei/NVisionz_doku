envisionz-framework-audiometrik

Measure first, then encode

Function overview for the presentation of the envisionz framework, as of 28 Sep 2026. The measurement chain for audio methods: signals in floating point and as integers, WAV without a third-party library, spectra, windows and the Bark scale, time alignment and six error measures up to the noise-to-mask ratio, plus the command line for measurement series. For each method: purpose, API, use in the framework, applications, examples. The examples are shortened. Many API names are German (e.g. Messstrecke = measurement chain, Ausrichtung = alignment, Masse = measures); they are shown unchanged.

6error measures, from “bit-exact” to NMR
1dependency: framework-math (FFT); no third-party library
8192samples largest searched offset (default)

Principles of the module

Measurement comes before the codec. Before an own method is built, it must be measurable whether a change is better or worse – otherwise one hears a difference and does not know whether it is one.
Align first, then measure. Every codec brings its own delay. The offset is determined by cross-correlation and compensated; the padded edges are not measured.
Coarse, and saying so. The psychoacoustic model is deliberately simple (Schroeder spreading, fixed offset). The NMR is good for comparing two methods, not as a statement about audibility.
Own framework module. The computation does not live in the command-line tool, because the distortion measure will later enter the encoder's objective function.
One FFT in the workspace. Power spectrum and cross-correlation use envisionz.math.Fft; the former own copy is gone.

Signals envisionz.audiometrik

Audiosignal and Ganzsignal – floating point for the measures, integers for the lossless path

Audiosignal holds channels as double in the range −1…+1; all measurements work on it. Ganzsignal is the same thing as integers, with word length – the form in which a lossless encoder sees its input and in which “bit-exact” is to be taken literally. Background, formulas and pseudocode →

Spectra, windows and hearing model envisionz.audiometrik

Spektren, Fenster, Barkskala

Spektren: power spectrum and cross-correlation via the FFT from framework-math. Fenster: Hann, Hamming, sine and Kaiser-Bessel-derived (with an own Bessel function I0). Barkskala: Bark band partition, band energies and a masking threshold with Schroeder spreading and a fixed offset – without tonality estimation and without the threshold in quiet. Alignment envisionz.audiometrik

Ausrichtung – compensate offset and level before measuring

Without this step a perfect codec with 500 samples of delay would get a catastrophic SNR. The offset is estimated from the cross-correlation of the mono sums, optionally also the level. Shifting pads one edge with zeros; that is why the alignment reports the valid range. Background, formulas and pseudocode →

Measurement chain and report envisionz.audiometrik

Messstrecke, Messwert, Bericht – everything in one call

The measurement chain aligns, cuts both signals to the valid range (with one frame of safety margin) and delivers, in this order, offset, measured range, level factor if requested, bit-exactness, largest deviation, SNR, segmental SNR, spectral distance and NMR as mean and peak. Defaults: frame 1024, hop 512, offset up to 8192, alignment on, level matching off, 10 dB offset. Bericht appends one line to a CSV and writes the header if needed. Example – reference against decoded version, one line to the CSV

List<Messwert> werte = new Messstrecke().rahmenlaenge(2048).vergleiche(bezug, pruef);
werte.forEach(w -> System.out.println(w.alsZeile()));
long strombytes = Files.size(Path.of("kodiert.bin"));
Bericht.ergaenze(Path.of("rd.csv"), "128kbps", Messstrecke.bitrateKbps(strombytes, bezug.dauerSekunden()), werte);

Command line envisionz-commandline-audiometrik · cmd.audiometrik

Audiometrik – the measurement chain in the house format -name:value

Two tasks: vergleich (comparison, default) and steckbrief (profile). The CSV is appended, not overwritten – a curve is built from several calls. Return codes for scripts: 0 bit-exact, 10 deviating, 1 usage error, 2 switch error, 3 other error. Without arguments the usage line is shown.

Examples

Audiometrik -op:steckbrief -in:datei.wav
Audiometrik -op:vergleich -ref:original.wav -pruef:dekodiert.wav -strom:kodiert.bin -ausgabe:rd.csv -kennung:128kbps
Audiometrik -ref:a.wav -pruef:b.wav -rahmen:2048 -pegel:ja -abzug:6
