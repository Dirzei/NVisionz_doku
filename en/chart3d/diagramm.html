Axes, box and colour scale – background

How measured values get into the chart box: mapping onto the unit cube (linear, logarithmic, reversed), choice of tick marks, equal scales, fitting the view, and the scale that turns values into colours (also in bands). Pseudocode following the source, conventions, checks, limits.

What this is about

The drawing engine (background) knows only triangles in space. A chart, by contrast, has axes with value ranges, tick marks and labels, and its values have arbitrary units. Chart3DView therefore separates the world of measured values from the world of drawing: each axis (Axis3D) maps its value range onto the interval [0,1][0,1]; the geometry is drawn in the unit cube, whose centre (0.5|0.5|0.5)(0.5\,|\,0.5\,|\,0.5) is the camera target. The scale (ColorScale) translates a value into a colour.

Mapping onto the unit cube

For a linear axis with range [a,b][a, b] the mapping is affine, for a logarithmic axis (a,b>0a, b > 0) linear in the base-10 logarithm; reversed mirrors u→1−uu \to 1-u:

ulin=v−ab−a,ulog⁡=log10⁡v−log10⁡alog10⁡b−log10⁡a,ureversed=1−uu_{\text{lin}} = \frac{v - a}{b - a}, \qquad u_{\log} = \frac{\log_{10} v - \log_{10} a}{\log_{10} b - \log_{10} a}, \qquad u_{\text{reversed}} = 1 - u

The inverse fromUnit computes back accordingly (v=a+u(b−a)v = a + u(b-a) or v=10log10⁡a+u(log10⁡b−log10⁡a)v = 10^{\log_{10} a + u(\log_{10} b - \log_{10} a)}). Values ≤0\le 0 on a logarithmic axis are raised to 10−30010^{-300} before taking the logarithm so that no error ever occurs.

setRange(lo, hi):
    if not (hi > lo):                           empty, reversed or NaN
        c ← lo if finite, else 0;   d ← max(1e−9, |c|·1e−3)
        lower ← c − d;  upper ← c + d
    else: lower ← lo;  upper ← hi

toUnit(v):
    if logarithmic:  u ← (log10(max(1e−300, v)) − log10(lower)) / (log10(upper) − log10(lower))
    else:            u ← (v − lower) / (upper − lower)
    return reversed ? 1 − u : u

Source: envisionz-framework-chart3d/src/envisionz/chart3d/chart/Axis3D.java. Values outside the range are not clamped; they lie outside the cube.

Convention in the framework: An empty or reversed range is widened to a small range around the start value (example: range 5…5 becomes 4.995…5.005) so that there is never a division by zero.

Worked example. Range −20…60-20 \dots 60, linear: u(−20)=0u(-20) = 0, u(0)=20/80=0.25u(0) = 20/80 = 0.25, u(20)=0.5u(20) = 0.5, u(60)=1u(60) = 1. Logarithmic 1…10001 \dots 1000: u(10)=1/3u(10) = 1/3, u(100)=2/3u(100) = 2/3, u(101.5)=0.5u(10^{1.5}) = 0.5.

Tick marks

On a linear axis a "round" spacing is sought that gives approximately the desired number of ticks (default 6). From the raw spacing r=(b−a)/nr = (b-a)/n the power of ten m=10⌊log10⁡r⌋m = 10^{\lfloor\log_{10} r\rfloor} and the mantissa q=r/mq = r/m are formed; the step is

Δ=m⋅{1q<1.52q<35q<710otherwise\Delta = m \cdot \begin{cases} 1 & q < 1.5 \\ 2 & q < 3 \\ 5 & q < 7 \\ 10 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}

and the ticks lie at the multiples of Δ\Delta within the range, starting at ⌈a/Δ⌉Δ\lceil a/\Delta\rceil\Delta. Values smaller in magnitude than 10−9Δ10^{-9}\Delta are set to exactly 0 so that no label "-0.00" appears.

Examples. 0…100 \dots 10, n=6n = 6: r=1.67r = 1.67, q=1.67q = 1.67, Δ=2\Delta = 2 → 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. −3.7…8.2-3.7 \dots 8.2: r=1.98r = 1.98, Δ=2\Delta = 2 → −2,0,2,4,6,8-2, 0, 2, 4, 6, 8. 3…473 \dots 47: r=7.33r = 7.33, q≥7q \ge 7, Δ=10\Delta = 10 → 10, 20, 30, 40.

On a logarithmic axis the ticks lie at 10e10^e; if the range spans at most three decades (e1−e0≤3e_1 - e_0 \le 3), 2⋅10e2\cdot10^e and 5⋅10e5\cdot10^e are added. For 1…10001\dots1000 these are 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000. Custom ticks with custom labels can be given with setTicks(values, texts).

Labels. The number of decimals depends on the span: ≥100\ge 100 none, ≥10\ge 10 one, ≥1\ge 1 two, otherwise three. Magnitudes ≥105\ge 10^5 or (non-zero) <10−3< 10^{-3} appear in exponential notation with two decimals; logarithmic decades as 1e3. The decimal separator is always the point (Locale.ROOT).

Source: envisionz-framework-chart3d/src/envisionz/chart3d/chart/Axis3D.java, ticks, logTicks, formatTick. Marks (marke) and ranges (bereich) are attached to the axis as records; they are labelling only and do not affect the range.

Equal scales

Normally each axis fills the full edge length 1 of the cube – a box with 10 units in xx and 2.5 in zz is therefore drawn as a cube. With setEqualAspect each axis is instead stretched by its span relative to the largest and centred about the middle:

fi=max⁡(10−6,simax⁡(sx,sy,sz)),ui'=12+(ui−12)fif_i = \max\!\Bigl(10^{-6},\ \frac{s_i}{\max(s_x, s_y, s_z)}\Bigr), \qquad u_i' = \tfrac12 + \bigl(u_i - \tfrac12\bigr)\,f_i

The positions of walls, grid, ticks and all geometry use the same stretched mapping so that they fit together.

Fitting the view

fitToView(width, height) chooses the camera distance so that the box including its labels just fits into the image. The eight cube corners are projected with the current camera; from the bounding rectangle and the available space (width and height, minus legend, title and a margin for tick numbers and axis titles) a factor follows with which the camera is zoomed. Because zooming under perspective is not exactly proportional, this is repeated up to four times until the factor deviates from 1 by less than 2 %.

fitToView(width, height):
    w ← width − legend width;  h ← height − legend height          at least 50 each
    margin ← tick gap + widest tick label + 1.6·height of the axis title   8 if the box is off
    available ← (w − 2·margin,  h − 2·margin − (title ? 26 : 0))   at least 20 each
    repeat at most 4 times:
        project the 8 corners of the (stretched) cube;  (x0,x1,y0,y1) ← bounding rectangle
        f ← max((x1 − x0)/available.width,  (y1 − y0)/available.height)
        if |f − 1| < 0.02: stop
        camera.zoom(f)                                             ρ ← ρ·f

Source: envisionz-framework-chart3d/src/envisionz/chart3d/chart/Chart3DView.java, fitToView, streckung, gestreckt. The default view is θ=−55∘\theta = -55^\circ, φ=65∘\varphi = 65^\circ, ρ=3.6\rho = 3.6 (with room for the labels).

The colour scale

A ColorScale is a function value → colour with a lower and an upper limit. ColorScale.of(lo, hi, ramp) first maps the value to t=(v−lo)/(hi−lo)t = (v-lo)/(hi-lo), clamps to [0,1][0,1] and calls the ramp. ColorScale.banded(lo, hi, B, ramp) divides into BB equally wide bands and takes the colour of the band centre:

b=min⁡(B−1,⌊tB⌋),colour=ramp(b+12B)b = \min\bigl(B-1,\ \lfloor tB\rfloor\bigr), \qquad \text{colour} = \text{ramp}\!\Bigl(\frac{b + \tfrac12}{B}\Bigr)

With hi≤lohi \le lo, t=0t = 0. The scale carries its number of bands (bands(), 0 for a continuous scale); the renderer needs it to draw isolines at the band boundaries. In the renderer the mapping is translated into a table of 2048 entries so that only a look-up is needed per pixel (see Shading and colour by value).

Worked example. Ten bands over 0…10 \dots 1: a value 0.37 lies in band ⌊3.7⌋=3\lfloor 3.7\rfloor = 3 and gets the colour at t=3.5/10=0.35t = 3.5/10 = 0.35; the value 1.0 lies in band min⁡(9,10)=9\min(9, 10) = 9 and gets t=0.95t = 0.95. A value 1.3 is clamped to 1 and shares its colour with 1.0.

Source: envisionz-framework-chart3d/src/envisionz/chart3d/color/ColorScale.java. The tables Jet, Viridis and others come from framework-chart (ColormapColorScale).

How it is checked

  • Chart3dprobe, section axis scaling: u(minimum)=0u(\text{minimum}) = 0, u(maximum)=1u(\text{maximum}) = 1, u(middle)=12u(\text{middle}) = \tfrac12, quarter point for −20…60-20\dots60 and v=0v = 0 (tolerance 10−910^{-9}); round trip to 10−910^{-9} and 10−1210^{-12} respectively; reversed axis; logarithmic 1…10001\dots1000 with u(10)=13u(10) = \tfrac13, u(100)=23u(100) = \tfrac23; empty and reversed ranges are widened.
  • Chart3dprobe, ticks: within the range, strictly increasing, equal spacing, a round range hits both limits, more requested ticks give more ticks; on a logarithmic axis the decades 1, 10, 100, 1000 are present.
  • chart.Uebernahmeprobe: fitToView, equal scales and the legend positions in the image; anchors for axis limits at 0 and 1 and anchor colours at the anchor points in the Chart3dprobe.

Limits

  • A logarithmic axis needs positive limits; a range with a≤0a \le 0 is handled via the value 10−30010^{-300} and gives no meaningful display.
  • Values outside the range are not clipped; the drawing then lies outside the box.
  • The tick choice knows only the steps 1, 2, 5 and 10 per power of ten; the actual number of ticks therefore differs from the requested one.
  • A span of zero is widened to ±0.1%\pm 0.1\,\% of the value (to ±10−9\pm 10^{-9} for the value 0); differences drawn within it are rounding noise.
  • fitToView fits the box, not the data; geometry outside the cube may protrude.
