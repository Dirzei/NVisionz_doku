envisionz-framework-chart3d

3D charts with their own depth buffer – without OpenGL, without Orson Charts

Function overview for the presentation of the envisionz framework, as of 28 Sep 2026. Own camera, own matrices, own rasteriser with depth buffer; on top of it axes, legends and six chart types, styles and a control rail of settings cards for Swing. For each method: purpose, API, use in the framework, applications, examples. The examples are shortened. Many API names are German (e.g. Einstellkarte = settings card, Diagrammtafel = chart board, Stil = style, Gitterquelle = grid source); they are shown unchanged.

Principles of the module

Everything in-house. Own Framebuffer, own Mat4, own Camera. Depends only on framework-swing (resource bundle) and framework-math (Catmull-Rom spline).
Depth per pixel instead of sorting. The rasteriser decides with the depth buffer; intersecting surfaces need no special treatment. None of Orson's four depth-sort keys rendered intersecting cutting planes correctly.
Colour from the value, not from the triangle. Corner values are coloured per pixel through the colour scale; band boundaries follow the value field instead of the tessellation, and isolines can never deviate from the colour change.
Text via Java2D, position from the same projection. Labels stay parallel to the screen and still sit at the right place in space.
Data coordinates stay data coordinates. The axes map into the unit cube; the camera does not know whether a quantity is in pascal or bar. The vertical axis is z.
Probes with closed-form targets. Determinant and orthogonality of the view matrix, frustum edge on image edge, colours at the support points – nothing is compared with an earlier run. Pure painting is left to inspection and the proof images.

The rendering engine envisionz.chart3d · .render · .scene

ZBufferRenderer, Framebuffer, Scene3D, Camera, Mat4, Vec3

Per image: transform vertices, clip at the near plane, sort into tiles, fill tiles in parallel. Anti-aliasing by supersampling (1 to 4). Gouraud shading via vertex normals with crease angle, depth cueing, edge emphasis, outlines, isolines at band boundaries, a clipping plane and hit testing per pixel. The camera orbits a target, in perspective or orthographic projection. Background, formulas and pseudocode →

What for

Own 3D scenes outside the chart form; native-image friendly because it is only Java2D and arithmetic.

Example – rasterise a scene directly

Scene3D s = new Scene3D();
s.addTriangles(tris, eckwerte);                     // 9 coordinates or 3 values per row
ZBufferRenderer r = new ZBufferRenderer();
r.setGouraud(true);
r.setValueScale(ColorScale.of(0, 1, t -> Color.getHSBColor((float) (0.7 * (1 - t)), 1f, 1f)));
Camera cam = new Camera();
cam.setOrbit(Math.toRadians(-122), Math.toRadians(70.6), 3.0);
BufferedImage bild = r.render(s, cam, 800, 600);

Factories and data sources envisionz.chart3d.chart · envisionz.chart3d.data

Surface3DFactory, Scatter3DFactory, Line3DFactory, Volume3DFactory, Category3DFactory, Pie3DFactory

Six chart types, each with a path via arrays and one via a data source – one drawing path, not two. The surface passes values per corner, scatter points are coloured per point, lines per segment from the value at its middle; bars stand on a reversed category axis so that the largest series is at the back; the pie carries percentages in the legend. Volume takes shells with colour and opacity.

Efficiency surfaces, measurement clouds, paths in space, iso-surfaces from point clouds, bar and pie charts in 3D.

Example – surface from a grid

ColorScale skala = ColorScale.banded(zMin, zMax, 10, t -> Color.getHSBColor((float) (0.7 * (1 - t)), 1f, 1f));
Chart3DView v = Surface3DFactory.create(xv, yv, z, nI, nJ, "Wirkungsgrad", "W2", "PR", "η", skala, false);
Axis convention: Orson put y upwards, this engine z. When converting old calls this must be checked per factory, otherwise the view tips over.

Background, text and line colours, fonts, walls, grid, text orientation, legend position, shading, supersampling, depth cueing and edges, series colours – as an immutable set with mit… (“with …”) derivations. Four presets, including one that looks like Orson Charts.

Controls in Swing envisionz.chart3d.swing

Chart3DPanel, Diagrammtafel, Einstellrail and the settings cards

The panel rotates, zooms and pans with the mouse, renders coarser while dragging and fine at rest, reports hits and shows short help. The chart board arranges several charts in columns, with a coupled camera and one style for all. Settings sit as cards in a vertical rail: view, axes, legend, title, markers, theme; Farbwahl lets the application plug in its own colour chooser.

Proof images and tools envisionz.chart3d.proof · cmd.chart3d

ProofScenes, Chart3DProof, Chart3DTypes, Chart3DViews, Chart3DPruefung, Chart3DDemo, Kartenmass

Proof scenes with a known answer (triangle, cube, crossing planes, surface, path in a box, volume) and tools that write proof images from them into docs/: the categorical and point-based chart types, contact sheets with a full orbit and increasing zoom, a visual check of the painting and a demo. Kartenmass measures the width of the settings cards. Proof images need a human to look at them; they do not replace a probe.
