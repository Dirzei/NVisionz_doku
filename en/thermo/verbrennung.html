Combustion – combustion calculation and fuels

The computation behind Combustion (port of VERB2) and JetFuel: humid air, element balances and air ratio, base solution without dissociation, equilibrium of the 17 species by Newton in the logarithm, adiabatic flame temperature, NASA-7 polynomials of the NJFCP fuels with an exact bridge to the 0 K reference level. Pseudocode following the source, examples, checks, limits.

What this is about

Downstream of an engine's combustor the cycle calculation needs the composition of the exhaust gas, its molar mass and gas constant, and the temperature reached by adiabatic combustion. At low temperature stoichiometry suffices: C becomes CO2, H becomes H2O, oxygen is left over. Above about 1800 K the products dissociate noticeably: CO, H2, OH, H, O, NO appear, and this dissociation consumes energy that the flame temperature lacks.

Combustion is the Java port of the ACM model VERB2 (2016). It works with a fixed set of 17 species and the equilibrium constants of the 12 reactions linking them; the constants come from the partition functions (background partition functions) instead of the polynomial fits of the original. For general gas mixtures and arbitrary species sets there is the Gordon-McBride method (equilibrium – background); both are checked against each other.

Humid air

psiluf(T, p, φ) returns the mole fractions of the humid combustion air. The saturation vapour pressure comes from an Antoine equation (temperature in °C, pressure in mmHg):

log10⁡ps=8.07131−1730.63233.426+θ,ps[Pa⁡]=ps[mmHg⁡]⋅101325760,T≥255K\log_{10} p_{s} = 8.07131 - \frac{1730.63}{233.426 + \theta}, \qquad p_s\ [\mathrm{Pa}] = p_s\ [\mathrm{mmHg}]\cdot\frac{101\,325}{760}, \qquad T \ge 255\ \mathrm K

(below that ps=0p_s = 0). Then xH2O=φps/px_{\mathrm{H_2O}} = \varphi\,p_s/p, and dry air with O2 0.20946, CO2 0.00033, Ar 0.00928 (rest N2) is scaled by 1−xH2O1 - x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}. Also returned are the molar mass MLM_L and the gas constant RL=8.314459848/MLR_L = 8.314459848/M_L in kJ/(kg K)·10³.

Convention in the framework: Combustion keeps this Antoine equation so that the numbers of the port stay unchanged. For psychrometrics on an IAPWS basis – saturation over water after IF97, over ice below the triple point, plus humidity ratio, dew point, enthalpy and specific volume – there is the separate building block FeuchteLuft in the package envisionz.thermo.feuchte (overview).

Element balances, air ratio and base solution

The fuel is given by its elemental analysis (mass fractions C, H, S, O, N, water, ash – cma … ama in the code, normalised to sum 1 as gC,gH,…g_C, g_H, \dots). The reference quantity is one mole of humid air. The oxygen demand per gram of fuel and the supply per mole of air are

zZ=gC12.01115+gH2⋅2.0159+gS32.064−gO31.9988,zN=xO2z_Z = \frac{g_C}{12.01115} + \frac{g_H}{2\cdot 2.0159} + \frac{g_S}{32.064} - \frac{g_O}{31.9988}, \qquad z_N = x_{\mathrm{O_2}}

giving the minimum air requirement Lmin⁡=zZML/zNL_{\min} = z_Z M_L/z_N (kg air per kg fuel) and the stoichiometric fuel-air ratio βst=1/Lmin⁡\beta_{st} = 1/L_{\min}. An input β>0.3\beta > 0.3 is interpreted, as in the original, as air ratio λ\lambda (β=βst/λ\beta = \beta_{st}/\lambda), otherwise as fuel-air ratio (λ=βst/β\lambda = \beta_{st}/\beta). With zw=βMLz_w = \beta M_L (grams of fuel per mole of air) the element amounts in mol per mol of air follow:

dC=zwgC12.01115+xCO2,dH=2zw(gH2.0159+gW18.0153)+2xH2Od_C = \frac{z_w g_C}{12.01115} + x_{\mathrm{CO_2}}, \qquad d_H = 2 z_w\left(\frac{g_H}{2.0159} + \frac{g_W}{18.0153}\right) + 2x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}

dO=2xO2+xH2O+2xCO2+zw(2gO31.9988+gW18.0153),dN=2xN2+2zwgN28.0134,dS=zwgS32.064,dAr=xArd_O = 2x_{\mathrm{O_2}} + x_{\mathrm{H_2O}} + 2x_{\mathrm{CO_2}} + z_w\left(\frac{2 g_O}{31.9988} + \frac{g_W}{18.0153}\right), \quad d_N = 2x_{\mathrm{N_2}} + \frac{2 z_w g_N}{28.0134}, \quad d_S = \frac{z_w g_S}{32.064}, \quad d_{Ar} = x_{Ar}

(the formulas in the code additionally contain all minor species of the air, which are zero here). The stoichiometric oxygen is dO,st=2(dC+dS)+dH/2d_{O,st} = 2(d_C + d_S) + d_H/2; the ratio dO/dO,std_O/d_{O,st} is returned as relox.

Base solution without dissociation. With excess oxygen: nH2O=dH/2n_{\mathrm{H_2O}} = d_H/2, nCO2=dCn_{\mathrm{CO_2}} = d_C, nSO2=dSn_{\mathrm{SO_2}} = d_S, nN2=dN/2n_{\mathrm{N_2}} = d_N/2, nAr=dArn_{Ar} = d_{Ar}, nO2=(dO−dO,st)/2n_{\mathrm{O_2}} = (d_O - d_{O,st})/2. With oxygen deficiency the water-gas equilibrium KW=K1/K2K_W = K_1/K_2 (CO+H2O↔CO2+H2\mathrm{CO + H_2O \leftrightarrow CO_2 + H_2}) distributes the available oxygen z=dO−dC−2dSz = d_O - d_C - 2d_S between CO2 and H2O. With a=nCO2a = n_{\mathrm{CO_2}} and nH2O=z−an_{\mathrm{H_2O}} = z - a, KW(dC−a)(z−a)=a(dH/2−z+a)K_W(d_C - a)(z - a) = a(d_H/2 - z + a) gives the quadratic equation

a2−2z1a+z2=0,z1=z(KW−1)+dCKW+dH/22(KW−1),z2=zdCKWKW−1a^2 - 2 z_1 a + z_2 = 0, \qquad z_1 = \frac{z(K_W - 1) + d_C K_W + d_H/2}{2(K_W - 1)}, \qquad z_2 = \frac{z\, d_C K_W}{K_W - 1}

with the root a=z1∓z12−z2a = z_1 \mp \sqrt{z_1^2 - z_2} (minus for KW>1K_W > 1). H2S follows from K10K_{10} with the pressure factor p/101325p/101\,325 Pa: nH2S/nSO2=K10(p/ps)nH2nCO⁡2/(nCO22n)n_{\mathrm{H_2S}}/n_{\mathrm{SO_2}} = K_{10}\,(p/p_s)\,n_{\mathrm{H_2}} n_{\mathrm{CO}}^2 / (n_{\mathrm{CO_2}}^2 n). This solution is either the result (diss = false) or the start value of the equilibrium.

Sulphur and oxygen together (since 2026-09-29, finding WT24). How much SO2 forms decides how much oxygen is left for CO2 and H2O, and conversely the split via K10K_{10} depends on H2, CO and CO2. The base solution therefore solves both together: a fixed point over nH2Sn_{\mathrm{H_2S}}, each step with z=dO−dC−2nSO2z = d_O - d_C - 2n_{\mathrm{SO_2}} and dH/2−nH2Sd_H/2 - n_{\mathrm{H_2S}} in the water-gas equilibrium, until nH2Sn_{\mathrm{H_2S}} changes by less than 10−13dS10^{-13} d_S (damped after 20 steps). The VERB2 original (subroutine PSIGAB) computes once with an assumed split (all SO2 or all H2S) and splits afterwards; the oxygen of the redistributed sulphur went to no species (−55 % in the O balance at λ = 0.5 and 60 % S). Without sulphur it is a single step and bit-identical with the original.

Very rich with sulphur (z≤0<dO−dCz \le 0 < d_O - d_C): all sulphur is taken as H2S, z=dO−dCz = d_O - d_C, nH2=dH/2−dS−nH2On_{\mathrm{H_2}} = d_H/2 - d_S - n_{\mathrm{H_2O}}. The same shift condition gives the same form with dH/2−dSd_H/2 - d_S instead of dH/2d_H/2 in the numerator of z1z_1 and the same denominator 2(KW−1)2(K_W - 1) – z1z_1 and z2z_2 must share the leading coefficient KW−1K_W - 1. Until 2026-09-28 the code had the denominator 2KW−12K_W - 1 (finding WT13, fixed) – it also stands so in the VERB2 original (PSIGAB, second branch), whose first branch correctly writes 2(KW−1)2(K_W - 1): a missing parenthesis the port had taken over. Without dissociation the solution therefore missed the shift condition: at λ = 0.35, fuel 30 % C, 10 % H, 60 % S, 1500 K, xCO2xH2/(xCO⁡xH2O)x_{\mathrm{CO_2}}x_{\mathrm{H_2}}/(x_{\mathrm{CO}}x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}) was 0.075 times KWK_W, now 1.004 times; at 50 % C, 5 % H, 45 % S, λ = 0.40, H2 became negative. With dissociation only the start value was affected; the equilibrium is the same before and after (to 10⁻¹²).

The equilibrium of the 17 species

The 17 species are N2, O2, CO2, Ar, H2O, SO2, CO, H2, H, OH, O, NO, NO2, SO, N, CH4, H2S. The twelve reactions (rkOf) and their constants KpK_p at the reference pressure p∘=1p^\circ = 1 bar, from FundamentalSpecies.lnK:

No.ReactionNo.Reaction
1CO + ½ O2 → CO27SO + ½ O2 → SO2
2H2 + ½ O2 → H2O8NO + ½ O2 → NO2
3½ H2 + OH → H2O9N → ½ N2
4O → ½ O210H2 + SO2 + 2 CO → H2S + 2 CO2
5H → ½ H211K1/K2K_1/K_2 (water gas)
6NO → ½ N2 + ½ O212CH4 + H2O → CO + 3 H2

Unknowns are the logarithms of the mole fractions of the major species N2, O2, CO2 (with hydrogen also H2O, with sulphur also SO2) and the logarithm of the total amount nn per mole of air. All minor species follow in closed form from the mass-action laws; with a=p∘/pa = \sqrt{p^\circ/p}, for example

xCO⁡=axCO2K1xO2,xH2=axH2OK2xO2,xOH⁡=axH2OK3xH2,xO=axO2K4,xNO⁡=xN2xO2K6x_{\mathrm{CO}} = \frac{a\,x_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}{K_1\sqrt{x_{\mathrm{O_2}}}},\quad x_{\mathrm{H_2}} = \frac{a\,x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}}{K_2\sqrt{x_{\mathrm{O_2}}}},\quad x_{\mathrm{OH}} = \frac{a\,x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}}{K_3\sqrt{x_{\mathrm{H_2}}}},\quad x_{\mathrm O} = \frac{a\sqrt{x_{\mathrm{O_2}}}}{K_4},\quad x_{\mathrm{NO}} = \frac{\sqrt{x_{\mathrm{N_2}}x_{\mathrm{O_2}}}}{K_6}

xH2S=K10xH2xSO2xCO⁡2a2xCO22,xCH4=xCO⁡xH23K12xH2Oa4,xAr=dArnx_{\mathrm{H_2S}} = \frac{K_{10}\,x_{\mathrm{H_2}}x_{\mathrm{SO_2}}x_{\mathrm{CO}}^2}{a^2\,x_{\mathrm{CO_2}}^2}, \qquad x_{\mathrm{CH_4}} = \frac{x_{\mathrm{CO}}\,x_{\mathrm{H_2}}^3}{K_{12}\,x_{\mathrm{H_2O}}\,a^4}, \qquad x_{Ar} = \frac{d_{Ar}}{n}

The exponent of aa is −Δν-\Delta\nu of the reaction in each case; NO has Δν=0\Delta\nu = 0 and no pressure factor. The equations are the relative element balances and the summation condition:

fE=n∑jaEjxj−dEdE(E=N,O,C⁡[,H][,S]),fΣ=∑jxj−1f_E = \frac{n\sum_j a_{Ej}x_j - d_E}{d_E}\quad (E = \mathrm{N, O, C}[, \mathrm{H}][, \mathrm{S}]), \qquad f_\Sigma = \sum_j x_j - 1

Pseudocode – Combustion.equilibrium

a ← √(10⁵ / p)
withH ← d_H > 10⁻¹⁴·(d_C + d_O + d_N);  withS ← d_S > 10⁻¹⁴·(d_C + d_H + d_O + d_N)
u ← (ln x_N2, ln x_O2, ln x_CO2 [, ln x_H2O] [, ln x_SO2], ln n)   from the base solution, with floors
norm ← max |f(u)|
it ← 0
while it < 200 and norm > 10⁻¹²:
    J ← numerical Jacobian, forward difference with h = 10⁻⁷ per column
    Δu ← solve J·Δu = f                       Gauss with column pivoting
    s ← 1;  if max|Δu| > 2: s ← 2/max|Δu|          step limit in the logarithm
    for k ← 1 to 8:                            backtracking
        u' ← u − s·Δu;  norm' ← max |f(u')|
        if norm' < norm or norm' < 10⁻¹²: stop
        s ← s/2
    u ← u';  norm ← norm';  it ← it + 1
if not norm ≤ 10⁻⁶: throw IllegalStateException          also catches NaN
return all 17 xj from u (minor species via K1 … K12)

Source: envisionz-framework-thermo/src/envisionz/thermo/Combustion.java, methods burnImpl, equilibrium, minors, resid, solve. Compared with the ACM original, the mole fractions are consistent including argon (no auxiliary mixture without Ar).

Convention in the framework: the pressure factors refer to p∘=1p^\circ = 1 bar, the reference pressure of FundamentalSpecies. This is a correction of 28.09.2026: previously the normalisation used 101 325 Pa, NO had a pressure factor despite Δν=0\Delta\nu = 0, and H2S and CH4 had wrong exponents (up to 77 % off); demonstrated with the Gleichgewichtsprobe. Gas constant of the result R=8.3143/MR = 8.3143/M kJ/(kg K)·10³. verbOf is synchronized and remembers exactly one result (key: all inputs), so that NFML decks querying several selectors of the same combustion compute only once.

Adiabatic flame temperature

The adiabatic flame temperature satisfies the enthalpy balance per mole of air between air plus fuel and the products in equilibrium at TadT_{ad}:

Hprod⁡(Tad)=Hair⁡(TL)+zwhf,F,Hprod⁡(T)=n∑jxj(T)[ΔfHj∘(0K)+Hj(T)−Hj(0)]H_{\mathrm{prod}}(T_{ad}) = H_{\mathrm{air}}(T_L) + z_w\,h_{f,F}, \qquad H_{\mathrm{prod}}(T) = n\sum_j x_j(T)\,\bigl[\Delta_f H^\circ_j(0\,\mathrm K) + H_j(T) - H_j(0)\bigr]

with the total amount from the argon anchor n=dAr/xArn = d_{Ar}/x_{Ar} and 52RT\tfrac52 RT for argon. The enthalpy of formation of the fuel (in J/g, 0 K reference) is back-calculated from the lower heating value HuH_u:

hf,F=Hu1000+gC12.01115ΔfHCO2∘+(gH2.0159+gW18.0153)ΔfHH2O∘+gS32.064ΔfHSO2∘h_{f,F} = \frac{H_u}{1000} + \frac{g_C}{12.01115}\Delta_f H^\circ_{\mathrm{CO_2}} + \left(\frac{g_H}{2.0159} + \frac{g_W}{18.0153}\right)\Delta_f H^\circ_{\mathrm{H_2O}} + \frac{g_S}{32.064}\Delta_f H^\circ_{\mathrm{SO_2}}

// adiabaticT(p, β, analysis, φ, T_L, Hu, diss)
h_fF ← from Hu (formula above);  H_air ← Σ x_i (HF0 + H−H0)(T_L), Ar: 5/2·R·T_L
lo ← max(T_L, 400);  hi ← 3200
repeat 60 times:
    T ← (lo + hi)/2
    r ← burn(T, p, β, …)                              equilibrium at T
    f ← H_prod(r, T) − H_air − z_w·h_fF
    if f > 0: hi ← T  else lo ← T
return (lo + hi)/2

Source: Combustion.java, adiabaticTImpl, productEnthalpy; adiabaticTFuel likewise, but with nFhF(TF)n_F h_F(T_F) instead of zwhf,Fz_w h_{f,F} (see below). With …Cached, KpK_p and the product enthalpy go through SpeciesCache.

Real fuels: NJFCP kerosenes

JetFuel contains nine fuels of the National Jet Fuels Combustion Program after Xu, Wang, Colket and Edwards (2015): A1–A3 (JP-8, Jet A, JP-5) and C1–C6 (alternative fuels). A multi-component mixture has no partition function; here the NASA-7 polynomials of the report are the primary source – a documented exception to the module's principle. Two temperature ranges, 298–1000 K and 1000–3000 K:

cpR=a0+a1T+a2T2+a3T3+a4T4,HRT=a0+a1T2+a2T23+a3T34+a4T45+a5T\frac{c_p}{R} = a_0 + a_1 T + a_2 T^2 + a_3 T^3 + a_4 T^4, \qquad \frac{H}{RT} = a_0 + \frac{a_1 T}{2} + \frac{a_2 T^2}{3} + \frac{a_3 T^3}{4} + \frac{a_4 T^4}{5} + \frac{a_5}{T}

S∘R=a0ln⁡T+a1T+a2T22+a3T33+a4T44+a6\frac{S^\circ}{R} = a_0\ln T + a_1 T + \frac{a_2 T^2}{2} + \frac{a_3 T^3}{3} + \frac{a_4 T^4}{4} + a_6

The molar basis is the integer pseudo formula (A2: C11H22, Mapp⁡=11⋅12.011+22⋅1.00794M_{\mathrm{app}} = 11\cdot 12.011 + 22\cdot 1.00794), because ΔfH298∘\Delta_f H^\circ_{298} in the report refers to it. Bridge to the 0 K reference level. The NASA enthalpy refers to the elements at 298.15 K, FundamentalSpecies to 0 K; [H298−H0][H_{298} - H_0] of the fuel is unknown. With

hf0,eff⁡=ΔfH298∘+nC[H298−H0]C(gr)⁡+nH2[H298−H0]H2=ΔfH298∘+1050nC+8468nH2J/mol⁡h_{f0,\mathrm{eff}} = \Delta_f H^\circ_{298} + n_C\,[H_{298} - H_0]_{\mathrm{C(gr)}} + \frac{n_H}{2}\,[H_{298} - H_0]_{\mathrm{H_2}} = \Delta_f H^\circ_{298} + 1050\,n_C + 8468\,\frac{n_H}{2}\ \ \mathrm{J/mol}

and H(T)=hf0,eff⁡+[H(T)−H(298.15)]H(T) = h_{f0,\mathrm{eff}} + [H(T) - H(298.15)] the unknown quantity cancels: ΔfH∘(0K)+[H(T)−H0]=ΔfH298∘−[H298−H0]F+∑el⁡[H298−H0]+[H(T)−H0]F\Delta_f H^\circ(0\,\mathrm K) + [H(T) - H_0] = \Delta_f H^\circ_{298} - [H_{298} - H_0]_F + \sum_{\mathrm{el}}[H_{298} - H_0] + [H(T) - H_0]_F, and [H(T)−H0]F−[H298−H0]F=H(T)−H(298)[H(T) - H_0]_F - [H_{298} - H_0]_F = H(T) - H(298). So H(T)H(T) is exactly consistent with the partition-function species in every balanced reaction.

farStoich(φ, T_L, p) uses the air from psiluf: FARst⁡=Mapp⁡/(L⋅ML)\mathrm{FAR}_{st} = M_{\mathrm{app}}/(L\cdot M_L) with L=(nC+nH/4)/xO2L = (n_C + n_H/4)/x_{\mathrm{O_2}} moles of air per mole of fuel. adiabaticTFuel puts the real enthalpy of the fuel vapour at its inlet temperature into the balance, nFHF(TF)n_F\,H_F(T_F) with nF=zw/Mapp⁡n_F = z_w/M_{\mathrm{app}}, and so also captures preheating.

How it is checked

  • Gleichgewichtsprobe, section "Combustion", four cases (1800–2600 K, 1–20 bar, λ = 0.8 to 1.5, two of them with sulphur): in equilibria computed by the general Gordon-McBride method with the partition-function species, the twelve mass-action laws of Combustion (with p∘=1p^\circ = 1 bar) must hold, max⁡|ln⁡Q−ln⁡K|≤10−9\max|\ln Q - \ln K| \le 10^{-9}; and the mole fractions of Combustion.burnCached itself must match these equilibria (all x>10−14x > 10^{-14}, relative 10−910^{-9}; measured according to the context file 3⋅10−133\cdot10^{-13}). This probe produced the finding on the pressure factors.
  • ThermoTest, section WT24: four cases with sulphur (λ = 0.35 to 0.9, 0.3 to 60 % S): O/N equal to that of a lean calculation with the same air, H2S/SO2 in equilibrium K10K_{10} and the shift condition, each to 10⁻⁹; without sulphur no H2S and SO2 (bit-identity with the old code was shown once during the change). Counter-check wt24-schwefel-nachtraeglich red.
  • ThermoTest, section WT13: shift condition of the rich base solution with sulphur to 2 % and no negative mole fractions (two cases); counter-check wt13-fett-nenner red.
  • Regression anchors (context file): burn(2000 K, β = 0.05, diss) with λ = 1.3645, xCO2 = 0.09868, 3 Newton steps; Tad(λ=2)=1488.4T_{ad}(\lambda = 2) = 1488.4 K.
  • test/JetFuelCompare.java against the tables of the NJFCP report: cpc_p, S, H to 0.05 %, ΔfH298∘\Delta_f H^\circ_{298} exact, heating value closure via CODATA product values to 0.013 %.
  • test/JetFuelTadCompare.java: the maxima of Tad(φ)T_{ad}(\varphi) of all nine fuels at 1 atm and 298 K lie at 2277–2298 K, the report shows 2280–2300 K – at the same time an end-to-end test of the equilibrium calculation.

Limits

  • Fixed species set of 17 species; no condensed phases, no soot. Very rich mixtures (φ≥2\varphi \ge 2) are outside meaningful statements.
  • Base solution with sulphur without dissociation: until 2026-09-28 the O balance did not close (finding WT24, fixed, see above). The base solution has no minor species (SO, OH, NO …); hot gases need diss = true.
  • Heating-value path against fuel path: adiabaticT mixes 0 K enthalpies of formation with a heating value at 298 K without a sensible correction of the fuel and therefore deviates systematically by about 10–15 K from the strict path adiabaticTFuel.
  • Search range: the flame temperature bisection runs between max(TL, 400 K) and 3200 K; higher temperatures are clipped at 3200 K.
  • Convergence: Newton throws on non-convergence (norm > 10⁻⁶ after 200 steps); the start values from the base solution have floors (10⁻¹², 10⁻⁹ for O2, 10⁻¹⁵ for SO2).
  • Run time: an exact burn costs about a second (partition functions at every call), a flame temperature 60 of them. For user interfaces and curves use the …Cached paths.
  • verbOf is a bottleneck in parallel batch runs because of synchronized and the single-entry memory.
  • JetFuel: polynomials 298–3000 K, vapour phase; vaporisation only as a constant hvh_v, no phase model.
  • Equilibrium only. Combustion does not compute finite reaction rates. For that there are Kinetics/Reactor0D and the package envisionz.thermo.kinetik with mechanisms, box solver and stiff Rodas integration (overview).
