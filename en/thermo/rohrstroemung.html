Duct flow with friction and heat – background

The computation behind Rohrstroemung: the Fanno line (adiabatic flow with wall friction), the Rayleigh line (frictionless flow with heat addition), their inverses and a duct element that integrates both at once along the axis – with choking, checks and limits.

What it is about

In a duct of constant cross-section two effects change a compressible flow even though the area stays the same: friction at the wall and heat that is added or removed. Both drive the Mach number towards 1 – subsonic flow accelerates, supersonic flow is decelerated. If the duct is longer than the critical length or the heat larger than the critical heat, the duct chokes: the inlet state can no longer be maintained. The class Gasdynamik holds the shock relations; the two lines live in the new class next to it.

The Fanno line

Adiabatic (T0T_0 fixed) with the Darcy friction factor λ\lambda (λ=4cf\lambda = 4 c_f). Referred to the state at M=1M = 1 (star):

TT*=γ+12+(γ−1)M2,pp*=1MTT*,p0p0*=1M[2+(γ−1)M2γ+1]γ+12(γ−1),\frac{T}{T^*} = \frac{\gamma+1}{2+(\gamma-1)M^2}, \qquad \frac{p}{p^*} = \frac1M\sqrt{\frac{T}{T^*}}, \qquad \frac{p_0}{p_0^*} = \frac1M\Big[\frac{2+(\gamma-1)M^2}{\gamma+1}\Big]^{\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)}},

and the critical length after which the flow reaches M=1M = 1,

λL*D=1−M2γM2+γ+12γln⁡(γ+1)M22+(γ−1)M2.\frac{\lambda L^*}{D} = \frac{1-M^2}{\gamma M^2} + \frac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma}\ln\frac{(\gamma+1)M^2}{2+(\gamma-1)M^2}.

Derivation. Mass ρu=const\rho u = \text{const}, energy cpT+u2/2=cpT0c_pT + u^2/2 = c_pT_0, momentum dp+ρudu=−λDρu22dxdp + \rho u\,du = -\frac{\lambda}{D}\frac{\rho u^2}{2}dx. The first two give T(M)T(M) as above; eliminating pp and ρ\rho leaves

λDdx=1−M2γM4(1+γ−12M2)dM2,\frac{\lambda}{D}\,dx = \frac{1-M^2}{\gamma M^4\,\big(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\big)}\,dM^2 ,

whose integral from MM to 1 is the formula for L*L^*. For M→∞M \to \infty, λL*/D\lambda L^*/D tends to −1/γ+γ+12γln⁡γ+1γ−1-1/\gamma + \frac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma}\ln\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1} (0.8215 at γ=1.4\gamma = 1.4): a supersonic duct cannot be arbitrarily long. The inverse M(λL*/D)M(\lambda L^*/D) is found on the chosen branch with Brent.

The Rayleigh line

Frictionless with heat addition; the impulse p(1+γM2)p(1 + \gamma M^2) and the mass flux stay fixed:

pp*=1+γ1+γM2,TT*=M2(pp*)2,T0T0*=2(γ+1)M2(1+γ−12M2)(1+γM2)2,\frac{p}{p^*} = \frac{1+\gamma}{1+\gamma M^2}, \qquad \frac{T}{T^*} = M^2\Big(\frac{p}{p^*}\Big)^2, \qquad \frac{T_0}{T_0^*} = \frac{2(\gamma+1)M^2\big(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\big)}{(1+\gamma M^2)^2},

p0p0*=pp*[2+(γ−1)M2γ+1]γγ−1.\frac{p_0}{p_0^*} = \frac{p}{p^*}\Big[\frac{2+(\gamma-1)M^2}{\gamma+1}\Big]^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}} .

T0/T0*T_0/T_0^* has its maximum 1 at M=1M = 1: the flow accepts no more heat than q*=cpT0(T0*/T0−1)q^* = c_pT_0(T_0^*/T_0 - 1). At M=0.5M = 0.5, T0/T0*=56/81T_0/T_0^* = 56/81 – the check tests this in closed form.

Duct element with friction and heat

For an arbitrary distribution dT0/dxdT_0/dx together with friction there is no closed solution. Following Shapiro (influence coefficients), with f=γM2λ/Df = \gamma M^2\lambda/D and q=(dT0/dx)/T0q = (dT_0/dx)/T_0

dM2dx=M2(1+γ−12M2)1−M2[(1+γM2)q+f],\frac{dM^2}{dx} = \frac{M^2\big(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\big)}{1-M^2}\Big[(1+\gamma M^2)\,q + f\Big],

1pdpdx=−f(1+(γ−1)M2)2(1−M2)−γM2(1+γ−12M2)1−M2q.\frac{1}{p}\frac{dp}{dx} = -\frac{f\,\big(1+(\gamma-1)M^2\big)}{2(1-M^2)} - \frac{\gamma M^2\big(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\big)}{1-M^2}\,q .

The integration uses Dormand-Prince from framework-math in 400 substeps. The denominator 1−M21 - M^2 shows the choking: if the flow reaches M=1M = 1 before the end of the duct, the element stops, locates the choking point by bisection and reports it. Without heat the element lies exactly on the Fanno line, without friction exactly on the Rayleigh line – the check tests both to 1e-8.

element(M1, p1, T01, λ, D, L, dT0/dx):
    y ← (M1², ln p1, T01)
    for each of the 400 substeps:
        Dormand-Prince on (dM²/dx, dln p/dx, dT0/dx)
        if |1 − M²| < 1e-6: choking point by bisection, end "choked"
    p0 from p, M, γ

How it is checked

  • At M=1M = 1 all ratios are 1 and L*=0L^* = 0 for γ\gamma = 1.4, 1.3, 1.67 (1e-15).
  • Fanno and Rayleigh tables at MM = 0.5, 2, 3 to the rounding of the table (5e-5); limit 0.8215; impulse and mass flux of the Rayleigh line fixed (1e-14).
  • Inverses on both branches from MM 0.05 to 8 to 1e-9; critical heat against 56/81.
  • Duct element without heat equals Fanno, without friction equals Rayleigh (1e-8); twice the critical length chokes exactly at L*L^*; friction and heat together: T0T_0 rises by exactly the 150 K added, mass flow conserved (1e-9), entropy rises.

Limits

  • Ideal gas with fixed γ\gamma; λ\lambda fixed along the length (given from outside, for instance from Rohrreibung).
  • No area change, no shocks in the duct: a choked element is reported, not continued with a normal shock.
