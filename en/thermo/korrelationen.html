Heat transfer – heat transfer and friction correlations

The computation behind Rohrreibung, Rohrwaermeuebergang and Aussenumstroemung: pipe friction factor after Hagen-Poiseuille, Colebrook-White (Newton), Haaland and Petukhov; Nusselt number in the pipe after Hausen, VDI (simultaneous entry), Gnielinski and Dittus-Boelter with a continuous transition range; flat plate, cylinder after Churchill-Bernstein, free convection after Churchill-Chu. Ranges of validity, examples, checks.

What this is about

Cooling passages in turbine blades, oil and fuel lines, casing and heat exchanger design need two numbers: how much heat the wall gives to the fluid (heat transfer coefficient α\alpha) and how much pressure the flow loses on the way. For technical geometries both are accessible only through empirical correlations written in dimensionless groups:

Re=ρwdμ,Pr=μcpλ,Nu=αdλ,Ra=gβΔTL3νa,Δp=λRLdρw22Re = \frac{\rho w d}{\mu}, \qquad Pr = \frac{\mu c_p}{\lambda}, \qquad Nu = \frac{\alpha d}{\lambda}, \qquad Ra = \frac{g\beta\,\Delta T\,L^3}{\nu a}, \qquad \Delta p = \lambda_R\,\frac{L}{d}\,\frac{\rho w^2}{2}

The classes take dimensionless groups and return Nusselt numbers or friction factors; the properties come from outside (e.g. from transport or IF97). Rohrwaermeuebergang.alpha(Nu, λ, d) computes α=Nuλ/d\alpha = Nu\,\lambda/d, Rohrreibung.druckverlust the Darcy-Weisbach formula.

Heat transfer in a circular pipe

All methods return the mean Nusselt number over the pipe length L, properties at the mean fluid temperature; d/Ld/L captures the entry (d/L=0d/L = 0: very long pipe).

Laminar, fully developed: Nu=3.66Nu = 3.66 at constant wall temperature (3.6568, rounded as in the VDI Heat Atlas), Nu=48/11Nu = 48/11 at constant heat flux.

Hausen (thermal entry, developed velocity profile, constant wall temperature), Graetz number Gz=RePrd/LGz = Re\,Pr\,d/L:

Nu=3.66+0.0668Gz1+0.04Gz2/3Nu = 3.66 + \frac{0.0668\,Gz}{1 + 0.04\,Gz^{2/3}}

VDI laminar (hydrodynamic and thermal entry at once, VDI Heat Atlas section G1):

Source: envisionz-framework-thermo/src/envisionz/thermo/waerme/Aussenumstroemung.java.

Convention in the framework: everything dimensionless; roughness relative; Recrit = 2300 in the pipe (friction and heat transfer), 10⁴ as the start of fully turbulent heat transfer, 5·10⁵ on the plate. Inadmissible inputs (not positive, not finite) are rejected with IllegalArgumentException; outside the stated ranges of validity the correlations compute rather than reject – the ranges are in the class comments.

How it is checked

test/envisionz/thermo/waerme/Waermeuebergangsprobe.java, 46 checks, two kinds of target values:

  • Formula anchors: the same published formula recomputed independently in Python (Colebrook there by bisection instead of Newton), tolerance 10⁻¹² relative – catches any change of a coefficient or exponent.
  • Literature anchors: comparison with an independent, older correlation of the same case (Blasius, Hilpert, McAdams, Morgan) with the scatter the literature itself states; Haaland at most 1.5 % from Colebrook. They show that the formula is the right one, not just typed in correctly.
  • Limiting cases and continuity of the composite pipe range at 2300 and 10⁴; 10 counter-probes red.

Found during construction: the own Javadoc gave the mixed plate boundary layer as 0.037(Re0.8−871)Pr1/30.037(Re^{0.8} - 871)Pr^{1/3}; correct is (0.037Re0.8−871)Pr1/3(0.037\,Re^{0.8} - 871)Pr^{1/3}. The code computed correctly, the formula anchor revealed it.

Limits

  • Transition range 2300 < Re < 10⁴: the linear blending is a convention, not physics; the friction factor jumps at 2300.
  • Constant properties over the cross-section; no correction for the temperature dependence of viscosity at the wall (such as (μ/μw)0.14(\mu/\mu_w)^{0.14} or (Pr/Prw)0.11(Pr/Pr_w)^{0.11}).
  • Circular pipe only; non-circular ducts via the hydraulic diameter are the caller's business. No ribs, turbulators or rotation (Coriolis, buoyancy in rotating cooling passages).
  • Ranges of validity are not checked; Colebrook below 4000 and Gnielinski below 3000 return numbers outside their basis.
  • Single phase; no boiling, no condensation.
