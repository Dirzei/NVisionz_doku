Chemical equilibrium – minimising the Gibbs energy

The computation behind Gleichgewicht, Gasgemisch, Reaktanden and Zustandssummenspezies: the Gordon-McBride method (NASA RP-1311, as in CEA) for the problems (T, p), (h, p) and (s, p), the damping, the stopping criteria, the derivatives of the equilibrium up to the speed of sound. Pseudocode following the source, example, check against CEA, limits.

What this is about

A hot gas mixture of the elements C, H, O, N, S and Ar at given temperature and pressure settles to the composition at which its Gibbs energy GG is minimal. Which species appear is not fixed in advance: at 1500 K exhaust gas is practically CO2, H2O, N2, O2; at 3000 K CO, OH, H, O, NO join in percent amounts. The method must therefore work with an arbitrary set of species, most of which are traces.

Unlike Combustion (combustion calculation), which couples a fixed set of 17 species through 12 reactions, Gleichgewicht states the problem in general: given are the element amounts and two state variables, wanted are the amounts of all species. The method is that of Gordon and McBride on which NASA's CEA program is built. It also delivers the derivatives of the equilibrium – cpc_p of the reacting mixture, isentropic exponent and speed of sound – needed by nozzle, shock and detonation calculations (rocket, shock, detonation).

Formulation

For njn_j mol of species jj per kg of mixture, n=∑jnjn = \sum_j n_j, the chemical potential of the ideal gas is

μjRT=gj∘(T)RT+ln⁡njn+ln⁡pp∘,gj∘RT=Hj∘RT−Sj∘R,p∘=1bar⁡\frac{\mu_j}{RT} = \frac{g_j^\circ(T)}{RT} + \ln\frac{n_j}{n} + \ln\frac{p}{p^\circ}, \qquad \frac{g_j^\circ}{RT} = \frac{H_j^\circ}{RT} - \frac{S_j^\circ}{R}, \qquad p^\circ = 1\ \mathrm{bar}

G=∑jnjμjG = \sum_j n_j\mu_j is minimised under the element balances ∑jakjnj=bk∘\sum_j a_{kj} n_j = b_k^\circ (akja_{kj}: atoms of element kk in species jj; bk∘b_k^\circ: element amount in mol per kg). With Lagrange multipliers πk\pi_k (dimensionless, referred to RTRT) the condition for the minimum is

μjRT−∑kakjπk=0for every species j\frac{\mu_j}{RT} - \sum_k a_{kj}\,\pi_k = 0 \qquad \text{for every species } j

For the problems (h, p) and (s, p) the temperature is an additional unknown, and the energy or entropy balance is added, ∑jnjHj∘=h0\sum_j n_j H_j^\circ = h_0 or ∑jnjSj=s0\sum_j n_j S_j = s_0 with Sj/R=Sj∘/R−ln⁡(nj/n)−ln⁡(p/p∘)S_j/R = S_j^\circ/R - \ln(n_j/n) - \ln(p/p^\circ).

The Gordon-McBride Newton method

The unknowns are ln⁡nj\ln n_j, ln⁡n\ln n and – with h or s fixed – ln⁡T\ln T. Linearising the conditions, every species correction can be expressed through the Lagrange multipliers:

Δln⁡nj=−μjRT+∑kakjπk+Δln⁡n+Hj∘RTΔln⁡T\Delta\ln n_j = -\frac{\mu_j}{RT} + \sum_k a_{kj}\,\pi_k + \Delta\ln n + \frac{H_j^\circ}{RT}\,\Delta\ln T

Substituting this into the element balances, the sum of amounts and the energy or entropy balance leaves a linear system of size elements + 1 (+ 1) in πk\pi_k, Δln⁡n\Delta\ln n, Δln⁡T\Delta\ln T – independent of how many species take part. With the abbreviations hj=Hj∘/RTh_j = H_j^\circ/RT, cj=cp,j∘/Rc_j = c_{p,j}^\circ/R, sj=Sj/Rs_j = S_j/R, mj=μj/RTm_j = \mu_j/RT:

∑k(∑jaijakjnj)πk+(∑jaijnj)Δln⁡n+(∑jaijnjhj)Δln⁡T=bi∘−bi+∑jaijnjmj\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}a_{kj}n_j\Bigr)\pi_k + \Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}n_j\Bigr)\Delta\ln n + \Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}n_j h_j\Bigr)\Delta\ln T = b_i^\circ - b_i + \sum_j a_{ij}n_j m_j

∑k(∑jakjnj)πk+(∑jnj−n)Δln⁡n+(∑jnjhj)Δln⁡T=n−∑jnj+∑jnjmj\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{kj}n_j\Bigr)\pi_k + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j - n\Bigr)\Delta\ln n + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j h_j\Bigr)\Delta\ln T = n - \sum_j n_j + \sum_j n_j m_j

For (h, p) the energy balance is added,

∑k(∑jakjnjhj)πk+(∑jnjhj)Δln⁡n+(∑jnjcj+∑jnjhj2)Δln⁡T=h0RT−∑jnjhj+∑jnjhjmj\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{kj}n_j h_j\Bigr)\pi_k + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j h_j\Bigr)\Delta\ln n + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j c_j + \sum_j n_j h_j^2\Bigr)\Delta\ln T = \frac{h_0}{RT} - \sum_j n_j h_j + \sum_j n_j h_j m_j

for (s, p) the entropy balance:

∑k(∑jakjnjsj)πk+(∑jnjsj)Δln⁡n+(∑jnjcj+∑jnjhjsj)Δln⁡T=s0R−∑jnjsj+n−∑jnj+∑jnjsjmj\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{kj}n_j s_j\Bigr)\pi_k + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j s_j\Bigr)\Delta\ln n + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j c_j + \sum_j n_j h_j s_j\Bigr)\Delta\ln T = \frac{s_0}{R} - \sum_j n_j s_j + n - \sum_j n_j + \sum_j n_j s_j m_j

It is solved by Gaussian elimination with column pivoting. Elements with bk∘=0b_k^\circ = 0 and all species containing them are removed from the calculation beforehand.

Damping and stopping

Major species are those with mole fraction xj=nj/n≥10−8x_j = n_j/n \ge 10^{-8}, the rest are traces. The Newton step is shortened by a factor λ≤1\lambda \le 1 so that

  • ln⁡nj\ln n_j of the major species, ln⁡n\ln n and 10⋅ln⁡T10\cdot\ln T change by at most 2: λ≤2/max⁡(|Δln⁡nj|,|Δln⁡n|,10|Δln⁡T|)\lambda \le 2/\max(|\Delta\ln n_j|, |\Delta\ln n|, 10|\Delta\ln T|);
  • no trace jumps above the fraction 10−410^{-4} in one step: for rising traces λ≤(ln⁡10−4−ln⁡xj)/(Δln⁡nj−Δln⁡n)\lambda \le (\ln 10^{-4} - \ln x_j)/(\Delta\ln n_j - \Delta\ln n).

Converged means the full step (λ=1\lambda = 1) is small enough: ∑jnj|Δln⁡nj|≤10−12∑jnj\sum_j n_j|\Delta\ln n_j| \le 10^{-12}\sum_j n_j for the major species, |Δln⁡n|≤10−12|\Delta\ln n| \le 10^{-12}, |Δln⁡T|≤10−12|\Delta\ln T| \le 10^{-12}, and for the traces min⁡(|Δln⁡nj|,105xj|Δln⁡nj|)≤10−9\min(|\Delta\ln n_j|,\ 10^5\,x_j|\Delta\ln n_j|) \le 10^{-9}. The second form accounts for traces sometimes fixing a direction of the π\pi on their own (for example O next to CO2 and H2O when O2 is only 10−910^{-9}); the system is then ill-conditioned by about 1/x1/x, and ln⁡nj\ln n_j of the trace jitters by ε/x\varepsilon/x without h, s or the major species noticing more than 10−1510^{-15}. Traces at the floor x=e−700x = e^{-700} that keep falling do not count.

Pseudocode – Gleichgewicht.loese(g, b0, kind, T0, p, target, neighbour)

check b0 ≥ 0, p > 0, T0 > 0;  active elements: b0k > 0;  active species: only active elements
if neighbour state: ln n, ln nj from there (floor 10⁻³⁰⁰·n)
else: n ← ½·Σ b0;  ln nj ← ln(n/number of active species)
for it ← 1 to 500:
    T ← e^lnT;  for each active species: (cj, hj, s°j) ← values(T)
        mj ← hj − s°j + ln nj − ln n + ln(p/p°)
    build system (elements + 1 [+ 1]) as above;  x ← Gauss(system)
    for each active species: Δj ← −mj + Δln n + hj·Δln T + Σk akj·πk
    λ ← 1
    for each trace (xj < 10⁻⁸) with Δj − Δln n > 0:
        λ ← min(λ, (ln 10⁻⁴ − ln xj)/(Δj − Δln n))
    largest ← max(maxmajor|Δj|, |Δln n|, 10·|Δln T|)
    if largest > 2: λ ← min(λ, 2/largest)
    if λ ≥ 1 and criteria (see above) met:
        apply full step;  return result(…)             with derivatives
    ln nj ← max(ln nj + λ·Δj, ln n − 700);  ln n += λ·Δln n;  ln T += λ·Δln T
    if T < 50 K: T ← 50 K
throw "no convergence after 500 steps"

Derivatives of the equilibrium

When TT or pp change, the equilibrium shifts; the properties of the reacting mixture contain this shift. Following RP-1311, section 2.5, (∂πk/∂ln⁡T)p(\partial\pi_k/\partial\ln T)_p, (∂ln⁡n/∂ln⁡T)p(\partial\ln n/\partial\ln T)_p and (∂πk/∂ln⁡p)T(\partial\pi_k/\partial\ln p)_T, (∂ln⁡n/∂ln⁡p)T(\partial\ln n/\partial\ln p)_T come from two linear systems with the same matrix, each at the converged state:

∑k(∑jaijakjnj)∂πk∂ln⁡T+(∑jaijnj)∂ln⁡n∂ln⁡T=−∑jaijnjhj,∑k(∑jakjnj)∂πk∂ln⁡T=−∑jnjhj\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}a_{kj}n_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\pi_k}{\partial\ln T} + \Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}n_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\ln n}{\partial\ln T} = -\sum_j a_{ij}n_j h_j, \qquad \sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{kj}n_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\pi_k}{\partial\ln T} = -\sum_j n_j h_j

∑k(∑jaijakjnj)∂πk∂ln⁡p+(∑jaijnj)∂ln⁡n∂ln⁡p=∑jaijnj,∑k(∑jakjnj)∂πk∂ln⁡p=n\sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}a_{kj}n_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\pi_k}{\partial\ln p} + \Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}n_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\ln n}{\partial\ln p} = \sum_j a_{ij}n_j, \qquad \sum_k\Bigl(\sum_j a_{kj}n_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\pi_k}{\partial\ln p} = n

From these:

(∂ln⁡V∂ln⁡T)p=1+∂ln⁡n∂ln⁡T,(∂ln⁡V∂ln⁡p)T=−1+∂ln⁡n∂ln⁡p\left(\frac{\partial\ln V}{\partial\ln T}\right)_p = 1 + \frac{\partial\ln n}{\partial\ln T}, \qquad \left(\frac{\partial\ln V}{\partial\ln p}\right)_T = -1 + \frac{\partial\ln n}{\partial\ln p}

cp,eq⁡R=∑jnjcj+∑jnjhj2+(∑jnjhj)∂ln⁡n∂ln⁡T+∑i(∑jaijnjhj)∂πi∂ln⁡T\frac{c_{p,\mathrm{eq}}}{R} = \sum_j n_j c_j + \sum_j n_j h_j^2 + \Bigl(\sum_j n_j h_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\ln n}{\partial\ln T} + \sum_i\Bigl(\sum_j a_{ij}n_j h_j\Bigr)\frac{\partial\pi_i}{\partial\ln T}

cv,eq⁡=cp,eq⁡+nR(∂ln⁡V/∂ln⁡T)p2(∂ln⁡V/∂ln⁡p)T,γs=−cp,eq⁡/cv,eq⁡(∂ln⁡V/∂ln⁡p)T,aeq⁡=nRTγsc_{v,\mathrm{eq}} = c_{p,\mathrm{eq}} + nR\,\frac{(\partial\ln V/\partial\ln T)_p^2}{(\partial\ln V/\partial\ln p)_T}, \qquad \gamma_s = -\frac{c_{p,\mathrm{eq}}/c_{v,\mathrm{eq}}}{(\partial\ln V/\partial\ln p)_T}, \qquad a_{\mathrm{eq}} = \sqrt{nRT\,\gamma_s}

The "frozen" quantities at fixed composition are cp,fr⁡=R∑jnjcjc_{p,\mathrm{fr}} = R\sum_j n_j c_j, γfr⁡=cp,fr⁡/(cp,fr⁡−nR)\gamma_{\mathrm{fr}} = c_{p,\mathrm{fr}}/(c_{p,\mathrm{fr}} - nR), afr⁡=γfr⁡nRTa_{\mathrm{fr}} = \sqrt{\gamma_{\mathrm{fr}}\,nRT}. The state also provides h=RT∑njhjh = RT\sum n_j h_j, s=R∑njsjs = R\sum n_j s_j, u=h−nRTu = h - nRT, ρ=p/(nRT)\rho = p/(nRT).

Fixed composition. gefroren(g, n, T, p) computes the state without equilibrium (ideal-gas derivatives, (∂ln⁡V/∂ln⁡T)p=1(\partial\ln V/\partial\ln T)_p = 1, (∂ln⁡V/∂ln⁡p)T=−1(\partial\ln V/\partial\ln p)_T = -1); gefrorenSP finds the temperature of a frozen isentrope by Newton in ln⁡T\ln T, Δln⁡T=(s0−s)/cp\Delta\ln T = (s_0 - s)/c_p, limited to ±0.5, until |Δln⁡T|<10−14|\Delta\ln T| < 10^{-14} (at most 100 steps).

Property data and units

Species come through the interface Spezies: name, elemental composition, molar mass, the gas constant with which the dimensionless quantities are formed, and cp∘/Rc_p^\circ/R, H∘/RTH^\circ/RT, S∘/RS^\circ/R at 1 bar. The enthalpy is absolute (including the enthalpy of formation) on a common reference level; whether that is 0 K or 298.15 K does not matter as long as all species of a calculation share it. Gasgemisch checks that all species have the same gas constant and builds the element matrix.

Zustandssummenspezies provides the 23 species of FundamentalSpecies: H∘/RT=[ΔfH∘(0K)+H−H0]/(RT)H^\circ/RT = [\Delta_f H^\circ(0\,\mathrm K) + H - H_0]/(RT), S∘/RS^\circ/R from the partition function, cp∘c_p^\circ as the central difference of the enthalpy with ±10−3\pm 10^{-3} K, R=8.31441R = 8.31441 J/(mol K). The default is the tabulated path via SpeciesCache (200–4000 K); the exact path costs about 0.6 s per temperature for H2O. The last computed temperature is remembered per species. Reaktanden forms the element amounts and the enthalpy per kg from species (or from a formula with its own enthalpy, for example a liquid fuel).

Convention in the framework: element amounts bb in mol per kg of mixture, amounts njn_j in mol/kg, hh in J/kg, ss in J/(kg K), pp in Pa, reference pressure p∘=105p^\circ = 10^5 Pa. Gas phase only; property data from the partition functions (F5). NASA-9 polynomials exist only in the probe, as a tool for the comparison with CEA.

Limits

  • Gas phase only: no condensed species (soot, water, sulphuric acid), no ions. Very rich or very cold equilibria in which condensate would form are therefore not correct.
  • Species set of the partition functions: 23 species. A set of 60–80 species for C/H/O/N/Ar/S is open (WS5-D).
  • Property data: the partition-function species deviate noticeably from NASA-9 in places (CH3O up to 35.7 kJ/mol in the Gibbs energy, OH 2.2 kJ/mol, cp of CH4 11.6 % at 3000 K); this enters the composition. See partition functions.
  • Temperature range: tabulated 200–4000 K, outside exact and slow; the Newton iteration clamps T≥50T \ge 50 K.
  • Start value: uniform without a neighbour state; for sequences of states (nozzle, shock) a neighbour as start speeds things up considerably.
