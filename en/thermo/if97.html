Water and steam – IAPWS-IF97 and transport properties

The computation behind If97 and Wassertransport: the five regions of the industrial formulation, saturation line and B23 boundary, properties from Gibbs and Helmholtz functions, the backward paths T(p,h), T(p,s), p(h,s) via the forward equations, the density search in region 3, viscosity after R12-08 and thermal conductivity after R15-11. Pseudocode, examples, checks, limits.

What this is about

Steam cycles, heat recovery boilers, water and steam injection in gas turbines and cooling circuits need the properties of water from the triple point far into the supercritical region. The scientific reference is IAPWS-95, a Helmholtz equation with 56 terms (Helmholtz equations). For practical use IAPWS issued the industrial formulation IF97 in 1997: five simpler equations for five regions of the p-T plane, fast and explicit in the usual input variables, deviating from IAPWS-95 within its uncertainty.

If97 implements release R7-97(2012), all five regions. One deliberate departure from the usual implementation: the backward equations of IF97 (T(p,h), T(p,s), p(h,s), v(p,T) in region 3) are not used. Instead the forward equations are inverted numerically – slower, but as consistent as the forward equations themselves; the backward equations deviate from them by a few millikelvin.

Below TcT_c the isotherm p(ρ)p(\rho) has a van der Waals loop in region 3 and therefore up to three roots. For liquid (p≥ps(T)p \ge p_s(T)) and above TcT_c the largest applies, for vapour the smallest. The staged bracketing finds the right one as long as the loop is wider than 0.01 kg/m³.

Backward paths via the forward equations

(p, h) and (p, s). Below the critical pressure the saturation at pp is computed first. If the target lies between the values of saturated liquid and saturated vapour, the result is wet steam with quality x=(z−z')/(z''−z')x = (z - z')/(z'' - z'), and v,h,u,sv, h, u, s are mixed linearly (cp, cv, w are not defined there). Otherwise T is found by Brent (tolerance 10−1010^{-10} K) on the right branch: liquid branch between 273.15 K and TsT_s, vapour branch between TsT_s and 2273.15 K (1073.15 K above 50 MPa). The branch must be prescribed, because vonPT would always pick the liquid exactly at the saturation temperature.

(h, s). At fixed hh, ss falls strictly with pressure, also through the wet-steam region:

(∂s∂p)h=−vT<0\left(\frac{\partial s}{\partial p}\right)_h = -\frac{v}{T} < 0

So the search is one-dimensional: Brent in ln⁡p\ln p (tolerance 10−1310^{-13}) between 1 Pa (or the triple-point pressure) and 100 MPa or the highest pressure at which hh is still valid (found by 60 bisections), with vonPH inside.

// vonPGroesse(p, z, quantity): common path of vonPH and vonPS
if p_triple ≤ p < p_c:
    sat ← saturation(p, T_s(p));  z' ← quantity(sat.liquid);  z'' ← quantity(sat.vapour)
    if z' ≤ z ≤ z'': return mixture(sat, x = (z − z')/(z'' − z'))
    if z < z': return search(p, z, 273.15 … T_s, liquid branch)
    return search(p, z, T_s … T_upper, vapour branch)
if p < p_triple: return search(p, z, 273.15 … T_upper, vapour)
return search(p, z, 273.15 … T_upper, liquid/supercritical)

Viscosity and thermal conductivity

Both in the form for industrial use, with T=T/647.096\bar T = T/647.096, ρ=ρ/322\bar\rho = \rho/322 and the density from IF97.

Viscosity after IAPWS R12-08, μ=μ0(T)μ1(T,ρ)μ2\mu = \mu_0(\bar T)\,\mu_1(\bar T, \bar\rho)\,\mu_2 in µPa s with μ2=1\mu_2 = 1:

The omitted critical part μ2\mu_2 exceeds 2 % only for 645.91 K < T < 650.77 K and 245.8 < ρ < 405.3 kg/m³.

Convention in the framework: strictly SI – p in Pa, h and u in J/kg, s, cp, cv in J/(kg K), μ in Pa s, λ in W/(m K). Outside the range of validity all methods except psatGleichung/tsatGleichung throw an IllegalArgumentException. Exactly on the saturation line a (p, T) state counts as liquid. The coefficients were transcribed from the page images of the releases and every mantissa checked against the text.

Worked examples

Turbine expansion in a steam cycle, live steam 100 bar, 550 °C, isentropic to 0.08 bar:

If97.Zustand ein = If97.vonPT(100e5, 823.15);    // region 2: h = 3501.940 kJ/kg, s = 6.75837 kJ/(kg K)
If97.Zustand aus = If97.vonPS(0.08e5, ein.s());  // wet steam: T = 314.660 K, x = 0.807588, h = 2113.991 kJ/kg
double wt = ein.h() - aus.h();                   // 1387.949 kJ/kg

Feed water 150 bar, 470 K: ρ = 878.030 kg/m³, cp = 4408.80 J/(kg K), μ = 1.40214·10⁻⁴ Pa s, λ = 0.67294 W/(m K), Pr = 0.91861.

Region 3 at 25 MPa, 650 K (just supercritical pressure): ρ = 488.875 kg/m³, cp = 15 731 J/(kg K). Saturation: ps(373.15K)=101417.98p_s(373.15\ \mathrm K) = 101\,417.98 Pa, Ts(105Pa⁡)=372.75592T_s(10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}) = 372.75592 K.

How it is checked

test/envisionz/thermo/wasser/If97probe.java, 203 checks:

  • Verification tables of the releases: IF97 tables 5 (region 1), 15 (region 2), 33 (region 3), 35 and 36 (saturation), 42 (region 5) and the B23 point – v, h, u, s, cp, w to 10⁻⁸ relative (the tables have nine digits). The density in table 33 is matched only up to the rounding of the tabulated pressure times (∂ρ/∂p)T(\partial\rho/\partial p)_T; the probe computes this tolerance. According to the module description IF97 agrees to at most 3.2·10⁻⁹.
  • R12-08 table 4 (viscosity), R15-11 tables 4, 7, 8, 9 with all intermediate quantities (λ2, ρ, ∂ρ/∂p, cp, cv, μ) to 10⁻⁸ or 10⁻⁷; from table 5 only λ1, because the rest needs IAPWS-95.
  • Internal relations: Clapeyron dps/dT=Δs/Δv\mathrm dp_s/\mathrm dT = \Delta s/\Delta v between region 4 and the basic equations (10⁻³, difference quotient); equal Gibbs energy of the phases; backward paths (p,T) → h → (p,h) → T and via s to 10⁻⁹ for 17 points in all regions; (h,s) → (p,T) to 10⁻⁸ in p and 10⁻⁹ in T, also in wet steam; quality via h and s to 10⁻¹⁰.
  • Edges: rejection outside the range of validity. 11 counter-probes are red.

Found during construction: a transcription error in coefficient L22L_{22} of R15-11 (3.55772244 instead of 3.55777244), visible only in table 4 at 298.15 K as a 0.1 % deviation.

Limits

  • Validity (IF97 section 1): 273.15–1073.15 K up to 100 MPa, 1073.15–2273.15 K up to 50 MPa. Below 273.15 K psatGleichung is an extension over subcooled water, not an IAPWS statement.
  • Close to the critical point (van der Waals loop narrower than 0.01 kg/m³) the assignment of the density root is no longer certain; IF97 is of limited use there anyway.
  • Run time: the backward paths need many forward evaluations; (h, s) nests two searches.
  • Transport properties: viscosity without critical part; above 1173.15 K both formulations are extensions. Wet steam has no transport properties.
  • For properties of the highest accuracy and in the critical region: IAPWS-95 (Helmholtz equations).
