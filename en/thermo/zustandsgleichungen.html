Equations of state behind one contract – background

The computation behind EquationOfState, GasState and the six implementations: what a flow solver needs from an equation of state, the derivatives (∂p/∂ρ)e and (∂p/∂e)ρ and their link to the speed of sound, the inversion (ρ, e) → T per implementation, AGA8-DC92. Pseudocode, examples, checks, limits.

What this is about

A finite-volume solver for compressible flow (machieve) transports the conserved quantities density ρ\rho, momentum ρu→\rho\vec u and total energy ρet\rho e_t. After every step it has to recover the thermodynamic state from them: pressure, temperature, enthalpy, speed of sound. Which property model is behind it – ideal gas, natural gas after AGA8 or GERG-2008, steam after IF97 – the solver should not need to know. That is what the contract EquationOfState provides: four conversions with the fixed result GasState.

GasState carries ρ,u,v,w,e,T,p,h,a\rho, u, v, w, e, T, p, h, a, the isentropic exponent κ\kappa, the derivatives (∂p/∂ρ)e(\partial p/\partial\rho)_e and (∂p/∂e)ρ(\partial p/\partial e)_\rho and viscosity η\eta and thermal conductivity λ\lambda (NaN where the model does not provide them), all in SI.

The derivatives of the contract

Flux schemes such as Roe or AUSM and the Jacobians of implicit solvers need the pressure as a function of the conserved quantities and its derivatives at fixed e or fixed ρ – not at fixed T. For any equation of state

(∂p∂e)ρ=(∂p/∂T)ρcv,a2=(∂p∂ρ)s=(∂p∂ρ)e+pρ2(∂p∂e)ρ\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial e}\right)_\rho = \frac{(\partial p/\partial T)_\rho}{c_v}, \qquad a^2 = \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial\rho}\right)_s = \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial\rho}\right)_e + \frac{p}{\rho^2}\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial e}\right)_\rho

The second relation follows from de=Tds+(p/ρ2)dρ\mathrm de = T\,\mathrm ds + (p/\rho^2)\,\mathrm d\rho: along an isentrope de=(p/ρ2)dρ\mathrm de = (p/\rho^2)\,\mathrm d\rho. The real-gas implementations therefore compute (∂p/∂e)ρ(\partial p/\partial e)_\rho from the equation and (∂p/∂ρ)e=a2−(p/ρ2)(∂p/∂e)ρ(\partial p/\partial\rho)_e = a^2 - (p/\rho^2)(\partial p/\partial e)_\rho from the exact speed of sound. The isentropic exponent is uniformly κ=a2ρ/p\kappa = a^2\rho/p.

For the ideal gas (ee depends on TT only) (∂p/∂ρ)e=RT(\partial p/\partial\rho)_e = RT and (∂p/∂e)ρ=ρR/cv=ρ(γ−1)(\partial p/\partial e)_\rho = \rho R/c_v = \rho(\gamma - 1).

Convention in the framework: GasState.dpdRho is (∂p/∂ρ)e(\partial p/\partial\rho)_e in m²/s², dpde is (∂p/∂e)ρ(\partial p/\partial e)_\rho in kg/m³. For AGA8 both violated this convention until 26.09.2026 (Grüneisen parameter without the factor ρ; ∂p/∂ρ at fixed T instead of fixed e – for natural gas at 70 bar 0.36 instead of 23.9 and 92 400 instead of 111 600 m²/s²); since then corrected via the relation above. Water below its density maximum (about 4 °C) has (∂p/∂T)ρ<0(\partial p/\partial T)_\rho < 0: the IF97 binding takes the magnitude from cp−cv=T(∂p/∂T)ρ2/(ρ2(∂p/∂ρ)T)c_p - c_v = T(\partial p/\partial T)_\rho^2/(\rho^2(\partial p/\partial\rho)_T) and the sign separately from a density difference at fixed pressure.

AGA8-DC92

The compressibility factor of the AGA8 detail characterisation (1992) is a series in the reduced density Dr=K3DD_r = K^3 D (DD molar density in mol/l):

Z=1+BD−Dr∑n=1318Cn*T−un+∑n=1358Cn*T−un(bn−cnknDrkn)Drbnexp⁡(−cnDrkn)Z = 1 + B\,D - D_r\sum_{n=13}^{18}C_n^* T^{-u_n} + \sum_{n=13}^{58}C_n^* T^{-u_n}\,(b_n - c_nk_nD_r^{k_n})\,D_r^{b_n}\exp(-c_nD_r^{k_n})

with the second virial coefficient B=∑n=118Bn*T−unB = \sum_{n=1}^{18}B_n^* T^{-u_n} and the mixture quantities Cn*C_n^*, KK from the composition (AGA8MixingState). From this the class computes the derivatives Fd=ZF_d = Z, Fdd=Z+D∂Z/∂DF_{dd} = Z + D\,\partial Z/\partial D, the residual Helmholtz energy and its T derivatives; the ideal part comes from the Cordes-DeWit cp form (Aly-Lee form with sinh and cosh terms). Hence:

p=FdDRT,h=RTM(Fr,t+F0,t+Fd),a2=RTM(Fdd−Fdt2F0,tt+Fr,tt)p = F_d\,DRT, \qquad h = \frac{RT}{M}(F_{r,t} + F_{0,t} + F_d), \qquad a^2 = \frac{RT}{M}\left(F_{dd} - \frac{F_{dt}^2}{F_{0,tt} + F_{r,tt}}\right)

D ← ρ/M;  T ← 300 K;  F_d ← FundEqD(D, 300 K)            once before the loop, as in F77
for it ← 1 to 100:
    (F_r,t, F_r,tt) ← FundEqT(D, T);  (F_0,t, F_0,tt) ← CordesDeWit(T)

IF97 and Helmholtz: inversion at fixed density

IF97 is formulated in (p, T). At fixed density the pressure is therefore searched: below TcT_c, ρ against the saturation densities decides the branch (in between lies wet steam, which cannot be represented in GasState and is rejected), then Brent in ln⁡p\ln p (10−1410^{-14}). The energy search in T begins only where the isochore leaves the wet-steam region (untereTemperatur: first sign change of saturation density against ρ from 273.15 K, 100 stages, Brent) and ends at 2273.15 K or the highest temperature with a valid pressure (60 bisections). This is exact but slow (several hundred IF97 evaluations) – meant for boundaries and initial fields.

The Helmholtz binding computes in (ρ, T) directly. Inversions at fixed density are Newton in T with the exact derivatives cvc_v or (∂p/∂T)ρ(\partial p/\partial T)_\rho, starting at the last result of the same thread (ThreadLocal), stopping at |ΔT|≤10−9T|\Delta T| \le 10^{-9}T – two to three evaluations in the flow solver. If that fails, Newton from the geometric middle of the interval follows, safeguarded by Brent (10−12T10^{-12}T).

How it is checked

test/envisionz/thermo/zustand/Zustandsgleichungsprobe.java, 45 checks:

  • AGA8 against the NIST implementation (usnistgov/AGA8, Detail.cpp, block TEST_DETAIL: 21-component gas, 400 K, 50 MPa; molar density 12.80792403648801 mol/l, compressibility factor 1.173801364147326, molar mass 20.54333051 g/mol) – according to the module description to 7·10⁻¹³.
  • Bit-identical before and after the move from machieve: nine states, computed with the old classes, stored as bit patterns; in addition all 42 test runners of machieve give the same output before and after the move.
  • Round trips (p, T) → (ρ, e) → (p, T) and (ρ, p) → T for every equation.
  • Derivatives of the contract (∂p/∂ρ)e(\partial p/\partial\rho)_e, (∂p/∂e)ρ(\partial p/\partial e)_\rho against difference quotients via the inversion. For LuftIdeal only to 10⁻⁷, because AirFunctions.cp is a difference of the enthalpy table, not its derivative.
  • IF97 binding against If97 and Wassertransport themselves, including the density anomaly below 4 °C; 9 counter-probes red. The Helmholtz binding is checked by the Helmholtzprobe.

Limits

  • isentropicExpansion: AGA8 throws UnsupportedOperationException. Until 2026-09-28 the two ideal gases ignored s0s_0 and determined T from h0−u2/2h_0 - u^2/2 with the passed velocity estimate (finding WT25, fixed). Now they compute the isentrope like LuftIdeal; the entropy comes from their method entropie(p, T) with the reference 298.15 K and 101 325 Pa, s=cpln⁡(T/Tref⁡)−Rln⁡(p/pref⁡)s = c_p\ln(T/T_\mathrm{ref}) - R\ln(p/p_\mathrm{ref}) or integrated with cp(T)c_p(T). Entropies of different classes have different references and cannot be compared with each other; s0s_0 must come from the same class.
  • Thread safety: the contract does not require it (until 2026-09-28 it said “MUST be thread-safe”, which AGA8 never met; corrected since). Thread-safe are LuftIdeal, If97Zustandsgleichung, HelmholtzZustandsgleichung and the ideal gases; AGA8EquationOfState is not (work buffers per instance, stateful cp object, static counter) – one instance per thread.
  • AGA8 clamps instead of throwing: if the inversion does not converge, a state at the clamp (50 or 5000 K) comes back; the counter NEWTON_CLAMP_HITS shows how often.
  • Single phase only: IF97 and Helmholtz pure fluids reject wet steam; GERG-2008 has no phase equilibrium.
  • Transport properties only for IF97 (η, λ); the others return NaN (dilute-gas transport: transport).
  • ThermallyPerfectIdealGas defaults to an exchangeable air fit (Cengel), not a property source of the module.
