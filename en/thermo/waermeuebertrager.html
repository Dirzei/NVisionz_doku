Heat transfer – heat exchangers by ε-NTU and LMTD

The computation behind Waermeuebertrager: effectiveness ε(NTU, Cr) for counterflow, parallel flow, four kinds of crossflow and shell-and-tube, crossflow with both streams unmixed exactly after Mason, the inversion NTU(ε) also for the non-monotonic case, log-mean temperature difference without cancellation, correction factor F, rating and sizing. Pseudocode, examples, checks, limits.

What this is about

Recuperator, oil and air coolers, intercoolers, heat recovery boilers: two streams exchange heat through a wall. Two questions come up again and again. Rating: the device is given (overall heat transfer coefficient times area, UAUA), and so are the inlet temperatures – how much heat is transferred, how warm do the streams leave? Sizing: the heat is prescribed – which UAUA is needed? The ε-NTU method answers both in closed form or with a one-dimensional search; the classical log-mean temperature difference method (LMTD) is equivalent and enters as the correction factor F.

Notation

C=m˙cpC = \dot m c_p is the heat capacity rate in W/K, Cmin⁡C_{\min} and Cmax⁡C_{\max} the smaller and larger of the two streams. Then

Cr=Cmin⁡Cmax⁡(0≤Cr≤1),NTU=UACmin⁡,ε=Q˙Q˙max⁡,Q˙max⁡=Cmin⁡(Th,in⁡−Tc,in⁡)C_r = \frac{C_{\min}}{C_{\max}}\ (0 \le C_r \le 1), \qquad NTU = \frac{UA}{C_{\min}}, \qquad \varepsilon = \frac{\dot Q}{\dot Q_{\max}}, \qquad \dot Q_{\max} = C_{\min}\,(T_{h,\mathrm{in}} - T_{c,\mathrm{in}})

ε\varepsilon says which fraction of the largest possible heat is transferred; it depends only on NTUNTU, CrC_r and the flow arrangement. Cr=0C_r = 0 is the limiting case of a condensing or evaporating stream; then ε=1−e−NTU\varepsilon = 1 - e^{-NTU} for every arrangement.

Inversion NTU(ε)

Sizing needs NTUNTU for a given ε\varepsilon. The class inverts all arrangements uniformly by bracketing and bisection. One arrangement is delicate: for crossflow with both streams mixed ε\varepsilon is not monotonic in NTU – at Cr=1C_r = 1 it has a maximum of 0.5645 at NTU 2.98 and then falls towards 0.5. The inversion returns the smallest NTU there, i.e. the rising branch.

// ntu(arrangement, ε, Cr)
if ε = 0: return 0
upper ← 1;  previous ← ε_arr(0.5, Cr)
repeat:
    e ← ε_arr(upper, Cr)
    if e ≥ ε: stop
    if e < previous:                          ε falls again
        upper ← location of maximum(arr, Cr, upper/4, upper)   golden section, 10⁻¹² relative
        if ε_arr(upper) < ε: throw "not reachable";  stop
    previous ← e;  upper ← 2·upper
    if upper > 10⁴: throw "not reachable"           e.g. above the parallel-flow limit
lower ← 0
while upper − lower > 10⁻¹³·upper (at most 200):
    m ← (lower + upper)/2;  if ε_arr(m) < ε: lower ← m  else upper ← m
return (lower + upper)/2

LMTD and correction factor F

The log-mean temperature difference with the temperature differences ΔT1\Delta T_1, ΔT2\Delta T_2 at the two ends:

ΔTlm=ΔT1−ΔT2ln⁡(ΔT1/ΔT2)=ΔT2xln⁡(1+x),x=ΔT1−ΔT2ΔT2\Delta T_{lm} = \frac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2)} = \Delta T_2\,\frac{x}{\ln(1 + x)}, \qquad x = \frac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\Delta T_2}

The second form is computed: ΔT1−ΔT2\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2 is formed exactly even for nearly equal end differences, whereas ΔT1/ΔT2−1\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2 - 1 is already rounded; the logarithm uses log1p. For |x|<10−6|x| < 10^{-6} the class takes the series ΔT2(1+x/2−x2/12+x3/24)\Delta T_2(1 + x/2 - x^2/12 + x^3/24) – continuous in the limit of equal end differences.

For arrangements other than counterflow Q˙=UAFΔTlm,counterflow⁡\dot Q = UA\,F\,\Delta T_{lm,\mathrm{counterflow}}. The equivalence of both methods yields the correction factor without a separate formula per arrangement:

F=NTUcounterflow⁡(ε,Cr)NTUarrangement⁡(ε,Cr)F = \frac{NTU_{\mathrm{counterflow}}(\varepsilon, C_r)}{NTU_{\mathrm{arrangement}}(\varepsilon, C_r)}

At equal ε\varepsilon and CrC_r the outlet temperatures are equal, hence also ΔTlm,counterflow⁡\Delta T_{lm,\mathrm{counterflow}}; the smaller UAUA of counterflow relates to the larger one of the arrangement by FF.

Rating (nachrechnen): Cmin⁡C_{\min}, CrC_r, NTU=UA/Cmin⁡NTU = UA/C_{\min}, ε\varepsilon, Q˙=εCmin⁡(Th,in⁡−Tc,in⁡)\dot Q = \varepsilon C_{\min}(T_{h,\mathrm{in}} - T_{c,\mathrm{in}}), Th,out⁡=Th,in⁡−Q˙/ChT_{h,\mathrm{out}} = T_{h,\mathrm{in}} - \dot Q/C_h, Tc,out⁡=Tc,in⁡+Q˙/CcT_{c,\mathrm{out}} = T_{c,\mathrm{in}} + \dot Q/C_c. Sizing (auslegen): ε=Q˙/Q˙max⁡\varepsilon = \dot Q/\dot Q_{\max}, UA=NTU(ε)⋅Cmin⁡UA = NTU(\varepsilon)\cdot C_{\min}.

Convention in the framework: ε always refers to Cmin⁡C_{\min}; CrC_r outside [0, 1] is rejected. Unreachable ε (above the parallel-flow limit 1/(1+Cr)1/(1 + C_r) or above the maximum of the mixed-mixed crossflow) throw IllegalArgumentException with the limit in the message. The shell-and-tube formula holds for one shell pass and an even number of tube passes.

How it is checked

test/envisionz/thermo/waerme/Waermeuebertragerprobe.java, 46 checks:

  • Formula anchors: the relations per arrangement recomputed independently in Python, 10⁻¹² relative; Mason there with a fixed number of terms instead of a stopping criterion.
  • Independent second paths: F from the ε-NTU equivalence against Bowman's closed formula for the 1-2 shell-and-tube exchanger (10⁻⁹); the heat of a rating against UAΔTlmUA\,\Delta T_{lm} for counterflow; energy balance.
  • Limiting cases: Cr = 0 gives 1−e−NTU1 - e^{-NTU} for every arrangement; counterflow Cr = 1 gives NTU/(1 + NTU); parallel flow never exceeds 1/(1 + Cr); all arrangements lie between parallel flow and counterflow.
  • Literature: 0.476 after Kays and London; 8 counter-probes red.

Found during construction: (1) the inversion assumed monotonicity and reported reachable ε as unreachable for the mixed-mixed crossflow – it now searches for the maximum; (2) the LMTD lost 2·10⁻¹² near equal end differences via ln⁡(ΔT1/ΔT2)\ln(\Delta T_1/\Delta T_2) – now log1p of the exactly formed difference.

Limits

  • Constant heat capacity rates and constant U across the device; no phase change except the limiting case Cr = 0; no axial wall conduction, no leakage or bypass streams.
  • Arrangements restricted to the seven forms; plate exchangers or multi-pass cross-counterflow are not covered (only approximately via shell-and-tube with n passes).
  • U comes from outside, e.g. from the correlations (correlations) and the wall resistance.
  • Unreachable ε are rejected, not clipped to the limit.
