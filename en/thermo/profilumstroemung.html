Exact airfoils and airfoil polar – background

The computation behind Konform, Profilpolare and Profilkopplung: Joukowski and Kármán-Trefftz airfoils as exact solutions of potential flow against which the panel method can be measured, and the polar from the panel method and an integral boundary layer with transition after Michel, drag after Squire and Young a displacement correction of the lift and the computation coupled simultaneously by Newton up to shortly before separation.

What it is about

The panel method (Panel) computes the inviscid flow around any airfoil approximately. How well only shows on an airfoil whose flow is known exactly: the conformal map of the flow around a circle yields such airfoils together with pressure distribution and lift in closed form. The airfoil polar goes one step further: from the inviscid pressure distribution a boundary layer is computed on each side, from its state at the trailing edge the drag, and from its displacement thickness a correction of the lift – in one step or, coupled, such that boundary layer and outer flow match.

Joukowski and Kármán-Trefftz

A circle with centre ζm\zeta_m and radius aa passing through the point ζ=b\zeta = b is mapped by

z−nbz+nb=(ζ−bζ+b)n,n=2−τπ,\frac{z - nb}{z + nb} = \Big(\frac{\zeta - b}{\zeta + b}\Big)^n, \qquad n = 2 - \frac{\tau}{\pi},

onto an airfoil with trailing-edge angle τ\tau (Kármán-Trefftz). For n=2n = 2 this is the Joukowski map z=ζ+b2/ζz = \zeta + b^2/\zeta with a cusped trailing edge; for n→1n \to 1 the identity. The code uses the ratio form z=nb(1+r)/(1−r)z = nb\,(1 + r)/(1 - r) with r=((ζ−b)/(ζ+b))nr = ((\zeta - b)/(\zeta + b))^n: the power of a ratio has its branch cut outside the circle, the separate powers of ζ±b\zeta \pm b had it on the airfoil.

The derivative

dzdζ=4n2b2r(1−r)2(ζ2−b2)\frac{dz}{d\zeta} = \frac{4n^2b^2\,r}{(1-r)^2(\zeta^2 - b^2)}

vanishes only at ζ=b\zeta = b (for n<2n < 2 it becomes singular there); that is where the trailing edge sits.

Circulation and lift

At angle of attack α\alpha the circle sees the known potential flow with circulation Γ\Gamma. The Kutta condition requires the stagnation point at the image of the trailing edge; with β=arcsin⁡(Im⁡ζm/a)\beta = \arcsin(\mathrm{Im}\,\zeta_m/a) this is

Γ=4πaUsin⁡(α+β),cl=2ΓUc=8πacsin⁡(α+β).\Gamma = 4\pi a\,U\sin(\alpha + \beta), \qquad c_l = \frac{2\Gamma}{U c} = \frac{8\pi a}{c}\sin(\alpha + \beta).

The velocity on the airfoil is that of the circle divided by |dz/dζ||dz/d\zeta|; at the trailing edge the limit applies (stagnation for n<2n < 2, finite for the Joukowski airfoil). Integrating the pressure cp=1−(w/U)2c_p = 1 - (w/U)^2 over the contour gives the same lift (Kutta-Joukowski) and no drag (d’Alembert). Because of the singularity at the trailing edge the integration uses a spacing clustered at the ends, θ=−β+2πs−sin⁡2πs\theta = -\beta + 2\pi s - \sin 2\pi s; uniform in θ\theta the integral stalled at 3e-8.

The chord is the largest distance of a contour point from the trailing edge (sampling, then Brent); for the flat plate (ζm=0\zeta_m = 0, a=ba = b) it is 4b4b and cl=2πsin⁡αc_l = 2\pi\sin\alpha.

Airfoil polar

Steps per angle of attack:

rechne(contour, α, Re):
    Panel → c_p at each panel midpoint, arc length s
    stagnation point: zero crossing of the velocity near the largest c_p
    each side: u_e(s) as PCHIP, Thwaites up to transition, then Head to the trailing edge
    c_d = sum of Squire-Young of both sides
    optionally: shift the contour outwards by δ₁, close the gap at the trailing edge with a wedge,
                panel again → corrected c_l

Transition after Michel. The laminar part follows Thwaites; transition lies where the momentum-thickness Reynolds number reaches the curve

Reθ=1.174(1+22400Res)Res0.46Re_\theta = 1.174\Big(1 + \frac{22\,400}{Re_s}\Big)Re_s^{0.46}

(located exactly with Brent between two nodes). If the laminar layer separates first (λ=−0.09\lambda = -0.09), separation counts as transition. Behind it Head starts with H=1.4H = 1.4 and continuous θ\theta.

Drag after Squire and Young. The wake is not computed; Squire and Young integrated the development of θ\theta in the wake in closed form with a mean shape factor and obtained per side

cd=2θTEc(ue,TEU)HTE+52.c_d = \frac{2\theta_{TE}}{c}\Big(\frac{u_{e,TE}}{U}\Big)^{\frac{H_{TE} + 5}{2}} .

On the flat plate (ue=Uu_e = U) this is exactly twice the momentum loss; laminar Thwaites gives θ=0.45νx/U\theta = \sqrt{0.45\,\nu x/U}, hence per side cd=20.45/Re=1.342/Rec_d = 2\sqrt{0.45/Re} = 1.342/\sqrt{Re} against Blasius 1.328/Re1.328/\sqrt{Re} – 1.1 %, the deviation of Thwaites’ approximation.

Displacement correction

The boundary layer displaces the outer flow by δ1=Hθ\delta_1 = H\theta. Each node of the contour is moved outwards along the normal by δ1\delta_1 and the panel method is run once more. At the trailing edge the shift opens a gap of width δ1,u+δ1,l\delta_{1,u} + \delta_{1,l}; a wedge made of the extended last panels closes it until the sides are as far apart again as on the unshifted contour, and the Kutta condition sits at its end. Without displacement no wedge is created – the result is then bit-identical to the panel method.

One step as an approximation. Iterated (boundary layer of the shifted contour, shift again, with under-relaxation) this direct coupling does not converge: on the NACA 0012 at 4° clc_l jumped between 0.35 and 0.50, because the turbulent boundary layer just upstream of the trailing edge alternately stayed attached and separated – the known singularity of the direct coupling at separation (Goldstein). Profilpolare therefore stays with one step as a quick approximation; the coupled computation is Profilkopplung.

Coupled computation

The direct coupling prescribes ueu_e to the boundary layer and gets δ1\delta_1 back; at separation this problem has no solution any more. If instead ueu_e is treated as an unknown next to θ\theta and δ1\delta_1, and the effect of the displacement on the outer flow is added as a linear equation, the system stays regular there as well – this is Veldman's quasi-simultaneous interaction and Drela's simultaneous solution. Profilkopplung solves all equations at once with Newton.

rechne(contour, α, Re):
    influence: panel method with blowing → u_e0, matrix D (Kutta included)
    stagnation point from u_e0, start: boundary layer of the inviscid solution
    transition location from the start
    repeat:
        Newton over (θ, δ₁, u_e per panel midpoint, stagnation point) at fixed transition location
        if the stagnation point wants to leave its pair of panels → split the sides anew, continue
        new transition location from the converged u_e (Michel)
    until the location no longer changes
    c_l from the circulation, c_d after Squire-Young

Influence matrix

The displacement acts on the outer flow like blowing with the normal velocity

vn=d(ueδ1)dsv_n = \frac{d(u_e\,\delta_1)}{ds}

(Lighthill). In the panel method it enters the boundary condition as an extra right-hand side: Az=rA\,z = r becomes Az=r+EVA\,z = r + E\,V, the Kutta row stays. The tangential velocity is linear in the unknowns zz, so with m=ueδ1m = u_e\delta_1 per panel midpoint and the difference matrix GG (V=GmV = G\,m, at the nodes the mean of the neighbouring midpoints)

ue,i=ue0,i+∑jDijue,jδ1,j,D=TA−1EG.u_{e,i} = u_{e0,i} + \sum_j D_{ij}\, u_{e,j}\,\delta_{1,j}, \qquad D = T A^{-1} E\, G .

DD is formed once per angle of attack; the signs of the sides (upstream of the stagnation point the contour points against the flow) are in Dij=σi(TA−1EG)ijσjD_{ij} = \sigma_i (T A^{-1} E G)_{ij} \sigma_j. Without blowing it is bit-identical to the panel method. At the trailing edge the displacement flux leaves the body into the wake, which is not modelled.

DD is checked against the panel method on the contour shifted by δ=10−4sin2⁡(πx)\delta = 10^{-4}\sin^2(\pi x): the change of vtv_t agrees to 10 % of its largest value (the shifted contour measures the velocity δ\delta further out, a curvature term), that of the circulation to 4.8 % with 100 and 1.7 % with 200 panels per side – it goes to zero with the number of panels.

Equations per panel midpoint

Laminar after Thwaites, as the trapezoidal rule between the stations k−1k-1 and kk:

θk2uk6−θk−12uk−16νΔs12(uk5+uk−15)=0.45,δ1,kθk=H(λk),λk=θk2νuk−uk−1Δs.\frac{\theta_k^2 u_k^6 - \theta_{k-1}^2 u_{k-1}^6}{\nu\,\Delta s\,\tfrac12(u_k^5 + u_{k-1}^5)} = 0.45, \qquad \frac{\delta_{1,k}}{\theta_k} = H(\lambda_k),\quad \lambda_k = \frac{\theta_k^2}{\nu}\frac{u_k - u_{k-1}}{\Delta s} .

At the two first stations behind the stagnation point the stagnation flow θ2(due/ds)/ν=0.075\theta^2 (du_e/ds)/\nu = 0.075 holds, with the gradient between these two stations. Turbulent after Head, discretised logarithmically as in Drela:

ln⁡θkθk−1+(H+2)ln⁡ukuk−1=Δs2(cf2θ),ln⁡ukθkH1,kuk−1θk−1H1,k−1=Δs(0.0306(H1−3)−0.6169θH1),\ln\frac{\theta_k}{\theta_{k-1}} + (\bar H + 2)\ln\frac{u_k}{u_{k-1}} = \frac{\Delta s}{2}\,\overline{\Big(\frac{c_f}{2\theta}\Big)}, \qquad \ln\frac{u_k\theta_k H_{1,k}}{u_{k-1}\theta_{k-1}H_{1,k-1}} = \Delta s\,\overline{\Big(\frac{0.0306\,(H_1 - 3)^{-0.6169}}{\theta H_1}\Big)},

the bar meaning the mean of both stations, cfc_f after Ludwieg-Tillmann; at transition the turbulent part starts with H=1.4H = 1.4. In addition the coupling at each station and one equation for the stagnation point: the signed tangential velocity, linear between the two panel midpoints around it, vanishes there. Together 3n+13n + 1 equations for 3n+13n + 1 unknowns.

A jump in Head's shape factor. The usual fit of H1(H)H_1(H) has two branches that do not meet at H=1.6H = 1.6 (5.3088 against 5.2868). For a marching method this does not matter, but Newton oscillated at one turbulent station between H=1.598H = 1.598 and 1.602, and the residual stalled at 4⋅10−34\cdot10^{-3}. Between 1.58 and 1.62 the branches are therefore blended with w=3t2−2t3w = 3t^2 - 2t^3; outside it is exactly Head's fit.

Newton, stagnation point and transition

The Jacobian is formed with central differences for the boundary-layer rows (each row depends only on its station and the one before), in closed form for coupling and stagnation point. The step is damped: θ\theta and δ1\delta_1 by at most 30 % per step, ueu_e by at most 0.1, halved on an invalid state. At the end Newton converges quadratically: at 4° the residual falls to 4⋅10−114\cdot10^{-11} in 13 steps, the last steps with order 2.0.

The stagnation point is an unknown because it moves with the boundary layer – the layer lowers the circulation, the point moves towards the nose. If it wants to leave the pair of panels between whose midpoints it lies, the sides are split anew and Newton continues. With the inviscid stagnation point fixed, ueu_e at the first station of one side went to zero at 140 panels per side.

Transition is a station, not a continuous quantity. After each Newton run it is determined from the converged ueu_e (Thwaites in closed form, Michel or laminar separation \(\lambda < -0.09\)) and the computation is repeated with the new location until it no longer changes; if a location recurs, the most upstream one holds. If Newton stalls at a laminar station upstream of transition – the discrete boundary layer separates laminar there –, transition is moved there. The first location always comes from the start of the inviscid solution, so the result does not depend on the start: at 4° the inviscid start, \(\theta\) and \(\delta_1\) times 1.5 with \(u_e\) times 0.95, and the solutions at 0° and 8° give the same \(c_l\) and \(c_d\) to \(2\cdot10^{-13}\).

How it is checked

  • Kármán-Trefftz with n=2n = 2 equals Joukowski (1e-13), n→1n \to 1 tends to the identity; flat plate: chord 4b4b, cl=2πsin⁡αc_l = 2\pi\sin\alpha (1e-13).
  • Pressure integral equals Kutta-Joukowski on three airfoils and three angles, drag zero (1e-9); symmetric airfoil at 0°: cl=0c_l = 0.
  • Panel method on the Kármán-Trefftz airfoil (10°, τ=4°\tau = 4°) with 50 to 400 panels: the error in clc_l and cpc_p falls, at 400 panels clc_l to 0.2 %.
  • Polar without displacement: clc_l bit-identical to the panel method; with displacement smaller, the difference falls with the Reynolds number like turbulent δ1\delta_1 (1000.2100^{0.2} per factor 100).
  • Flat plate laminar against Blasius (1.1 %); with Michel transition at Re=107Re = 10^7 against Prandtl-Schlichting with transition correction (5 %).
  • NACA 0012 at Re=3⋅106Re = 3\cdot 10^6 with free transition, one displacement step: cd=0.0053c_d = 0.0053 at 0° (measured about 0.0058), cl=0.414c_l = 0.414 at 4° (inviscid 0.478, measured about 0.43); at 0° both sides equal.
  • Coupled: influence matrix against the panel method on the shifted contour; residual down to 10−1010^{-10}, quadratic at the end; clc_l and cdc_d independent of the start (1e-9); the difference to the inviscid clc_l falls with the Reynolds number; the laminar part against Thwaites in closed form on the coupled ueu_e (0.5 % from x=0.05x = 0.05, measured 4.6e-4).
  • Coupled polar at Re=6⋅106Re = 6\cdot10^6, 0° to 9°: attached, clc_l and cdc_d increasing, cdc_d against Abbott and von Doenhoff to 15 %, slope against Ladson to 6 %, separated at 10°; panels 60/100/160: clc_l to 0.5 %, cdc_d to 2 %.

Limits

  • Incompressible, two-dimensional; the wake is not modelled – the displacement flux leaves the body at the trailing edge, and the drag comes from Squire-Young instead of the momentum loss far downstream.
  • Coupled up to shortly before separation: separated boundary layers (H>2.4H > 2.4), separation bubbles and maximum lift are not modelled; the result is then flagged.
  • Transition jumps from panel to panel; cdc_d therefore depends on the number of panels by about 1 %. In the single displacement step it depends more strongly on it (NACA 0012 at 0°: 0.0055 / 0.0053 / 0.0047 with 60 / 100 / 160 panels per side), because ueu_e at the trailing edge enters the formula unchanged.
  • Transition only after Michel or at laminar separation; no eNe^N method, no free-stream turbulence.
