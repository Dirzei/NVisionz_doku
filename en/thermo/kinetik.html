Combustion – reaction kinetics and 0D reactor

The computation behind Kinetics and Reactor0D: the methane mechanism of Smooke and Giovangigli with Arrhenius rates and third bodies, the conversion of the CHEMKIN data to SI, reverse rates from equilibrium, the adiabatic constant-pressure reactor with the stiff Rosenbrock method RODAS4, the ignition delay. Pseudocode following the source, examples, checks and limits.

What this is about

The equilibrium says where a reacting mixture is heading, but not how fast. How long a premixed methane-air mixture at 1400 K takes to autoignite decides the design of a premixing duct; how fast CO burns out to CO2 after the flame decides emissions. For this one needs reaction kinetics: a set of elementary reactions with rate coefficients and a model of the reactor in which they run.

Kinetics contains the skeletal methane mechanism of Smooke and Giovangigli (1991): 16 species, 25 distinct reaction equations, published as 35 irreversible reactions, including 10 pairs with fitted reverse reactions and 7 third-body reactions; by default the calculation uses the 25 equations, each reversible from equilibrium. Reactor0D integrates this mechanism in an adiabatic, homogeneous reactor at constant pressure – the model of a shock tube experiment – and determines ignition delay times from it.

Reaction rates

Every reaction ii has a forward coefficient of the extended Arrhenius form

ki(T)=AiTβiexp⁡(−Ea,iRT),R=8.31446J/(molK)k_i(T) = A_i\,T^{\beta_i}\,\exp\left(-\frac{E_{a,i}}{\bar R T}\right), \qquad \bar R = 8.31446\ \mathrm{J/(mol\,K)}

and the rate (conversion per volume and time)

qi=ki(T)∏reactants⁡kof⁡ick⋅[M]δi,[M]=∑kεkckq_i = k_i(T)\,\prod_{\mathrm{reactants}\ k\ \mathrm{of}\ i} c_k \cdot [\mathrm M]^{\delta_i}, \qquad [\mathrm M] = \sum_k \varepsilon_k c_k

with the concentrations ckc_k in mol/m³, δi=1\delta_i = 1 for third-body reactions. The third-body efficiencies εk\varepsilon_k are the same for all seven M reactions: CH4 and H2O 6.5, CO2 1.5, CO 0.75, O2 and N2 0.4, all others 1.0. The species source terms and the heat release are

ω˙k=∑i(ν''ki−ν'ki)qi[mol/(m3s)],q˙=−∑kω˙khk(T)[W/m3]\dot\omega_k = \sum_i (\nu''_{ki} - \nu'_{ki})\,q_i\ \ [\mathrm{mol/(m^3\,s)}], \qquad \dot q = -\sum_k \dot\omega_k\,h_k(T)\ \ [\mathrm{W/m^3}]

with the molar enthalpy hk=ΔfHk∘(0K)+[Hk(T)−Hk(0)]h_k = \Delta_f H^\circ_k(0\,\mathrm K) + [H_k(T) - H_k(0)] from the partition functions.

Units. The tables in the source hold the original numbers of the CHEMKIN file: AA in (cm³/mol)n−1/s with the reaction order nn including M, EaE_a in cal/mol. The static block converts once:

ASI⁡=Acgs⁡⋅(10−6)n−1,Ea,SI⁡=4.184⋅Ea,cal⁡A_{\mathrm{SI}} = A_{\mathrm{cgs}}\cdot\left(10^{-6}\right)^{n-1}, \qquad E_{a,\mathrm{SI}} = 4.184\cdot E_{a,\mathrm{cal}}

Reverse rates from equilibrium (default)

Since the properties were merged (October 2026) the reactor no longer uses the ten fitted reverse reactions of the published file but makes each of the 25 reaction equations reversible through detailed balance: the forward reaction stays, the fitted reverse reaction is dropped, and the net rate is

qi=ki(T)[∏reactants⁡ck−1Kc,i(T)∏products⁡ck][M]δi,Kc,i=Kp,i(p∘RT)Δνi,ln⁡Kp,i=−∑kνkigk∘(T)RTq_i = k_i(T)\Bigl[\prod_{\mathrm{reactants}} c_k - \frac{1}{K_{c,i}(T)}\prod_{\mathrm{products}} c_k\Bigr]\,[\mathrm M]^{\delta_i}, \qquad K_{c,i} = K_{p,i}\left(\frac{p^\circ}{\bar R T}\right)^{\Delta\nu_i}, \qquad \ln K_{p,i} = -\sum_k \nu_{ki}\,\frac{g_k^\circ(T)}{\bar R T}

with p∘=105p^\circ = 10^5 Pa and Δνi\Delta\nu_i = products minus reactants (without M, which cancels). Derivation of KcK_c: at equilibrium ∏k(xkp/p∘)νki=Kp,i\prod_k (x_k p/p^\circ)^{\nu_{ki}} = K_{p,i}; with ck=xkp/(RT)c_k = x_k p/(\bar RT) this becomes ∏kckνki=Kp,i(p∘/RT)Δνi\prod_k c_k^{\nu_{ki}} = K_{p,i}\,(p^\circ/\bar RT)^{\Delta\nu_i}. Hence qiq_i vanishes exactly when the concentrations satisfy mass action, and the reactor runs into the equilibrium of the same properties. Kinetics recognises the reverse reactions itself: a later reaction with reactants and products exchanged and the same third body.

The published, irreversible version (35 reactions with the fitted reverse rates) remains available (Kinetics.omegaDot, Reactor0D.run(…, true)); it reaches no equilibrium. For comparison with this version krThermo(i, T) computes the reverse rate ki/Kc,ik_i/K_{c,i} with the exact partition functions instead of the table; the ratio fitted to thermodynamic shows how well the published mechanism fits the thermodynamics (see checks).

The adiabatic constant-pressure reactor

The state is y=(T,Y1,…,Y16)y = (T, Y_1, \dots, Y_{16}) with the mass fractions YkY_k. At constant pressure and without heat loss

dYkdt=ω˙kMkρ,dTdt=−∑khkω˙kρcp,ρ=pRT∑kYk/Mk,cp=∑kYkcp,kMk\frac{\mathrm dY_k}{\mathrm dt} = \frac{\dot\omega_k M_k}{\rho}, \qquad \frac{\mathrm dT}{\mathrm dt} = -\frac{\sum_k h_k\,\dot\omega_k}{\rho\,c_p}, \qquad \rho = \frac{p}{\bar R T \sum_k Y_k/M_k}, \qquad c_p = \sum_k Y_k\frac{c_{p,k}}{M_k}

with ck=ρYk/Mkc_k = \rho Y_k/M_k and the molar quantities hkh_k, cp,kc_{p,k}. Derivation of the temperature equation: adiabatic at fixed pressure the specific enthalpy h=∑kYkhk(T)/Mkh = \sum_k Y_k h_k(T)/M_k is conserved. From dh/dt=0\mathrm dh/\mathrm dt = 0 follows cpdT/dt+∑k(hk/Mk)dYk/dt=0c_p\,\mathrm dT/\mathrm dt + \sum_k (h_k/M_k)\,\mathrm dY_k/\mathrm dt = 0, and with the species equation the formula above. Mass is conserved because ∑kω˙kMk=0\sum_k \dot\omega_k M_k = 0 for every element-balanced reaction. Negative mass fractions are counted as zero in the right-hand side.

The system is stiff: radicals such as H, O, OH equilibrate in microseconds, ignition takes milliseconds. The eigenvalues of the Jacobian ∂f/∂y\partial f/\partial y therefore span three to four orders of magnitude; an explicit method would have to run with the step size of the fastest time constant. Integration therefore uses RODAS4 from framework-math, a linearly implicit Rosenbrock method of order 4 with embedded error estimator, L-stable: per step one Jacobian and one LU decomposition of I/(hγ)−JI/(h\gamma) - J (γ=1/4\gamma = 1/4), six stages, no Newton iteration. The Jacobian comes from forward differences with step 10−7⋅10^{-7}\cdotscale (temperature max⁡(T,300K)\max(T, 300\,\mathrm K), mass fractions 1); a dense LU solves the stage systems.

Ignition delay. After every accepted step an observer reports the new state; the largest rate of change ΔT/Δt\Delta T/\Delta t between two accepted steps is kept. Its time is τ\tau once the temperature is more than 50 K above the start; without ignition in the time window τ=−1\tau = -1 remains. The criterion is thus tied to the step boundaries of the integrator; the package reaktor by contrast locates a temperature rise exactly as an event.

Pseudocode – Reactor0D.run and integriere

// run(T0, p, Y0, t_end [, original])
warmUp()                                                 table rows of the 16 species (with entropy)
y ← (T0, Y0)
f(y)  ← rhs(y, p, original)                              reversible (default) or published
J(y)  ← forward differences of f, hi = 10⁻⁷·scalei
observer(t, y):                                          after every accepted step
    r ← (T(y) − Tbefore)/(t − tbefore)
    if r > max and T rises: max ← r;  if T − T0 > 50 K: τ ← t
    record the time series decimated (above 4000 rows drop every second one, double the stride)
Rodas.loese(f, J, LU, 0 → t_end; rtol 10⁻⁶, atol 10⁻¹², first step 10⁻⁹, at most 2·10⁶ steps)
if not arrived: throw
return (τ, t_end, T, Y, steps, time series)

Source: envisionz-framework-thermo/src/envisionz/thermo/Reactor0D.java, methods rhs, run, integriere, ignitionDelay; RODAS4 in envisionz.math.ode.Rodas (coefficients after Hairer and Wanner). Until October 2026 the reactor used its own implicit Euler method with step doubling; the ignition delay of the published version changed by +1.2 / +0.5 / +0.3 % at 1400 / 1600 / 1800 K – the older method was the less accurate one, shown against Radau IIA.

Convention in the framework: the properties hkh_k, cp,kc_{p,k}, gk∘g_k^\circ come from the single table SpeciesCache over the partition functions: nodes every 25 K from 200 to 1000 K, every 50 K up to 4000 K, every 100 K up to 5000 K, cubic Hermite in between; cpc_p is the derivative of the Hermite curve of the enthalpy and thus exactly consistent with it. Measured deviation against the exact evaluation: |Δh|/(RT)≤5⋅10−5|\Delta h|/(RT) \le 5\cdot10^{-5}, cpc_p relative ≤10−3\le 10^{-3} (worst case CH3O below 250 K). The rows of the 16 species with entropy cost about 30–40 s on first use (H2O and CO2 are expensive); warmUp() belongs in a background thread. Start mixtures: ch4Air(φ) with air as O2 + 3.76 N2; h2Air(φ) with a radical seed xH=10−6x_H = 10^{-6}, because the mechanism has no H2 initiation reaction (CH4 initiates via R15).

How it is checked

The recomputation with sympy (derivations and worked examples of this page) is in the source tree under docs/theorie/rechnung/thermo_kinetik.py.

envisionz.thermo.Reaktorprobe (20 checks, in the collective run) checks the path the calculation takes:

  • RE-01 – Kinetics.omegaDot and omegaDotUmkehrbar against the independently evaluated SmookeMethaneAirMechanism (CGS units, its own Arrhenius and third-body calculation) at 50 random states.
  • RE-03 – ignition delay at 1600 K with tolerance 10−610^{-6} against 10−910^{-9} (to 2⋅10−32\cdot10^{-3}), temperature history with RODAS4 against Radau IIA at 10−910^{-9} at six times around ignition (to 10−310^{-3}), end temperature to 0.5 K.
  • RE-04 – ignition delays at three temperatures, hotter ignites earlier.
  • RE-05 – elements and enthalpy conserved over the ignition; the reversible version ends in the (h, p) equilibrium of the same 16 species (T to 1 K, CO and OH to 1 %), the published one at 3332 K from 1600 K (documented limit).

Besides, test/KineticsCompare.java (report test/kinetics-validierung.txt, long runner) in five parts: A unit conversion for R1, R5, R9 (third body) and R15 at 1000 K against hand calculation; B fitted against thermodynamic reverse rates of the ten pairs at 1000, 1500, 2000 K: H2/O2 pairs 0.71 to 1.25, R13/R14 (CO + OH ↔ CO2 + H) 0.51 to 0.62, R32/R33 (H2O2 + OH ↔ H2O + HO2) 1.67 to 2.58 – a property of the published mechanism; C element conservation of ω˙\dot\omega for C, H, O below 10−9⋅max⁡|ω˙|10^{-9}\cdot\max|\dot\omega|; D ignition delay against the shock tube correlation; E end state of both versions against Combustion.

The six intermediate species (CH3, CH2O, HCO, CH3O, HO2, H2O2) are checked in FundamentalSpecies against the NASA polynomials of GRI-Mech 3.0 (partition functions).

Limits

  • Range of the mechanism: lean to stoichiometric methane-air flames and ignition trends around 1 bar. No falloff (Lindemann, Troe), no nitrogen chemistry (N2 is inert, NO formation is not represented), no C2 paths – rich mixtures and high pressures become inaccurate. Larger mechanisms (DRM19, GRI-Mech 3.0) are computed by the package reaktor.
  • Ignition delay about five times too long compared with shock tube measurements: the skeletal mechanism lacks CH3O2 and C2 ignition chemistry; it is tuned to flame propagation.
  • Equilibrium end state only in the reversible version: the published version with R34/R35 irreversible and fitted reverse rates dissociates too little after the flame and overshoots (3332 K from 1600 K instead of 2782.56 K).
  • Table 200–5000 K; outside it continues with the cpc_p at the edge – a protective continuation for temperature searches, not physics.
  • Ignition criterion max dT/dt between accepted steps, counted only after a temperature rise of 50 K; without ignition in the time window τ=−1\tau = -1.
  • Hydrogen only with a radical seed; above the chain-branching limit the seed affects τ\tau only weakly (logarithmically).

Assumptions and cost

  • Homogeneous, adiabatic, isobaric: the model of a shock tube experiment behind the reflected wave; a pressure rise from heat release (at constant volume) is not represented.
  • Ideal gas mixture, law of mass action: concentrations ck=ρYk/Mkc_k = \rho Y_k/M_k, no pressure dependence of the coefficients other than through [M].
  • Cost: 17 unknowns, per step 18 evaluations of the right-hand side for the Jacobian and six stages; 499 steps over 20 ms in the example.

What follows

  • For end states and exhaust composition the reversible version, or the equilibrium directly, is the right tool; the published version only for ignition delay and flame propagation, for which it is tuned.
  • The activation energy of the ignition delay is right, the absolute value is not: trends over temperature are usable, absolute ignition delays for designing a premixing duct are not without calibration.
  • The ignition delay hardly depends on the solver accuracy: with the default 10−610^{-6} it lies within 2⋅10−32\cdot10^{-3} of the value at 10−910^{-9}, the temperature history within 10−310^{-3} of Radau IIA (RE-03) – far below the model uncertainty of about a factor of five.
