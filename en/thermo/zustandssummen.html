Properties from partition functions – background

The computation behind FundamentalSpecies, AirFunctions and SpeciesCache: how molecular constants become cp(T), H(T) and S°(T), which model stage applies to which species, pseudocode following the source, how it is checked and where the limits are.

What this is about

Every cycle, map and combustion calculation needs the caloric properties of ideal gases: the specific heat cp(T)c_p(T), the enthalpy h(T)h(T) and the standard entropy s∘(T)s^\circ(T). The usual source are polynomial fits to tables (JANAF, NASA-7, NASA-9). The fits are fast and accurate within their range, but they say nothing about why cpc_p of nitrogen rises by about a quarter between 300 K and 2000 K – and their own fitting errors are invisible.

The module computes from statistical mechanics instead. A molecule can only take discrete energies (translation, rotation, vibration, electronic excitation). How the molecules of a gas at temperature TT distribute over these levels is fixed by the Boltzmann distribution; the partition function qq is the sum of the Boltzmann weights of all levels. All caloric properties of the ideal gas follow exactly from q(T)q(T) and its derivative. The inputs are spectroscopic molecular constants (vibrational wavenumbers, rotational constants, anharmonicities, electronic terms), not fitted coefficients.

Why cpc_p rises then becomes visible directly: at room temperature the vibration of N2 is practically not excited (the first vibrational level lies about 3350 K "high"); with rising temperature it "thaws" and takes up energy. Anharmonicity, vibration-rotation coupling and centrifugal stretching are corrections that amount to percent at high temperature – exactly where combustor and turbine operate.

Definitions and formulas

Energies are given as wavenumbers ν˜\tilde\nu in cm⁻¹. The second radiation constant c2=hc/kc_2 = hc/k turns them into a temperature scale; a level of energy EE has the Boltzmann weight

w=gexp⁡(−c2ET),c2=hck⋅100≈1.43879cmKw = g\, \exp\left(-\frac{c_2 E}{T}\right), \qquad c_2 = \frac{h c}{k}\cdot 100 \approx 1.43879\ \mathrm{cm\,K}

with degeneracy gg. The internal partition function is q=∑igiexp⁡(−c2Ei/T)q = \sum_i g_i \exp(-c_2 E_i/T); counting always starts at the ground state (E0=0E_0 = 0), so all enthalpies refer to H∘(0K)H^\circ(0\,\mathrm{K}). For every contribution, with xi=c2Ei/Tx_i = c_2 E_i/T,

URT=∑ixiwi∑iwi=T∂ln⁡q∂T,SR=URT+ln⁡q\frac{U}{RT} = \frac{\sum_i x_i\, w_i}{\sum_i w_i} = T\frac{\partial \ln q}{\partial T}, \qquad \frac{S}{R} = \frac{U}{RT} + \ln q

Translation has a closed form. With the ideal gas law (contribution pV=RTpV = RT) and the standard pressure p∘=1bar⁡p^\circ = 1\ \mathrm{bar}, Sackur-Tetrode gives

Htr⁡−H0RT=52,Str⁡∘R=52+ln⁡[(2πmkTh2)3/2kTp∘]=32ln⁡M+52ln⁡T+SC\frac{H_{\mathrm{tr}} - H_0}{RT} = \frac{5}{2}, \qquad \frac{S_{\mathrm{tr}}^\circ}{R} = \frac{5}{2} + \ln\left[\left(\frac{2\pi m k T}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}\frac{kT}{p^\circ}\right] = \frac{3}{2}\ln M + \frac{5}{2}\ln T + S_C

with MM in g/mol and the constant SCS_C containing the atomic mass unit, kk, hh and p∘p^\circ. The totals are sums over the degrees of freedom:

H∘−H0∘RT=52+Uint⁡RT,S∘R=Str⁡∘R+Uint⁡RT+ln⁡qint⁡\frac{H^\circ - H^\circ_0}{RT} = \frac{5}{2} + \frac{U_{\mathrm{int}}}{RT}, \qquad \frac{S^\circ}{R} = \frac{S_{\mathrm{tr}}^\circ}{R} + \frac{U_{\mathrm{int}}}{RT} + \ln q_{\mathrm{int}}

The equilibrium constant follows from the Giauque function and the enthalpies of formation at 0 K, ΔfH∘(0K)\Delta_f H^\circ(0\,\mathrm{K}) (table HF0):

ln⁡Kp=∑iνiΦi(T)−∑iνiΔfHi∘(0K)RT\ln K_p = \sum_i \nu_i\, \Phi_i(T) - \sum_i \nu_i \frac{\Delta_f H^\circ_i(0\,\mathrm{K})}{RT}

FundamentalSpecies does not return a heat capacity itself. Callers form it as a central difference of the enthalpy, for example AeroThermo.cpH2O with ±0.5\pm 0.5 K or the equilibrium species with ±10−3\pm 10^{-3} K.

Convention in the framework: the reference level of all enthalpies is 0 K (H∘−H0∘H^\circ - H^\circ_0, plus HF0 at 0 K); the standard pressure of entropy and of KpK_p is p∘=105p^\circ = 10^5 Pa. The gas constant in FundamentalSpecies is R=8.31441R = 8.31441 J/(mol K); the constants hh, kk, cc and the atomic mass unit are constants of the class. Other classes of the package deliberately use slightly different gas constants (see limits).

Which species with which model

The model is built in stages. Every stage has a data table; if a species' entry is null, it continues with the simpler stage – bit-identical to before the stage was introduced. Species can thus be refined one at a time without changing the others.

1a ROVIBdirect (v, J) double sum with anharmonicity, vibration-rotation coupling and centrifugal stretchingH2, N2, O2, CO, OH, NO, SO

Diatomic molecules: the (v, J) double sum

For the seven diatomic species, stage 1a replaces rotation and vibration together by the sum over all levels of the electronic ground state – the method the JANAF tables are computed with. The term values (in cm⁻¹) are

G(v)=ωe(v+12)−ωexe(v+12)2+ωeye(v+12)3G(v) = \omega_e\left(v+\tfrac12\right) - \omega_e x_e\left(v+\tfrac12\right)^2 + \omega_e y_e\left(v+\tfrac12\right)^3

Bv=Be−αe(v+12),Fv(J)=BvJ(J+1)−De[J(J+1)]2B_v = B_e - \alpha_e\left(v+\tfrac12\right), \qquad F_v(J) = B_v\, J(J+1) - D_e\,[J(J+1)]^2

and the internal partition function

qrv⁡=1σ∑v∑J(2J+1)exp⁡(−c2[G(v)−G(0)+Fv(J)]T)q_{\mathrm{rv}} = \frac{1}{\sigma}\sum_{v}\sum_{J}(2J+1)\,\exp\left(-\frac{c_2\,[G(v) - G(0) + F_v(J)]}{T}\right)

with symmetry number σ\sigma (2 for homonuclear molecules). The centrifugal constant DeD_e is entered as measured only for N2; for the other species the class uses the Kratzer relation of the rotating Morse oscillator,

De=4Be3ωe2D_e = \frac{4 B_e^3}{\omega_e^2}

checked on N2 (Kratzer 5.74·10⁻⁶ cm⁻¹ against measured 5.76·10⁻⁶ cm⁻¹). Its effect on cpc_p is not negligible: measured on N2, +0.10 % at 1000 K to +0.25 % at 2500 K.

Pseudocode – FundamentalSpecies.rovib(c, σ, T)

(ωe, ωexe, ωeye, Be, αe, De) ← c
E00 ← ωe/2 − ωexe/4 + ωeye/8                 G(0), zero of the count
vMax ← min(⌊ωe/(2·ωexe) − ½⌋, 80)           maximum of the Morse ladder, at most 80
q ← 0;  u ← 0
for v ← 0 to vMax:
    g ← G(v)
    if c2·(g − E00)/T > 300: stop               weight below e^−300
    Bv ← Be − αe·(v + ½)
    if Bv ≤ 0: stop                            unphysically high v
    ePrev ← −1
    for J ← 0 to 500:
        e ← g − E00 + Bv·J(J+1) − De·[J(J+1)]²
        if e < ePrev: stop                     centrifugal turnover: E falls again
        ePrev ← e
        x ← c2·e/T
        if x > 300: stop
        w ← (2J + 1)·exp(−x)
        q ← q + w;  u ← u + x·w
        if J > 10 and w < 10⁻¹⁴·q: stop
q ← q/σ;  u ← u/σ
return (u/q, ln q)                              U/(RT) and ln q

Polyatomic molecules: anharmonic level sum

For H2O and CO2 the harmonically computed vibrations were the main cause of the old deviation from JANAF (up to −2.5 % in cpc_p at 2500 K for H2O, see checks). Stage 1b sums the vibrational levels of a triatomic molecule one by one:

E(v1,v2,v3)=∑iωiai+∑i≤jxijaiaj,ai=vi+di2E(v_1, v_2, v_3) = \sum_{i} \omega_i\, a_i + \sum_{i \le j} x_{ij}\, a_i a_j, \qquad a_i = v_i + \frac{d_i}{2}

relative to the ground state E(0,0,0)E(0,0,0). did_i is the degeneracy of the mode (CO2: bending mode d2=2d_2 = 2); a dd-fold degenerate state with quantum number vv counts

g(v,d)=(v+d−1)!v!(d−1)!(d=2:g=v+1)g(v, d) = \frac{(v + d - 1)!}{v!\,(d - 1)!} \qquad (d = 2:\ g = v + 1)

For the degenerate bending mode of CO2 the l substructure is counted exactly: v2v_2 has l=v2,v2−2,…≥0l = v_2, v_2 - 2, \dots \ge 0, each with the extra energy g22l2g_{22}\, l^2, l=0l = 0 single, l≠0l \ne 0 double. The Fermi resonance ν1/2ν2\nu_1 / 2\nu_2 is not computed as a perturbation: it conserves the sum of the levels of a polyad, so the unperturbed level sum is the consistent basis (residual error second order in W/kTW/kT, according to the context file below 0.1 % in cpc_p).

Vibration-rotation coupling (stage 2). The rotational constants depend on the vibrational state, Xv=X0−∑iαiXviX_v = X_0 - \sum_i \alpha_i^X v_i for X=A,B,CX = A, B, C. Since the temperature dependence of the classical rotational sum (∝T3/2\propto T^{3/2}) is the same for all vv, a scalar weight per vibrational state suffices:

r(v)=A0B0C0AvBvCv,Xv≥0.02X0(clamp)r(v) = \sqrt{\frac{A_0 B_0 C_0}{A_v B_v C_v}}, \qquad X_v \ge 0.02\,X_0 \ \text{(clamp)}

Pseudocode – FundamentalSpecies.polyVib(ω, d, x, g22, rot0, α, T)

q ← 0;  u ← 0
prev1 ← −1

    prev1 ← e1;  if c2·e1/T > 300: stop
    for v2 ← 0 to 60:                     same, with guard in v2
        for v3 ← 0 to 60:
            e ← E(v1, v2, v3) − E0

            if e < 0: e ← 0                 floating-point residue of the ground state
            if c2·e/T > 300: stop
            gA ← g(v1, d1)·g(v3, d3)

Water: the asymmetric top after Watson

H2O is a light, strongly asymmetric top. The classical top formula overestimates its rotational sum at room temperature by about 1.5 %. Stage 2b therefore computes the rotational levels themselves: for every angular momentum JJ the (2J+1)×(2J+1)(2J+1)\times(2J+1) matrix of the Watson Hamiltonian (A reduction) is set up in the basis |J,K⟩|J, K\rangle,

⟨K|H|K⟩=12(B+C)[J(J+1)−K2]+AK2−ΔJJ2(J+1)2−ΔJKJ(J+1)K2−ΔKK4\langle K|H|K\rangle = \tfrac12(B+C)\,[J(J+1) - K^2] + A K^2 - \Delta_J J^2(J+1)^2 - \Delta_{JK} J(J+1) K^2 - \Delta_K K^4

⟨K±2|H|K⟩={14(B−C)−12δJJ(J+1)−14δK[K2+(K±2)2]}J(J+1)−K(K±1)J(J+1)−(K±1)(K±2)\langle K\pm2|H|K\rangle = \left\{\tfrac14(B - C) - \tfrac12\delta_J J(J+1) - \tfrac14\delta_K[K^2 + (K\pm2)^2]\right\}\sqrt{J(J+1) - K(K\pm1)}\,\sqrt{J(J+1) - (K\pm1)(K\pm2)}

and diagonalised with the cyclic Jacobi method. Every eigenvalue counts with degeneracy 2J+12J+1 (magnetic quantum number M); the sum over JJ stops when the contribution of a whole JJ shell falls below 10−1410^{-14} of the sum (at most J=200J = 200).

Leaving the quartic centrifugal terms directly in the level sum turned out not to be perturbatively controlled: above about 1000 K the expansion tips over, energies run backwards and cpc_p collapses. In the productive path the distortion terms in the matrix are therefore switched off (DIST_F = 0), and centrifugal stretching enters as an analytic first-order correction:

qcd⁡qrigid⁡=1−β⟨H4⟩=1+c1T,Δln⁡q=ln⁡(1+c1T),ΔURT=c1T1+c1T\frac{q_{\mathrm{cd}}}{q_{\mathrm{rigid}}} = 1 - \beta\,\langle H_4 \rangle = 1 + c_1 T, \qquad \Delta\ln q = \ln(1 + c_1 T), \qquad \Delta\frac{U}{RT} = \frac{c_1 T}{1 + c_1 T}

The averages ⟨H4⟩\langle H_4\rangle of the quartic terms are the classical equipartition moments with A⟨Ja2⟩=kT/2A\langle J_a^2\rangle = kT/2 etc.; the correction is therefore linear in TT. It is applied only while c1T>−0.9c_1 T > -0.9.

q ← 0;  u ← 0
for J ← 0 to 200:
    build HJ ((2J+1)×(2J+1)) from A, B, C       distortion only if DIST_F > 0
    λ ← JacobiEigenvalues(HJ)                  at most 60 sweeps, until Σ off-diag² < 10⁻¹⁶
    for each λk: x ← c2·max(λk, 0)/T
        if x ≤ 300: w ← (2J+1)·exp(−x);  q += w;  u += x·w
    if J > 3 and shell contribution·(2J+1) < 10⁻¹⁴·q: stop
q ← q/σ;  u ← u/σ
if DIST_F = 0:                                  productive path
    corr ← −⟨H4⟩(T)/kT                             equipartition moments, ΔJ…δK
    if corr > −0.9: ln q += ln(1 + corr);  U/RT += corr/(1 + corr)

Source: FundamentalSpecies.java, methods watsonRot and jacobiEigen; constants from the NIST Triatomic Spectral Database (MHz, converted to cm⁻¹ in the static block).

Base stage and electronic levels

Linear rigid rotor with Mulholland's quantum correction, x=c2B0/Tx = c_2 B_0/T:

ln⁡qrot⁡=−ln⁡(σx)+x3+x290,Urot⁡RT=1−x3−x245\ln q_{\mathrm{rot}} = -\ln(\sigma x) + \frac{x}{3} + \frac{x^2}{90}, \qquad \frac{U_{\mathrm{rot}}}{RT} = 1 - \frac{x}{3} - \frac{x^2}{45}

The factor 1/45 in the enthalpy is a corrected spot (formerly 1/90; effect below 0.04 %, only H2 below about 300 K). Non-linear rotor, classical:

qrot⁡=πσ(Tc2)3/21ABC,Urot⁡RT=32q_{\mathrm{rot}} = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{\sigma}\left(\frac{T}{c_2}\right)^{3/2}\frac{1}{\sqrt{ABC}}, \qquad \frac{U_{\mathrm{rot}}}{RT} = \frac32

Harmonic vibration per mode (degenerate modes appear several times in the table), u=c2ν˜/Tu = c_2\tilde\nu/T:

Uvib⁡RT=ue−u1−e−u,Svib⁡R=ue−u1−e−u−ln⁡(1−e−u)\frac{U_{\mathrm{vib}}}{RT} = \frac{u\, e^{-u}}{1 - e^{-u}}, \qquad \frac{S_{\mathrm{vib}}}{R} = \frac{u\, e^{-u}}{1 - e^{-u}} - \ln\left(1 - e^{-u}\right)

Electronic levels as a list (Ei,gi)(E_i, g_i), qel⁡=∑gie−c2Ei/Tq_{\mathrm{el}} = \sum g_i e^{-c_2 E_i/T} – for example O2 with 3Σ^3\Sigma, 1Δ^1\Delta at 7918 cm⁻¹ and 1Σ^1\Sigma at 13195 cm⁻¹, OH and NO with their spin-orbit doublets. For H2O2 the same table holds the torsional ladder: spectroscopic levels up to 776 cm⁻¹, above that a free internal rotor E=Fm2E = F m^2 (F=20.2F = 20.2 cm⁻¹, g=2g = 2) that gives the correct high-temperature limit cp,tors⁡→R/2c_{p,\mathrm{tors}} \to R/2.

There is also the Morse level sum anharmVib with E(v)=ωe(v+12)−ωexe(v+12)2E(v) = \omega_e(v+\frac12) - \omega_e x_e (v+\frac12)^2 as the older path for diatomics; since stage 1a it is no longer reached for any species, because all seven diatomics have a ROVIB entry.

Pseudocode – FundamentalSpecies.hFunc(sp, T) (sFunc analogous)

h ← 5/2                                         translation including pV

else:
    if linear:      h += 1 − x/3 − x²/45              Mulholland

    else: h += Σmodes u·e^−u/(1 − e^−u)

Fast paths: tables with Hermite interpolation

The exact evaluation is expensive: according to the class comments H2O with the Watson rotor costs about 0.6 s per temperature, CO2 at high temperature about 40–100 ms. For map calculations and user interfaces there are therefore tables, built once per JVM and then interpolated cubically after Hermite. On the interval [Ti,Ti+1][T_i, T_{i+1}] with τ=(T−Ti)/ΔT\tau = (T - T_i)/\Delta T:

y(T)=h00(τ)yi+h10(τ)ΔTy'i+h01(τ)yi+1+h11(τ)ΔTy'i+1y(T) = h_{00}(\tau)\,y_i + h_{10}(\tau)\,\Delta T\,y'_i + h_{01}(\tau)\,y_{i+1} + h_{11}(\tau)\,\Delta T\,y'_{i+1}

h00=2τ3−3τ2+1,h10=τ3−2τ2+τ,h01=−2τ3+3τ2,h11=τ3−τ2h_{00} = 2\tau^3 - 3\tau^2 + 1,\quad h_{10} = \tau^3 - 2\tau^2 + \tau,\quad h_{01} = -2\tau^3 + 3\tau^2,\quad h_{11} = \tau^3 - \tau^2

The slopes are physical: h'=cph' = c_p for the enthalpy, s∘′=cp/Ts^{\circ\prime} = c_p/T for the entropy. Because cpc_p at the nodes itself comes from the enthalpy table (central difference of the neighbouring nodes), hh, s∘s^\circ and Φ\Phi are mutually consistent.

SpeciesCache uses the same Hermite form but builds in parallel over all species and nodes and adds one node on either side so that the end nodes also have a central difference.

Convention in the framework: the tables are accelerators with a known error, never a silent replacement. AirFunctions.h/s0/cp are always tabulated; the exact paths are called hExact/s0Exact/cpExact. SpeciesCache is opt-in: only the …Cached paths of Combustion, burnFuel, adiabaticTFuel and the tabulated equilibrium species use it. warmUp() belongs in a background thread (air about 4 s, all species a few tens of seconds), never in the event thread of the user interface.

Map conversion: pressure ratio from Δh/T\Delta h/T

AirFunctions.pqOfDhqt replaces the old map function of ENVISIONZ-2020. Given are the map value Δh/T1\Delta h/T_1 in J/(kg K), the isentropic efficiency η\eta and the inlet temperature. The isentropic comparison process yields the pressure ratio via the entropy function:

Δhs={ηΔhcompressorΔh/ηturbine,h(T2s)=h(T1)±Δhs,π=exp⁡(±s∘(T2s)−s∘(T1)Rair⁡)\Delta h_s = \begin{cases}\eta\,\Delta h & \text{compressor}\\ \Delta h/\eta & \text{turbine}\end{cases}, \qquad h(T_{2s}) = h(T_1) \pm \Delta h_s, \qquad \pi = \exp\left(\pm\frac{s^\circ(T_{2s}) - s^\circ(T_1)}{R_{\mathrm{air}}}\right)

T2sT_{2s} comes from tFromH with the start value T1±Δhs/1005T_1 \pm \Delta h_s/1005.

Limits

  • H2 below about 500 K: the symmetry number enters as a factor 1/σ1/\sigma (high-temperature approximation of nuclear spin statistics). Ortho and para hydrogen are not separated; at 298 K cpc_p is therefore 1.3 % too high (29.2 against 28.84 J/(mol K)), S∘S^\circ is right (130.63 against 130.68).
  • H2O above 2000 K: the linear centrifugal correction overcorrects (+0.46 % at 2000 K, +0.86 % at 2500 K). Higher orders are not defined in the classical integral.
  • Electronically excited states carry the rotational and vibrational structure of the ground state; to be checked for O2 (1Δ^1\Delta at 7918 cm⁻¹) from about 2500 K.
  • Base stage (harmonic, rigid): CH4 −6 % in cp at 2000 K against GRI-Mech, CH3O +10 % at 300 K, NO2 and H2O2 2–3 %. CH3O deliberately deviates from the old GRI data set (electronic degeneracy 4 instead of 2).
  • Dissociation is not a matter of the species data: above about 3000 K the composition from the equilibrium determines the properties (equilibrium, combustion).
  • Run time: exact evaluation of H2O about 0.6 s; the tables cost seconds to tens of seconds once per JVM. Outside their range they fall back to the slow exact path.
  • Gas constants: the package deliberately holds different values side by side, each tied to its original: FundamentalSpecies 8.31441, AirFunctions 8314.41/M, Combustion 8.3143, psiluf 8.314459848, Kinetics, Reactor0D, JetFuel 8.31446 J/(mol K).
